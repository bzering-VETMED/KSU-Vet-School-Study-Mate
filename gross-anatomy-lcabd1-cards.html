🗺️ Overview

Alimentary tract runs from… to…Mouth to anus
Gastrointestinal tract runs from… to…Stomach to anus
Largest body cavity?Abdominal cavity
Serous lining of the abdomen?Peritoneum (parietal and visceral)
Dog vs. horse vs. ruminant fermentation?Dog: limited · horse: hindgut (cecum, colon) · ruminant: foregut (rumen)

🍽️ Stomach

Four regions of the stomach?Cardia, fundus, body, pylorus
Stomach inlet?Cardia
Stomach outlet?Pylorus
Cardiac sphincter in the dog: strong or weak?Weak (dogs vomit)
Pyloric sphincter regulates…Outflow into the duodenum
Site of gas accumulation in the stomach?Fundus
Lesser curvature attaches the…Lesser omentum
Greater curvature attaches the…Greater omentum
Pylorus: wide part, then narrow part?Pyloric antrum, then pyloric canal
Gastric folds that aid peristalsis?Rugae
Stomach lies on which side?Left, caudal to the liver
Empty stomach: palpable?No (under the costal arch)
Moderately full stomach extends…T9–T12
Greatly distended stomach extends…T9–L3
GDV usually twists which direction?Clockwise
GDV compresses which vessels?Caudal vena cava and portal vein

🌀 Small intestine & cecum

Three parts of the small intestine?Duodenum, jejunum, ileum
Longest part of the small intestine?Jejunum
Shortest part of the small intestine?Ileum
Ileum vs. ilium spelling?Ileum with an E (intestine); ilium is the bone
Descending duodenum runs…Right cranial → right caudal quadrant
Caudal duodenal flexure runs…Right caudal → left caudal, caudal to the root of the mesentery
Ascending duodenum runs…Left caudal → left cranial quadrant
Fold between ascending duodenum and descending colon?Duodenocolic fold
Turn from duodenum into jejunum?Duodenojejunal flexure
Jejunum is suspended by the…Mesojejunum
Jejunum is covered by the…Greater omentum
First thing you see when you open the abdomen?Greater omentum
Fold that marks the length of the ileum?Ileocecal fold
Ileum opens into the…Ascending colon (ileocolic orifice)
Cecum opens into the…Ascending colon (cecocolic orifice)
Do the ileum and cecum communicate in the dog?No
Cecum lies on which side?Right of midline
Removing the cecum is called…Typhlectomy

💩 Colon, rectum & anal canal

Order of the colon?Ascending → right colic flexure → transverse → left colic flexure → descending
Following the transverse colon, ingesta enters…Left colic flexure → descending colon
Transverse colon relative to the root of the mesentery?Cranial (in every species)
Artery in the root of the mesentery?Cranial mesenteric artery
Structures cranial to the root of the mesentery in large animals?Not palpable per rectum
Cranial rectum: peritoneal or retroperitoneal?Covered by peritoneum (pararectal fossae)
Caudal rectum: peritoneal or retroperitoneal?Retroperitoneal
Other retroperitoneal organ he named?Kidneys
Internal anal sphincter muscle type?Smooth muscle (involuntary)
External anal sphincter muscle type?Skeletal muscle (voluntary)
Three zones of the anal canal?Columnar, intermediate, cutaneous
Line between rectum and anal canal?Anorectal line
Line between anal canal and skin?Anocutaneous line
Anal sacs open in which zone?Cutaneous zone

🟤 Liver & bile

Liver produces… stored in…Bile, stored in the gallbladder
Largest organ in the body?Liver
Six liver lobes?Left lateral, left medial, quadrate, right medial, right lateral, caudate
Two processes of the caudate lobe?Caudate process and papillary process
Gallbladder sits between which lobes?Right medial and quadrate
Falciform ligament sits between which lobes?Left medial and quadrate
Falciform ligament attaches the liver to…The ventral body wall
Triangular ligaments attach the liver to…The diaphragm
Coronary ligament surrounds the…Caudal vena cava ("crown")
Hepatorenal ligament connects…Caudate process of the liver ↔ right kidney
Hepatogastric and hepatoduodenal ligaments connect…Liver ↔ stomach; liver ↔ duodenum
Common bile duct is formed by…Cystic duct + hepatic duct

🫘 Spleen & pancreas

Spleen lies on which side?Left of midline
Spleen attaches to the stomach by the…Gastrosplenic ligament (greater curvature)
Splenic tumor he named?Hemangiosarcoma
Pancreas lies in the…Mesoduodenum
Pancreas parts?Head (right lobe), body, tail (left lobe, near the spleen)
Pancreatic duct opens at the…Major duodenal papilla (with the common bile duct)
Accessory pancreatic duct opens at the…Minor duodenal papilla
Larger pancreatic duct in the dog?Accessory pancreatic duct (cats: the pancreatic duct)
Most common pancreas biopsy site?Left lobe (tail)
