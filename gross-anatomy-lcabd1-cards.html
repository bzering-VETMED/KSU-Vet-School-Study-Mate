⚠️ Precision traps

  • Ileum (small intestine, with E) vs ilium (hip bone, with I).
  • Ileocolic orifice (ileum to colon) vs cecocolic orifice (cecum to colon); ileum and cecum never connect in dog.
  • Cardia = inlet; pylorus = outlet. Cardiac sphincter = influx; pyloric sphincter = outflow.
  • Greater curvature = convex, greater omentum; lesser curvature = concave, lesser omentum.
  • Gallbladder: right medial + quadrate. Falciform ligament: left medial + quadrate.
  • Transverse colon is CRANIAL to root of mesentery; caudal duodenal flexure and ileum are CAUDAL.
  • Anorectal line (rectum/anal canal, columnar zone) vs anocutaneous line (anal canal/skin, intermediate zone).
  • Internal anal sphincter = smooth, involuntary; external = skeletal, voluntary.
  • Dog: accessory pancreatic duct is larger (minor papilla). Cat: pancreatic duct is larger (major papilla).
  • Major papilla = pancreatic duct + common bile duct; minor papilla = accessory pancreatic duct alone.
  • Alimentary tract = mouth to anus; gastrointestinal tract = stomach to anus.
  • Papillary process and caudate process belong to the caudate lobe; neither is a separate lobe.
