Lab 12 · Common calcaneal tendon

5Which muscles contribute tendons to the common calcanean tendon?

  1. Gastrocnemius, biceps femoris, gracilis, semitendinosus, SDF
  2. Gastrocnemius, quadriceps femoris, gracilis, and sartorius
  3. Gastrocnemius, biceps femoris, semimembranosus, SDF
  4. Gastrocnemius, cranial tibial, SDF, DDF
Tap to reveal answer
Gastrocnemius, biceps femoris, gracilis, semitendinosus, SDF
The gastrocnemius and superficial digital flexor are major contributors, while biceps femoris, semitendinosus, and gracilis contribute via fascial attachments.
Lab 12 · Vastus intermedius

6After reflecting rectus femoris, a damaged muscle lies directly against the smooth cranial surface of the femur and is partly covered by vastus lateralis. Loss of this muscle would directly reduce which movement?

  1. Extension of the tarsus
  2. Extension of the stifle
  3. Flexion of the digits
  4. Flexion of the hip
Tap to reveal answer
Extension of the stifle
The described muscle is vastus intermedius, one of the quadriceps heads; the quadriceps extends the stifle.
Lab 12 · Iliopsoas

7The joined psoas major and iliacus are traced distally toward the hip. Which attachment-and-action pair completes this muscle trace?

  1. Tuber calcanei; extension of the tarsus
  2. Lesser trochanter; flexion of the hip
  3. Tibial tuberosity; extension of the stifle
  4. Greater trochanter; extension of the hip
Tap to reveal answer
Lesser trochanter; flexion of the hip
Psoas major + iliacus form iliopsoas, which inserts on the lesser trochanter and flexes the hip.

Lab 12 · Superficial digital flexor

9A tendon crosses the medial surface of the gastrocnemius tendon, caps the tuber calcanei, and continues distally. Which muscle forms this tendon?

  1. Superficial digital flexor
  2. Gastrocnemius
  3. Medial digital flexor
  4. Lateral digital flexor
Tap to reveal answer
Superficial digital flexor
The superficial digital flexor tendon crosses superficially over the gastrocnemius tendon, caps the tuber calcanei, and continues distally.
Lab 12 · Popliteus

10A small sesamoid lies near the caudal aspect of the lateral tibial condyle, and the associated tendon passes deep to the lateral collateral ligament. Which muscle's tendon should be traced?

  1. Popliteus
  2. Rectus femoris
  3. Medial head of gastrocnemius
  4. Lateral head of gastrocnemius
Tap to reveal answer
Popliteus
The popliteus tendon is unique in passing deep to the lateral collateral ligament and commonly contains a small sesamoid.
