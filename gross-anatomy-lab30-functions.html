🦴 Laryngeal cartilages

4 main laryngeal cartilagesEpiglottic · arytenoid · cricoid · thyroid (the first three sit in the thyroid "trough")
Epiglottic cartilageMost rostral, triangular
Cricoid cartilage"Graduation ring": narrow ventral rim, WIDE dorsum
Thyroid cartilageOpen trough ("boat without a roof"); rostral cornu + caudal cornu; caudal thyroid incisure ventrally
Cricothyroid ligamentVentral; spans the gap between the thyroid + cricoid cartilages
Aryepiglottic foldFold between the epiglottic cartilage and the arytenoid (cuneiform process)
Arytenoid cartilage — 4 processesCuneiform (rostral, in the aryepiglottic fold) · corniculate (caudodorsal) · vocal (medial/ventral, vocal fold attaches) · muscular (LATERAL, muscles attach)

🎤 Folds + cavity

Vocal foldVocalis muscle + vocal ligament; attaches to the vocal process (caudal fold)
Vestibular (ventricular) foldRostral fold; contains the ventricularis muscle
Laryngeal ventricleDepression BETWEEN the vestibular fold (rostral) and vocal fold (caudal)
GlottisVocal fold + vocal process (each side)
Rima glottidis (glottic cleft)The opening between the left + right glottis → infraglottic cavity → trachea

💪 Laryngeal muscles

Cricoarytenoideus dorsalisDorsal cricoid lamina → muscular process of the arytenoid
Cricoarytenoideus lateralisLateral cricoid → muscular process of the arytenoid
ThyroarytenoideusThyroid cartilage → arytenoid; = vocalis (in the vocal fold) + ventricularis (in the vestibular fold)
Cricothyroid muscleCricoid → thyroid cartilage (ventrolateral)

👂 Ear

Auricular cartilagePinna (hyaline per video); helix = border (lateral + medial)
AntihelixTick-like bump in the canal opening — often mistaken for a tick
TragusCartilage lateral to/in front of the antihelix, hair-covered
Marginal cutaneous sac (pouch)Pouch at the caudal margin of the pinna by the antitragus — check for ticks on ear exams
Annular cartilageRing around the external acoustic meatus (seen after cutting the pretragic + intertragic incisures)
Tympanic membraneDeep in the canal; hard to find in lab

🦷 Mastication

Masseter muscleLateral to the mandible; transect off the zygomatic arch
Temporalis muscleTemporal fossa → coronoid process; medial attachment to the zygomatic arch
Zygomatic salivary glandVentral to the orbital ligament, seen after reflecting masseter + temporalis
Medial + lateral pterygoid musclesShiny muscle deep in the pterygopalatine fossa; NOT separated on the exam ("pterygoid muscles")
DigastricusCurves along the ventral angle of the mandible; rostral + caudal bellies

👅 Tongue + hyoid muscles

StyloglossusStylohyoid bone → tongue
HyoglossusBasihyoid → tongue
GenioglossusChin ("genio") → tongue; fan-shaped
SternohyoideusSternum → hyoid (basihyoid)
SternothyroideusSternum → thyroid cartilage (splits from sternohyoideus)
ThyrohyoideusThyroid cartilage → hyoid
MylohyoideusTransverse sling between the mandibles ("mylo" = molar) → hyoid; ventral, superficial
GeniohyoideusChin → hyoid; DEEP to the mylohyoideus
