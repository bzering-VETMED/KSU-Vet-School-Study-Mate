⚠️ Precision traps

  • Name changes: subclavian → AXILLARY (first rib, after superficial cervical + internal thoracic) → BRACHIAL (after the cranial circumflex humeral) → MEDIAN (after the common interosseous).
  • Cranial circumflex humeral = DIRECT branch of the axillary · caudal circumflex humeral = branch of the SUBSCAPULAR.
  • Deep brachial = first branch of the brachial · deep antebrachial = first branch of the MEDIAN (same logic).
  • External thoracic → SUPERFICIAL pectorals · lateral thoracic → DEEP pectoral.
  • Cephalic vein drains the MEDIAL paw/forearm · accessory cephalic drains the DORSAL side.
  • Superficial radial nerve branches (medial + lateral) run on EITHER SIDE of the cephalic vein → careful with venipuncture.
  • Median nerve: the only cutaneous nerve with NO autonomous zone.
  • Brachial plexus segments per nerve are NOT tested; plexus as a whole = C6–T2 (lecture).
✅ Closed: deep antebrachial artery · The brachial-artery slide listed it under the brachial, but the median + deep antebrachial slides AND the Lab 20 pre-lab video say it is the first (caudal) branch of the MEDIAN artery. Use median.

🧭 Skin zones

Cutaneous areaALL the skin a nerve supplies (more inclusive)
Autonomous zoneSkin supplied by ONLY that nerve, no overlap · testing site for nerve function
Overlap zonePeriphery, two or more nerves
Nerves with autonomous zonesMusculocutaneous, axillary, radial, ulnar · NOT the median
Testing sitesMusculocutaneous ~2 cm distal to the medial epicondyle · axillary ~2 cm caudal to the shoulder · radial = proximal metacarpals 2–3 · ulnar (caudal cutaneous antebrachial) ~4 cm distal to the olecranon · palmar ulnar = lateral 5th digit
Thoracic limb lymphNO single terminal lymph node (head = medial retropharyngeal)
