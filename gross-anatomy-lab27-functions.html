🟣 Plexus + trunk

Lumbosacral plexusVentral branches of L3–S2 (L3 + S2 may or may not contribute) → nerves to body wall, pelvic limb, pelvic viscera, perineum
Lumbosacral trunkL6, L7, S1 (+ part of S2) → cranial gluteal, caudal gluteal + sciatic nerves; a SUBSET of the plexus

🔵 Medial thigh

Obturator nerveL4–L6 → through the obturator foramen → adductor muscles (runs with the medial circumflex femoral branches)
Femoral nerveEmerges from the iliopsoas → iliopsoas + all four heads of the quadriceps; gives off the saphenous nerve
Saphenous nerveBranch of the femoral nerve; runs with the saphenous artery + medial saphenous vein → sartorius + skin of medial thigh, stifle, leg, paw

🟠 Pudendal + perineum

Pudendal nerveSacral nerves; medial to the superficial gluteal, lateral to coccygeus/levator ani → 3 branches
Caudal rectal nerveMost dorsal pudendal branch → external anal sphincter
Perineal nervesSmall branches → anus + perineum, continue to scrotum or labium
Dorsal nerve of the penis / clitorisMost ventral pudendal branch → dorsum of the penis (with the dorsal artery of the penis) / clitoris
Caudal cutaneous femoral nerveSacral plexus; runs with the caudal gluteal artery, surfaces near the sacrotuberous ligament → skin of the caudal thigh (easy to cut when skinning)

🟢 Gluteal + sciatic

Cranial gluteal nerveMiddle gluteal, deep gluteal + tensor fasciae latae (runs with the cranial gluteal artery)
Caudal gluteal nerveSole innervation of the superficial gluteal muscle
Sciatic nerveMuscular branches to the hamstrings (biceps femoris, semitendinosus, semimembranosus) → divides into common fibular + tibial nerves. Avoid with IM injections
Common fibular nerveCranial division of the sciatic (L6, L7) → superficial + deep fibular nerves; gives the lateral cutaneous sural nerve
Lateral cutaneous sural nerveCutaneous branch of the COMMON FIBULAR nerve → skin of the lateral crus
Tibial nerveCaudal division of the sciatic; between the 2 heads of the gastrocnemius → caudal crus muscles + plantar paw muscles; splits into medial + lateral plantar nerves
Caudal cutaneous sural nerveCutaneous branch of the TIBIAL nerve → skin of the caudal crus
Superficial fibular nerveRuns with the cranial branch of the lateral saphenous vein → dorsal paw
Deep fibular nerveCranial crus muscles; runs with the cranial tibial artery (cut BOTH crural + tarsal extensor retinacula to follow it)

🔴 Pes vessels + tarsal tunnel

Dorsal pedal arteryCranial tibial artery simply RENAMED at the tarsus (no branch given off)
Arcuate arterySmall branch of the dorsal pedal → dorsal metatarsal arteries (smaller than the lab-guide figure shows)
Perforating branchOf dorsal metatarsal artery II, between metatarsals II + III → deep plantar arch = main supply to the digits
Tarsal tunnelCalcaneal tuber + sustentaculum tali + flexor retinaculum; carries the lateral digital flexor tendon, medial + lateral plantar nerves, caudal branch of the saphenous artery
