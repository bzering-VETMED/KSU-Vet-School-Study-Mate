Left lateral lobe liver
📝 Slide note (professor)
The left lateral lobe is separate by a fissure from the left medial lobe. The free margin of the left lateral lobe is frequently notched. The visceral surface of the left lateral lobe is concave where it contacts the stomach.

❓ Name it (complete + specific)
Caudate lobe liver
📝 Slide note (professor)
The caudate lobe is indistinctly separated from the central mass of the liver. It lies transversely but it is mainly to the right of and dorsal to the main bulk of the organ. It has multiple parts that you need to identify. A caudate process, which caps around the right kidney and consists of a renal impression where the right kidney lies. A papillary process which can be seen through the lesser omentum if the liver is pulled forward. It lies in the lesser curvature of the stomach.

Identify the highlighted structure.📚 course slide · Lab 22 slides #72
❓ Name it (complete + specific)
Hepatic ducts
📝 Slide note (professor)
The biliary duct system is microscopic. Bile, which is secreted by the liver cells, is collected and then drained by the interlober ducts. The interlobar ducts unite to form hepatic ducts, these ducts come from each lobe of the liver. The arrangement of these ducts are variable. Dissection: Finding the hepatic ducts can be challenging, you will need to have someone tip the liver cranially as you are viewing the visceral surface near the lesser curvature of the stomach. You may need to clean this area out with water to remove debris from better visualization. You should then be able to trace all of the ducts going to each liver lobe.

❓ Name it (complete + specific)
Gallbladder
📝 Slide note (professor)
Remember how the gallbladder is located between the right medial and quadrate liver lobes. The gallbladder is storage for bile and when full, it can extend through the liver and contact the diaphragm. The gallbladder is often stained green in preserved specimens. The neck of the gallbladder is continued as the cystic duct.

Cystic duct
📝 Slide note (professor)
The cystic duct is the duct that carries bile into and out of the gallbladder, it spans from the neck of the gallbladder until the hepatic ducts unite with it to form the common bile duct.

❓ Name it (complete + specific)
Stomach
📝 Slide note (professor)
The stomach is divided into parts that blend imperceptibly with one another. The cardiac part is the smallest part and is situated nearest the esophagus. The fundus is dome-shaped and lies to the left of and dorsal to the cardia. The body of the stomach is the large middle portion. The pyloric part is the relatively shape bend on the lesser curvature before emptying into the duodenum.
