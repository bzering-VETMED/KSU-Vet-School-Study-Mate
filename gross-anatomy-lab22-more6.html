Spleen
📝 Slide note (professor)
The spleen lies in the superficial leaf of the greater omentum to the left of the median plane along the greater curvature of the stomach. Its position, shape and degree of distention are variable. In cadavers that were euthanized with barbiturates before preservation, the spleen will be severely engorged as that is a sequela of barbiturates.
