Schultz 2025 Exam 3 (Lectures 22–23) · Q1in Exam 3 scopeHow many connexins must be in each of the adjoining cells to form one gap junction? (Select one correct answer.)
  1. A.1
  2. B.3
  3. C.6
  4. D.12
  5. E.30.2 to 45
⬇️ Answer?
C. 6
💡 Why + source
Each cell contributes one connexon, a hexamer of 6 connexins; the full gap junction channel has 12. 'In each of the adjoining cells' means 6.

Schultz 2025 Exam 3 (Lectures 22–23) · Q3in Exam 3 scope
For questions 3-5: Leydig cells in the testes secrete testosterone into circulation. When exposed to testosterone, epithelial cells lining the vas deferens will express high levels of prostaglandin H synthase 2 (PTGS2 or COX2). Subsequently, when these cells are exposed to locally released bradykinin, they will secrete prostaglandin D (PGD) into the lumen and secrete prostaglandin E (PGE) into the interstitial fluid bathing the epithelial basolateral cell membranes. Here, PGE can interact with a G-protein coupled receptor (EP2) on these epithelial cells to stimulate adenylyl cyclase, generate cAMP, activate protein kinase A (PKA), phosphorylate an ion channel and ultimately secrete bicarbonate into the lumen.
In this scenario, which component is a cytosolic second messenger? (Select one correct answer.)
  1. A.cAMP
  2. B.G-protein coupled receptor (EP2)
  3. C.PKA (cAMP-dependent protein kinase)
  4. D.Prostaglandin E
  5. E.Prostaglandin H synthase 2
⬇️ Answer?
A. cAMP
💡 Why + source
Cyclic AMP is the small cytosolic second messenger made by adenylyl cyclase. EP2 is the receptor, protein kinase A is the effector enzyme, prostaglandin E is the first messenger.

  3. C.Nitric oxide (NO) to guanylyl cyclase
  4. D.Testosterone to sex hormone binding protein
  5. E.Testosterone to an androgen receptor
⬇️ Answer?
C. Nitric oxide (NO) to guanylyl cyclase
💡 Why + source
Nitric oxide diffuses from endothelium to nearby smooth muscle (paracrine) and binds soluble guanylyl cyclase, a cytosolic enzyme acting as receptor. Histamine/H1 and nicotinic receptors are membrane receptors.

Schultz 2025 Exam 3 (Lectures 22–23) · Q10in Exam 3 scopeselect allWhich of the following are correct values for the typical concentration of free ionized Ca²⁺ in a cell at rest? (select two correct answers)
  1. A.10⁻⁶ M (2x10⁻⁶ Eq/l)
  2. B.10⁻⁷ mM (2x10⁻⁷ mEq/l)
  3. C.10⁻⁴ mM (2x10⁻⁴ mEq/l)
  4. D.100 μM (200 μEq/l)
  5. E.100 nM (200 nEq/l)
⬇️ Answer?
C. 10⁻⁴ mM (2x10⁻⁴ mEq/l)
E. 100 nM (200 nEq/l)
💡 Why + source
Resting free calcium = 100 nanomolar = 10^-4 millimolar; calcium is divalent, so equivalents are double (200 nanoequivalents/liter, 2x10^-4 milliequivalents/liter). 10^-6 molar is 1 micromolar, 10x too high.
