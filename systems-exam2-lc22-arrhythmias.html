Immediate CPR + defibrillation, then correct reversible causes.
Treatment

Cardioversion vs defibrillation?

Tap to reveal
Cardioversion = synchronized to R wave for organized tachyarrhythmias. Defibrillation = unsynchronized for V-fib/pulseless VT.
Treatment

Pacemaker indications?

Tap to reveal
Refractory symptomatic bradyarrhythmias, especially sick sinus syndrome and advanced AV block.
Treatment

Atropine mechanism?

Tap to reveal
M₂ antagonist that removes the parasympathetic brake and speeds HR.
Treatment

Why block K⁺ channels?

Slow repolarization → longer refractory period → helps interrupt reentry.
