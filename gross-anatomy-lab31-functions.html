👁️ Extraocular muscles

PeriorbitaFibrous CT + smooth muscle cone around the eyeball + its muscles; incise lengthwise
Rectus muscles (4)Dorsal · ventral · lateral · medial (medial is deepest)
Obliquus muscles (2)Dorsal oblique + ventral oblique
Dorsal obliqueDorsomedial; tendon runs through the trochlea, inserts dorsally + obliquely on the sclera
TrochleaCartilage at the medial angle of the eye; pulley for the dorsal oblique tendon
Ventral obliqueFibers run obliquely across the ventral eyeball; ventral to the ventral rectus
Levator palpebrae superiorisVery thin, ON TOP of the dorsal rectus; elevates the upper eyelid
Retractor bulbi4 small muscles surrounding the optic nerve

💧 Glands

Lacrimal glandUnder the orbital ligament (cut it); produces tears
Superficial gland of the third eyelidGlandular tissue on the medial (bulbar) side of the third eyelid

🔵 Eyeball — 3 coats

External fibrous coatCornea + sclera + limbus
LimbusCorneoscleral junction
Middle vascular coat = uveaIris + ciliary body + ciliary processes + choroid (black layer)
PupilOpening in the iris; controls light reaching the lens
Ciliary processesFinger-like folds off the ciliary body
Internal coat = retinaNervous layer: pars optica (over choroid) · pars ciliaris (over ciliary body) · pars iridica (back of iris)
Ora serrataLine where the thick pars optica meets the thin pars ciliaris retinae
Tapetum lucidumShiny reflective layer of the choroid (eye shine); seen in the fundus
FundusWhat you see through an ophthalmoscope
Optic diskWhere the optic nerve starts; retina will not peel off here

🫧 Chambers + lens

Anterior chamberBetween the cornea and iris — aqueous humor
Posterior chamberBehind the iris, in front of the lens/ciliary processes — aqueous humor
Vitreous chamberBehind the lens — vitreous body (jelly)
ZonuleSuspends the lens: ciliary body + zonular fibers

🩸 Superficial veins (drainage order)

Dorsal nasal vein + angularis oculi veinJoin at the medial canthus → facial vein
Facial vein + lingual vein→ linguofacial vein
Linguofacial vein + maxillary vein→ external jugular vein

⚡ Superficial nerves

Facial (seventh cranial) nerveMOTOR; root found rostral to the mandibular gland, under the parotid
Auriculopalpebral nerveFacial branch, dorsal → rostral auricular branches + palpebral branches
Dorsal buccal branchFacial branch on the masseter, DORSAL to the parotid duct
Ventral buccal branchFacial branch along the mandible, VENTRAL to the parotid duct
Auriculotemporal nerveSENSORY; mandibular branch of the trigeminal (fifth cranial) nerve; piggybacks on the dorsal buccal branch

🦋 Neck viscera

Thyroid glandLeft + right lobes ventrolateral to the trachea/larynx, joined by an isthmus
Parathyroid glands2 per thyroid: external (visible, lighter) + internal (not seen)
Tracheal cartilagesC-shaped, open dorsally; gap bridged by the trachealis muscle (smooth)
Pharyngoesophageal limenRidge marking pharynx → esophagus transition
