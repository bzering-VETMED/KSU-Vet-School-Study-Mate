Card 1

Osmosis

Tap to reveal
Net diffusion of water across a semi-permeable membrane: low solute → high solute. Passive.
Card 2

Osmotic pressure (Π)

Tap to reveal
Pressure exerted by water diffusing down its gradient; proportional to particle number. Π=RT(Φic).
Card 3

Colligative property

Tap to reveal
Depends on particle number only, not identity, size, mass, or charge.
Card 4

Φ — osmotic coefficient

Tap to reveal
Unitless correction for non-ideal solutions; near 1; sugars can exceed 1.
Card 5

i

Tap to reveal
Number of particles one molecule makes in solution.
Card 6

Φic

Tap to reveal
Osmotically effective concentration; numerically osmolarity.
Card 7

Osmolarity vs osmolality

Tap to reveal
Osmoles/L solution vs osmoles/kg solvent.
Card 8

Tonicity

Tap to reveal
Relative to RBC: isotonic no change, hypertonic shrink, hypotonic swell.
Card 9

Aquaporin

Tap to reveal
Water channel; provides permeability, not driving force.
Card 10

Sequestered solute

Tap to reveal
Impermeant net-negative proteins/nucleic acids attract counter-ions; counter-ions create osmotic load.
Card 11

Donnan effect

Tap to reveal
Impermeant charged solutes alter distribution of permeant ions.
Card 12

Donnan rules

Tap to reveal
Electroneutrality within compartments; permeant ion products equalize, individual concentrations do not.
Card 13

Gibbs-Donnan equilibrium

Tap to reveal
Final state has standing ion gradients and osmotic tendency toward swelling.
Card 14

Na+/K+ ATPase 3:2

Tap to reveal
3 Na+ out : 2 K+ in. Net solute export opposes swelling; net positive export is electrogenic.
Card 15

Gradient directions

Tap to reveal
Inward Na+, outward K+, outward osmotic gradient.
Card 16

Cardiac glycosides

Tap to reveal
Inhibit Na+/K+ ATPase; source examples include digoxin/digitoxin, convallotoxin, bufotoxin/bufogenin, cymarin.
Card 17

LC06 arc

Tap to reveal
Trapped anions → counter-ions → osmolarity rises → water enters → swelling risk → pump exports net solute.
