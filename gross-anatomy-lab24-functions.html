⚠️ Precision traps

  • Vaginal artery (♀) = prostatic artery (♂).
  • Cranial branch: uterine artery (♀) / artery of the ductus deferens (♂) → gives the caudal vesical · caudal branch = middle rectal.
  • Artery of the penis ends as 3: artery of the BULB, DEEP artery, DORSAL artery.
  • Pouches dorsal → ventral: pararectal fossa, rectogenital, vesicogenital, pubovesical.
