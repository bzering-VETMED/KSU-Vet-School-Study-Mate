SECTION 1 – Electrocardiophysiology & Electrocardiogram

Q1Which channel is mainly responsible for section A in the depolarization profile of this regular cardiomyocyte?

✅ C · Section A = phase 2 plateau → calcium in through L-type channels (balanced by potassium out).

Q2Which class of antiarrhythmics acts primarily on the channel responsible for section B?

✅ B · Section B = phase 0 fast upstroke = sodium in (Nav1.5) → class 1 sodium-channel blockers (lidocaine).

Q3Cardiomyocytes pass an action potential directly to neighboring cardiomyocytes through:

✅ B · Cardiomyocytes have NO nicotinic receptors; gap junctions in intercalated discs make the heart depolarize as one wave (LC20).

Q4Calcium that enters during the plateau triggers contraction by binding to:

✅ D · Calcium transient in phase 2 binds troponin C → cross-bridges form (LC20 excitation–contraction coupling).

Q5If both the sinoatrial node and the atrioventricular node stop firing, which tissue would most likely pace the ventricles, and at about what rate?

✅ C · Automaticity hierarchy: sinoatrial node ~100 → atrioventricular node ~40 → Purkinje fibers ~25 (LC20).

Q6Which signaling sequence explains how norepinephrine increases heart rate?

✅ A · Sympathetic tug: norepinephrine → β1 → Gs → ↑cAMP → more funny current → steeper phase 4 → faster rate.

Q7Which electrode placement is correct for a standard ECG?

✅ B · White on right · snow (white) over grass (green) · smoke (black) over fire (red).

Q8What is the angle of lead II?

✅ B · Lead I 0° · II +60° · aVF +90° · III +120° · aVL −30° · aVR −150°.

Q9The ECG marked “A” is consistent with:

✅ B · PR gets longer, longer, longer, then a P wave with no QRS. Then the cycle repeats.

Q10Which of the following are NOT true about the ECG marked “A”? (select all that apply)

✅ A, C · Dropped beats make it irregular, and one P has no QRS. Every QRS still has a P, and the QRS shape is normal.

Q11The ECG marked “B” is consistent with which diagnosis?

✅ A · No distinct P waves (wavy fibrillatory baseline) + irregularly irregular R-R + narrow QRS.

Q12Which treatments would be appropriate for rate control in the patient with ECG “B”? (select all that apply)

✅ A, B, C · LC21–22: atrial fibrillation rate control = β-blockers, calcium channel blockers, digoxin. Pacemakers are for bradyarrhythmias.

Q13The ECG marked “C” is consistent with which diagnosis?

✅ E · A run of fast, wide, bizarre QRS complexes with no consistent P waves.

Q14The patient with ECG “C” is unstable. Which would be an appropriate immediate drug?

✅ A · Ventricular tachycardia: emergency stabilization with lidocaine and/or electrical cardioversion (LC21–22).

Q15The six-lead ECG marked “D” is consistent with:

✅ C · Lead II QRS is negative (not normal) → check lead I: negative → RIGHT axis deviation.

Q16A monitor shows normal-looking complexes at 90/min, but you cannot palpate a pulse and the Doppler is silent. This is:

✅ B · Electrical activity without contraction = excitation–contraction coupling has failed. Always confirm with pulse/Doppler (step 6).
SECTION 2 – Circulatory Microanatomy, Blood Smears, Bone Marrow & Hemostasis

Q17Which layer of the heart wall contains the coronary vessels and is continuous with the visceral pericardium?

✅ B · Epicardium = mesothelium + connective tissue + fat, carries coronary vessels and nerves (LC19).

Q18Compared with contractile cardiomyocytes, Purkinje fibers have:

✅ B · Large diameter (less resistance), pale cytoplasm, fewer myofibrils, more glycogen, many gap junctions, many sodium channels (LC19).

Q19Which vessel has numerous concentric elastic lamellae in its tunica media?

✅ B · Elastic arteries store energy in systole and release it in diastole (Windkessel effect).

Q20Which valves are anchored to papillary muscles by chordae tendineae?

✅ A · Semilunar valves have no chordae tendineae (LC19).

Q21On a normal equine blood smear you would expect:

✅ D · Horses: rouleaux (coin stacks), little central pallor, big bright-orange eosinophil granules.

Q22Compared with canine platelets, feline platelets are typically:

✅ A

Q23The smallest blood element, about 2–3 µm, formed as cytoplasmic fragments of megakaryocytes, is the:

✅ B · Red blood cells = 6–8 µm; platelets = 2–3 µm (LC32).

Q24Erythropoietin is produced mainly by the:

✅ B · Low oxygen → kidney → erythropoietin → more red blood cells (LC33).

Q25Which sequence of granulocyte development is correct?

✅ A · Blast → Pro → plain → Meta → band (>50% dented) → segmented.

Q26Which could cause a NON-regenerative anemia? (select all that apply)

✅ B, D · Non-regenerative = marrow can’t respond: no erythropoietin (kidney), iron deficiency, marrow disease, endocrine, chronic blood loss.

Q27Aspirin reduces platelet recruitment by inhibiting which enzyme?

✅ A · COX-1 makes thromboxane A2 in platelets; aspirin blocks it (LC34).

Q28Which platelet receptor binds fibrinogen to cross-link neighboring platelets?

✅ B · GPVI = collagen · GPIb = von Willebrand factor · GPIIb/IIIa = fibrinogen (aggregation).

Q29A dog ate an anticoagulant rodenticide 3 days ago. Which clotting factors will be deficient?

✅ B · Vitamin K–dependent factors: II, VII, IX, X (“1972”), plus proteins C, S, Z.

Q30Which natural anticoagulant inactivates factors Va and VIIIa?

✅ B · Antithrombin = thrombin + Xa · Protein C/S = Va + VIIIa · TFPI = tissue factor–VIIa.
SECTION 3 – Defensive Cells & Lymphatics

Q31Granulocyte colony-stimulating factor (G-CSF) most directly increases production of:

✅ D · Transcript: G-CSF is “a misnomer”; it pushes “strongly towards neutrophils.” IL-5 = eosinophils · IL-3 = basophils · M-CSF = monocytes.

Q32Basophils are especially sensitive to activation by:

✅ B · IgE → basophils/mast cells → histamine → allergic (type 2) responses.

Q33Cytotoxic T-cells recognize peptides presented on:

✅ B · Rule of 8: CD8 × MHC I = 8 · CD4 × MHC II = 8. MHC I is on almost every cell.

Q34Somatic hypermutation that strengthens B-cell antibodies mainly happens in:

✅ A · Nodules in lymph nodes and Peyer’s patches (LC35 transcript).

Q35Lymph entering a lymph node first flows into the:

✅ C · Afferent vessel → subcapsular → trabecular → medullary sinus → efferent vessel at the hilus.

Q36High endothelial venules, where T-cells enter the lymph node, are located in the:

✅ D

Q37Pig lymph nodes differ from most species because they are:

✅ C · Pigs and elephants: reversed lymph node organization. Hemal nodes (ruminants, horses) filter blood.

Q38Immune reactions to blood-borne antigens in the spleen begin in the:

✅ A

Q39Positive selection of developing T-cells occurs in the thymic:

✅ B · Positive selection (cortex) keeps T-cells that recognize self-MHC. Negative selection (medulla) removes strongly self-reactive ones.

Q40Thymic (Hassall’s) corpuscles are found in the:

✅ B
SECTION 4 – Cardiovascular Physiology (Dr. Musch)

Q41Sympathetic nerves increase heart rate by releasing ___ onto ___ receptors.

✅ C · Part 1 slide 12: sympathetic → norepinephrine → β1 → ↑HR.

Q42Parasympathetic innervation of the heart is carried by the:

✅ D · Cranial nerve (vagus) → acetylcholine → muscarinic receptors → ↓HR.

Q43Acetylcholine slows pacemaker cells mainly by:

✅ B · Part 1 slide 15: more K⁺ out + less Ca²⁺ in → hyperpolarized → longer time to threshold.

Q44According to the current understanding, heart rate rises from rest to exercise because of:

✅ C · Old theory: release the brake first, then gas at ~45%. New: both happen at the same time.

Q45Atropine increases heart rate because it:

✅ B · Atropine blocks the brake (muscarinic). Propranolol blocks the gas (β1).

Q46In a pacemaker cell, one action-potential cycle takes 300 ms. The heart rate is:

✅ B · 60,000 ms ÷ 300 ms = 200 beats/min (Part 1 slide 16).

Q47In chronic heart failure, the heart rate response to exercise is blunted mainly because of:

✅ A · Chronically high catecholamines → β1 downregulation → chronotropic incompetence (Part 6 slide 7).

Q48Which measurement is an index of preload?

✅ B · Preload = LVEDP, left atrial pressure, or LVEDV · afterload = LV systolic pressure · contractility = LV dP/dt.

Q49Which determinant of stroke volume has a NEGATIVE effect on stroke volume?

✅ A

Q50Which is the index of left ventricular contractility?

✅ C

Q51Which would DECREASE preload?

✅ B · Part 1 slide 17: ↓preload from shock, hemorrhage, or ↑intrapericardial pressure (tamponade).

Q52β1 stimulation of contractile cardiomyocytes shifts the Frank-Starling curve:

✅ C · ↑contractility → more stroke volume at any LVEDV (Part 1 slide 19).

Q53At high exercise intensities, stroke volume plateaus. Which structure limits further filling?

✅ C · The pericardium limits how much the ventricle can fill (Part 5).

Q54Which vessels are the “regulators,” with the largest pressure drop and highest resistance?

✅ B · Part 2 slide 3: aorta ~100 → small arteries ~90 → arterioles ~60 → capillary entry ~30 → vena cava ~10. Resistance and wall/lumen ratio peak at the arterioles.

Q55According to Poiseuille’s law, if a vessel’s radius is cut in half, resistance increases about:

✅ D · R ∝ 1/r⁴ → (½)⁴ = 1/16.

Q56Systemic vascular resistance is roughly 7 times greater than pulmonary vascular resistance. (True/False)

✅ True · SVR ≈ 13.3 vs PVR ≈ 1.9 → ~7× (Part 2).

Q57During whole-body dynamic exercise, total systemic vascular resistance increases. (True/False)

✅ False · Total SVR FALLS because the huge vasodilation in active muscle wins.

Q58A true mixed venous blood sample (CvO2) can only be obtained from the:

✅ C · Swan-Ganz catheter → pulmonary artery (Part 1 slide 3).

Q59During exercise the a-vO2 difference widens mainly because:

✅ B · CaO2 stays ~20 (humans); CvO2 falls ~16 → ~4 as muscle extracts more O2.

Q60Which Starling force pulls fluid INTO the capillary?

✅ C · Hydrostatic pushes OUT, oncotic pulls IN (hose and sponge).

Q61Precapillary sphincters control capillary recruitment in skeletal muscle. (True/False)

✅ False · Musch: precapillary sphincters are a myth; capillaries have no smooth muscle.

Q62Because capillary diameter is fixed, capillary flow depends on the pressure difference along the capillary. (True/False)

✅ True · In heart failure ↑venous pressure → ↓ΔP (Part 3).

Q63Which variable increases LINEARLY with exercise intensity?

✅ C · Linear: VO2, HR, Q, systolic pressure, MAP. Curvilinear: SV, a-vO2.

Q64At maximal exercise, skeletal muscle receives about what fraction of cardiac output?

✅ D · Muscle 15% at rest → ~80% at max.

Q65“Functional sympatholysis” describes:

✅ A · Metabolites “cut the sympathetic wire” so active muscle stays dilated.

Q66During dynamic exercise, systolic and diastolic pressures change how?

✅ B · Systolic follows Q (↑); diastolic follows SVR (↓ or same).

Q67At maximal exercise, kidney blood flow falls from about 20% to about 2% of cardiac output. (True/False)

✅ True · “Drop the zero”: kidney 20 → 2%, gut 30 → 3%.

Q68Epinephrine dilates skeletal muscle arterioles through β2 receptors. (True/False)

✅ True · Part 4: epinephrine → β2 → vasodilation in muscle.

Q69The “hallmark” of chronic heart failure is:

✅ B · Musch: “That will be on your exam.”

Q70Heart failure with PRESERVED ejection fraction is most often associated with:

✅ B · Hypertrophic (thick) = preserved EF, from hypertension. Dilated = reduced EF, from MI.

Q71The drug of choice for a patient presenting in ACUTE congestive heart failure is:

✅ C · Acute = Lasix (diuretic). Long-term = ACE inhibitor.

Q72In acute heart failure, the Frank-Starling curve shifts downward. (True/False)

✅ True · Same filling → less stroke volume. (Chronic HF → shifts right.)

Q73Left ventricular end-diastolic pressures above about 20 mmHg are associated with pulmonary congestion and edema. (True/False)

✅ True · LVEDP ~8 normal → ~25 in acute HF; >20 = decompensated congestive heart failure.

Q74Angiotensin II is a weaker vasoconstrictor than norepinephrine. (True/False)

✅ False · Strength: endothelin > angiotensin II > norepinephrine.

Q75A human has a blood pressure of 120/80 mmHg. The mean arterial pressure is about:

✅ B · 80 + (40 ÷ 3) ≈ 93.

Q76About 70% of the blood volume is found in the:

✅ D · Veins = capacitance vessels.

Q77The stroke volume of the left ventricle equals the stroke volume of the right ventricle. (True/False)

✅ True · Q(RV) = Q(LV) (Part 1 slide 7).

Q78Blood velocity is highest in the capillaries. (True/False)

✅ False · Capillaries: largest total cross-sectional area → LOWEST velocity.

Q79Pressure in the pulmonary circulation stays pulsatile all the way through the capillaries. (True/False)

✅ True · Systemic pulse is lost in the capillaries; pulmonary stays pulsatile (Part 2).
