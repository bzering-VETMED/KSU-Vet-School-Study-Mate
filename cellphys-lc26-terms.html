⚠️ Precision traps

  • cGMP vs cAMP: ANP → guanylyl cyclase → cGMP → protein kinase G. ACTH → cAMP → cortisol. Don't swap the letters.
  • PDE5 vs PDE2: PDE5 breaks down cGMP (Viagra target). PDE2 is activated by cGMP and breaks down cAMP (how ANP ↓cortisol).
  • ANP = atria (modest stretch, physiologic) vs BNP = ventricle (distension, pathologic). 'B' was historically 'brain' but it is NOT secreted by brain.
  • Units: pM for natriuretic peptides (ANP 10 pM normal, >100 pM pathologic; BNP ≤1 pM, >200 pM bad) vs mM for glucose. Watch pM vs nM vs mM options.
  • Autophosphorylation (receptor tyrosine kinase: insulin, growth factors) vs JAK2 phosphorylates the receptor (EPO receptor has NO intrinsic kinase).
  • MAPKKK → MAPKK → MAPK: each K phosphorylates the next; only MAPK enters the nucleus. RAS activates MAPKKK (Schultz's correction), it doesn't phosphorylate it.
  • RAS-GTP = ON, RAS-GDP = OFF. SOS loads GTP; RAS's own GTPase turns it off. Mutant RAS without GTPase = always on → tumor (proto-oncogene).
  • Insulin's two arms: IRS-1 → PI3K = insert GLUT4 now; SOS → RAS → MAPK = transcribe GLUT4 for next time.
  • EPO's two arms: STAT5 → bcl-2 = survival (↓apoptosis); MAPK → cyclin D = proliferation. Don't swap the products.
  • GLUT2 = β-cell glucose sensor; GLUT4 = insulin-regulated target-tissue transporter. IDD = low insulin, IRD = normal/high insulin, BOTH treated with insulin.
  • Corin cleaves pro-ANP (126 → 28 amino acids) vs renin cleaves angiotensinogen. ANP ↓ renin, aldosterone, ADH; ANP ↑ GFR.

Enzyme-linked receptorS1, S22; transcriptCell-surface receptor whose cytosolic domain IS an enzyme or is tightly associated with one; ligand is water-soluble.
Why a membrane receptor?transcriptWater-soluble ligands can't cross the membrane, so the receptor faces outside (contrast steroid hormones' cytosolic receptors, LC25).
Receptor guanylyl cyclaseS2, S4Receptor with an intrinsic guanylyl cyclase domain: guanosine triphosphate (GTP) → cyclic guanosine monophosphate (cGMP). Example: ANP receptor.
Atrial natriuretic peptide (ANP)S3, S7; transcriptPeptide from atrial secretory granules, released by stretch (pressure/volume); lowers blood pressure. Normal ~10 pM; >100 pM pathologic.
B-type natriuretic peptide (BNP)transcriptHistorically 'brain' natriuretic peptide but secreted by the VENTRICLE on distension. Normal ≤1 pM; >200 pM = badly distended heart.
ANP receptor structureS4Single membrane-spanning protein: extracellular binding domain, transmembrane domain, intracellular guanylyl cyclase domain. Unbound monomer = inactive.
Dimerization (ANP receptor)S3, S4; transcriptANP binding dimerizes the receptor; the two cyclase halves come together and become active.
Cyclic guanosine monophosphate (cGMP)S4Second messenger made from GTP by guanylyl cyclase; activates protein kinase G.
Protein kinase G (PKG)S4cGMP-activated kinase; phosphorylates CELL-TYPE/TISSUE-SPECIFIC target proteins.
Myosin phosphatase target subunit 1 (MYPT-1)S3 figure; transcriptPKG target in vascular smooth muscle; leads to activation of myosin light chain phosphatase.
Myosin light chain phosphatase (MLCP)S3 figure; transcriptDephosphorylates myosin light chain → smooth muscle relaxes → vasodilation → blood pressure drops.
Phosphodiesterase (PDE)S4; transcriptTerminates the signal: converts cGMP to GMP (slide typo 'PPE').
Phosphodiesterase 5 (PDE5)transcriptBreaks down cGMP; inhibited by sildenafil, tadalafil, vardenafil.
PDE5 inhibitorstranscriptSildenafil (Viagra), tadalafil (Cialis), vardenafil (Levitra): erectile dysfunction, PULMONARY ARTERIAL HYPERTENSION, prostatic hyperplasia.
CorintranscriptProtease that cleaves the 126-amino-acid natriuretic prohormone to the 28-amino-acid active peptide.
Natriuretic peptide receptor A/B (NPR-A/B)S7; transcriptANP/BNP receptors (slide 'NP-A/B'); mainly adrenal gland, kidney, vascular smooth muscle; → cGMP → PKG.
Phosphodiesterase 2 (PDE2)transcriptIn adrenal cells, cGMP ↑ PDE2 → breaks down cyclic adenosine monophosphate (cAMP) → ↓ACTH-stimulated cortisol (and modestly ↓aldosterone).
Adrenocorticotropic hormone (ACTH)transcriptRaises cAMP in adrenal cortisol-secreting cells → cortisol; ANP opposes this via PDE2.
Renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system (RAAS)S5, S6↓Renal perfusion → renin → angiotensin I → (ACE) angiotensin II → aldosterone, vasoconstriction, ADH, sympathetic activity → ↑blood pressure.
Angiotensin-converting enzyme (ACE)S5On pulmonary and renal endothelium; converts angiotensin I → angiotensin II.
Angiotensin II type 1 (AT1) receptortranscriptAngiotensin II receptor in adrenal cortex, arterioles, pituitary; a G protein-coupled receptor (GPCR).
Antidiuretic hormone (ADH) / V2 receptorS5; transcriptADH from posterior pituitary; V2 receptor in kidney = Gs-coupled GPCR → collecting duct water absorption. ANP ↓ADH.
ENaC and ROMKtranscriptEpithelial sodium channel and renal outer medullary potassium channel; aldosterone ↑ their expression in distal convoluted tubule and collecting duct.
ANP net renal/vascular effectS6, S7; transcriptAfferent dilation → ↑GFR; ↓renin → ↓angiotensin II → ↓aldosterone; ↓ADH; vasodilation → ↓Na⁺/water retention → ↓blood pressure.
Glomerular filtration rate (GFR)S7; transcriptRises when ANP dilates the afferent arteriole without changing the efferent arteriole.
Receptor tyrosine kinase (RTK)S2, S8Receptor with intrinsic tyrosine kinase; ligand brings two halves together → autophosphorylation. Mostly growth factor receptors.
AutophosphorylationS8, S11The receptor's own tyrosine kinase phosphorylates its own tyrosines after ligand binding.
Growth factor receptorsS8; transcriptFGF, VEGF, NGF, IGF-1, EGF, PDGF receptors (S8): ↑proliferation, ↑cell longevity, ↓programmed cell death.
Insulin receptorS11Two extracellular (binding) + two membrane-spanning (cytosolic tyrosine kinase) subunits; signals via TWO arms (IRS-1 and MAPK).
Insulin receptor substrate 1 (IRS-1)S11Adapter/scaffolding protein; binds activated insulin receptor and activates PI3K (skeletal muscle, adipose).
Phosphoinositide 3-kinase (PI3K)S11; transcriptPhosphorylates PIP2 → PIP3; through a series of steps drives GLUT4 vesicle fusion with the membrane.
Akt/PKB, PDK1, mTORC1transcriptCondensed steps between PI3K and GLUT4 insertion; Schultz: 'I don't think you need to know all those steps.'
Glucose transporter 4 (GLUT4)S11, S14Insulin-regulated glucose transporter stored in vesicles; inserted into the membrane → facilitated diffusion of glucose.
Glucose transporter 1 (GLUT1)S12Constitutive glucose transporter; also a transcription target of the insulin MAPK arm.
Glucose transporter 2 (GLUT2)S14; transcriptGlucose transporter of the pancreatic β cell; lets glucose in to raise ATP:ADP.
Son of sevenless (SOS)S12, S19; transcriptSlide: 'adapter protein'; Schultz: GTP EXCHANGE FACTOR that swaps GDP for GTP on RAS.
RAS (rat sarcoma virus protein)S2, S13; transcriptMONOMERIC G protein/GTPase: GTP-bound = on, GDP-bound = off; its GTPase is the timer.
Monomeric vs heterotrimeric G proteintranscriptRAS = single subunit in RTK pathways; heterotrimeric (α, β, γ) = GPCR pathways (LC27).
Mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK) cascadeS12; transcriptMAPKKK (Raf) → MAPKK (MEK) → MAPK (ERK); each phosphorylates the next.
MitogentranscriptMito = thread (chromosome in division) + gen = generate → drives cell proliferation.
MAPK (ERK)S12; transcriptThe ONLY kinase in the cascade that translocates into the nucleus; activates early-gene transcription.
Early genes: myc, jun, fosS12, S19Transcribed after MAPK enters the nucleus; jun and fos proteins co-activate target genes (GLUT4, GLUT1, cyclin D).
Proto-oncogeneS13; transcriptNormal growth-signaling gene; mutated RAS lacking GTPase stays on → excessive proliferation → cancer. 'Your first example.'
Pancreatic β cell insulin secretionS14; transcriptGlucose via GLUT2 → ↑ATP:ADP → ATP-sensitive K⁺ channel closes → depolarization → insulin secretion.
Insulin target tissues (S14)S14Liver, skeletal muscle, cardiac muscle, fibrocytes (GLUT4 drawn).
Insulin-deficient diabetes mellitus (IDD)S15Defective β cells → insufficient secretion, LOW insulin; treated with insulin.
Insulin-resistant diabetes mellitus (IRD)S15; transcriptDefective receptor/signaling in peripheral tissues; NORMAL or HIGH insulin; still treated with insulin (fewest side effects).
Glucose unit conversiontranscript5 mM glucose = 90 mg/dL ('just an FYI', systems link).
Starvation in plentyS16; transcriptCells starve despite hyperglycemia: weight loss, polydipsia/polyuria, weakness; hyperlipidemia, ketonuria, uremia.
Osmotic diuresisS16Glucose above renal threshold → glucosuria drags water → polyuria.
KetoacidosisS16Fatty acids → acetyl-CoA → acetoacetate → 4-OH butyrate + acetone → ketoacidosis → acidotic coma; ketonuria.
Uremia (diabetic)S16Amino acid metabolism for energy → urea production → uremia.
Tyrosine-kinase-associated receptorS2, S17, S18NO intrinsic kinase; after ligand-induced dimerization recruits a non-receptor tyrosine kinase (JAK2).
Erythropoietin (EPO)S20Made mainly by kidney; ↑ when oxygen partial pressure (PO2) is low; ↑ erythroid progenitor proliferation in bone marrow.
Janus kinase 2 (JAK2)S18; transcriptNon-receptor tyrosine kinase; binds dimerized EPO receptor, phosphorylates ITSELF and the RECEPTOR. Janus = god of doorways.
Signal transducer and activator of transcription 5 (STAT5)S18Phosphorylated by receptor/JAK2 complex → nucleus → transcription of bcl-2.
B-cell lymphoma 2 (BCL-2)S18; transcriptLongevity gene: prevents apoptosis, promotes survival (EPO's JAK-STAT arm).
Cyclin DS19Secondary gene from EPO's MAPK arm (via jun/fos); needed for cell division → proliferation.
Oclacitinib (Apoquel)transcriptJAK inhibitor used for canine atopic dermatitis ('totally off target' side note).
Recombinant human EPO (rhEPO)S20, S21Replaces EPO when a failing kidney can't make enough; misused as a doping agent.
EPO misuse riskS20↑Red blood cells → ↑blood viscosity → embolism risk.
Isoelectric focusing (EPO test)S21; transcriptSeparates native vs recombinant EPO by charge (gel pH ~3.8–5.2); basis of rhEPO urine test.
Receptor tyrosine phosphataseS2; transcriptSignals by REMOVING phosphates; example CD45 ('that's all I'm going to say').
Receptor serine/threonine kinaseS2; transcriptTGF-β, bone morphogenetic protein (BMP), Müllerian inhibiting substance → SMAD; FK506 link to immunosuppression; not covered.
