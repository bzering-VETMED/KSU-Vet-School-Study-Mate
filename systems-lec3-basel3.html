25 Q · LC38–39 pulmonary microanatomy · from Basel's slides only

Q1Which statement about respiratory epithelium is INCORRECT?

✅ A. It is simple cuboidal with many club cells · That's bronchiolar epithelium

Q2Snakes and many lizards ventilate their lungs mainly with:

✅ D. Muscles of the chest wall · Crocodilians = diaphragm · some lizards = buccal pumping

Q3Adult amphibians depend significantly on which for gas exchange?

✅ A. Skin, along with simple lungs · Larvae = gills and/or skin

Q4The glands in the tracheal submucosa are:

✅ B. Seromucous glands

Q5The relative number of club cells is highest in the:

✅ A. Respiratory bronchioles · Club cells increase down the tree

Q6Simple columnar/cuboidal ciliated epithelium with club cells, a muscularis mucosa, no glands, and no cartilage is a:

✅ C. Bronchiole

Q7In alveolar ducts, the epithelium is:

✅ D. Simple cuboidal, only in small patches · Sacs = no cuboidal left

Q8The stroma of the alveolar septum is made mostly of:

✅ C. Collagen and elastin · LC39 blood–air barrier slide

Q9Diffusion across the blood–air barrier is limited mainly by its:

✅ D. Area and thickness · Big area + thin barrier = efficient exchange

Q10The cause of feline asthma is:

✅ B. Over-reactive immune cells: more mucus and muscle contraction · Parasites and pneumonia were ruled out in the case

Q11Which is NOT a role of the pleural membrane and cavity?

✅ C. Exchanging O₂ with the pulmonary capillaries

Q12Submucosal glands of the airways are found in the:

✅ A. Submucosa

Q13Which is the correct airflow order?

✅ B. Nares → nasal cavity → pharynx → larynx → trachea

Q14Which structure does Basel classify as part of the LOWER airways?

✅ A. Larynx · Upper = nares, nasal cavity, pharynx

Q15Which feature is continuous in respiratory bronchioles but breaks into knobs in alveolar ducts?

✅ B. Smooth muscle (muscularis mucosa)

Q16Which is NOT true of the trachealis muscle?

✅ D. It secretes seromucous fluid

Q17Which is NOT a cell of the alveoli/alveolar septum?

✅ A. Club cell

Q18Compared with bronchial epithelium, bronchiolar epithelium is:

✅ C. Simple columnar/cuboidal with club cells

Q19Which airway has a continuous muscularis mucosa AND openings to alveoli?

✅ D. Respiratory bronchiole

Q20Which pair is correctly matched?

✅ A. Vestibular fold — respiratory epithelium

Q21Inflammation in feline asthma leads to all of these EXCEPT:

✅ C. Air entering the pleural cavity

Q22A connective tissue layer that blends into surrounding organs is called:

✅ B. Adventitia

Q23On Basel's upper airway slide, the hard and soft palates separate the nasal cavity from the:

✅ C. Oral cavity

Q24Alveolar macrophages:

✅ B. Phagocytose debris to keep alveoli clear

Q25Which change does NOT occur moving from bronchi to bronchioles?

✅ D. Glands increase
