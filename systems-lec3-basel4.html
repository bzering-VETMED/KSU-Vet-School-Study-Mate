🔬 Basel Practice Exam 4 · Misses

25 Q · LC38–39 pulmonary microanatomy · from Basel's slides only

Q1A biopsy of the olfactory region shows glands below the epithelium. Their secretion:

✅ B. Dissolves odorants and clears away old odors · Olfactory (Bowman's) glands

Q2In the olfactory epithelium, which cell type renews the epithelium?

✅ D. Basal cells · Basal = sits on the base, stem cell

Q3Fluid is removed from the pleural cavity faster than it enters. What does this, and what is the result?

✅ A. Lymphatics; slightly negative pleural pressure · Negative pressure holds the lungs open

Q4In which region did Basel say there is a lamina propria but NO muscularis mucosa?

✅ C. Nasal cavity · Trachea: may or may not see it

Q5The muscularis mucosa is MOST well developed in the:

✅ A. Bronchioles · Muscle replaces the cartilage

Q6The trachealis muscle is best described as:

✅ B. Muscle closing the back of the C-rings, separate from the mucosa · Slide lists it with the rings in the adventitia

Q7Basel said that where the trachea enters the thoracic cavity, its outer layer becomes:

✅ A. Serosa · Neck = adventitia · thorax = serosa (lecture)

Q8As airways become bronchioles, goblet cells are replaced by:

✅ B. Club (Clara) cells · Bronchi still have goblet cells, no club cells

Q9Which TWO cell types produce surfactant, per Basel?

✅ A. Club cells and type II pneumocytes · Club cells start it; type II cells make most

Q10A non-ciliated stem cell that detoxifies (CYP450) lives in the ___; a cuboidal surfactant-secreting cell lives in the ___.

✅ C. Bronchioles; alveoli · Club cell, then type II pneumocyte

Q11Small patches of cuboidal cells covering smooth muscle knobs are the hallmark of:

✅ B. Alveolar ducts

Q12The airway ends in a dead-end space with no knobs and no cuboidal cells. This is the:

✅ A. Alveolar sac

Q13The first airway with alveoli opening off its wall, still lined by cuboidal cells with continuous muscle, is the:

✅ C. Respiratory bronchiole

Q14How many types of bronchiole does Basel define for this course?

✅ B. Two: bronchiole and respiratory bronchiole · "I don't care" about the textbook subtypes

Q15The fiber framework (stroma) inside the wall between neighboring alveoli is made of:

✅ D. Collagen and elastin · Septum = the wall · stroma = its fibers

Q16Loose connective tissue directly beneath the airway epithelium is the:

✅ C. Lamina propria · Adventitia = outermost, blends into neighbors

Q17Which feature identifies the vestibular (false) fold?

✅ A. Respiratory epithelium with mixed glands · True fold = muscular, stratified squamous

Q18Cartilage PLATES (not rings) plus seromucous glands identify a:

✅ D. Bronchus

Q19Misregulation of diffuse neuroendocrine cells, which release serotonin, can contribute to:

✅ B. Asthma

Q20Club cells break down mucus mainly to:

✅ D. Keep mucus off the gas-exchange barrier · Lecture: mucus could cover the diffusion barrier

Q21Going from bronchioles to alveolar sacs, smooth muscle:

✅ C. Decreases until it is absent in the sacs

Q22In the nasal cavity, what supporting tissue often appears in the adventitia?

✅ D. Bone of the conchae and skull

Q23Horses are obligate nasal breathers because of the position of the:

✅ C. Epiglottis

Q24In spontaneous pneumothorax, air enters the pleural cavity through failure of the:

✅ D. Visceral pleura · Usually ruptured blebs/bullae

Q25Moving from the trachea to the bronchi, the epithelium:

✅ A. Stays pseudostratified but gets shorter · Also fewer goblet cells
