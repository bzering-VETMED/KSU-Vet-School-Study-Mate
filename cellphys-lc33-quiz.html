18 questions · Dr. Basel style · Exam 4

Exam-style questions on LC33 only, written from the slides (not an official key). Tap an answer to check it; select-all needs ✓ Check. Keep your FIRST answer unless you can name the fact that proves it wrong. 📖 = slide number.

Q1Superoxide anion, the most basic reactive oxygen species, is formed by which process?

✅ B · O2 + 1 e− → O2−·: gaining one electron is a single-electron reduction.📖 S6

Q2Which of the following is NOT a stage of a free radical chain reaction?

✅ C · S8 lists initiation, propagation, termination. Dismutation is what superoxide dismutase does.📖 S8

Q3During the propagation stage of a radical chain reaction, what happens?

✅ D · Each radical steals one electron from another molecule, making a new radical; termination is radical + radical.📖 S8

Q4In a neutrophil phagosome, NADPH oxidase has just produced superoxide. Which enzyme acts next in the respiratory burst?

✅ A · Burst order: NADPH oxidase → superoxide dismutase (H2O2) → myeloperoxidase (HOCl).📖 S10

Q5Myeloperoxidase produces bleach in the phagosome by combining hydrogen peroxide with which substrate?

✅ D · H2O2 + HCl → HOCl + H2O (S10).📖 S10

Q6In the Fenton reaction, which metal ion does the slide name as catalyzing hydroxyl radical formation from hydrogen peroxide?

✅ A · S10 panel header: 'Fe2+ and the Fenton Reaction' (2 H2O2 → H2O + HOO· + HO·).📖 S10

Q7Nitric oxide synthase uses NADPH and O2 to convert arginine into which products?

✅ C · Arginine → citrulline + ·NO (S10).📖 S10

Q8A dog has a femoral artery clot removed after hours of limb ischemia. Which metabolite built up during ischemia and drives a superoxide burst at reperfusion?

✅ B · S9: succinate builds up at Complex II during ischemia; reperfusion → superoxide.📖 S9

Q9A cat given Tylenol after a fall presents cyanotic, drooling, and lethargic. Why is acetaminophen so toxic in cats?

✅ A · Glucuronidation is the main non-toxic route; without it, all of the dose goes to NAPQI.📖 S12

Q10The same acetaminophen-toxic cat needs treatment. Which choice matches the slide's treatment and its rationale?

✅ C · NAPQI is detoxified by GSH; N-acetylcysteine is a GSH precursor.📖 S19

Q11Which enzyme uses NADPH to convert glutathione disulfide (GS–SG) back into two reduced glutathione molecules?

✅ B · Peroxidase consumes GSH on H2O2; reductase regenerates GSH with NADPH.📖 S16

Q12Glutathione is a tripeptide composed of which three amino acids?

✅ D · S16: glutamate–cysteine–glycine.📖 S16

Q13After vitamin E quenches a lipid radical, it becomes a stable radical itself. Which antioxidant regenerates it?

✅ C · S18 shows vitamin C donating H+ + e− to the vitamin E radical.📖 S18

Q14Which statement about the central respiratory receptors is correct?

✅ D · Central (ventrolateral medulla) receptors do not primarily sense O2.📖 S22

Q15A carotid body glomus cell becomes hypoxic and its K+ channels close. What happens next?

✅ B · K+ closed → depolarize → Ca2+ channels open → neurotransmitter release.📖 S23

Q16In a well-oxygenated cell, what happens to hypoxia-inducible factor (HIF)?

✅ A · High O2 → hydroxylated HIF → proteasome; low O2 → HIF survives.📖 S24

Q17During hyperoxia, how does the ASK1 pathway become activated?

✅ A · Reduced Trx holds ASK1; H2O2 oxidizes Trx → ASK1 free → MAPK cascade → pro-apoptotic genes.📖 S25

Q18Which of the following is NOT listed as a product of ROS damage to macromolecules?

✅ C · S11: oxidized/cross-linked DNA, DNA adducts; lipid peroxides/adducts; oxidized amino acids, protein dimers/adducts. Glucuronide is from the acetaminophen case, not ROS damage.📖 S11
