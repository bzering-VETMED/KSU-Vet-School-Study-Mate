Frontal sinus
📝 Slide note (professor): The frontal sinus is located between the outer and inner tables of the frontal bone. There are 3 frontal sinuses. The lateral frontal sinus is much larger than the other and is the only one of clinical relevance. The rostral frontal sinus is small and lies between the median plan and the orbit. The ethmoid labyrinth bulges into this sinus. Medial frontal sinus lies between the median septum and the walls of the other two sinuses. It is very small and may be absent.
