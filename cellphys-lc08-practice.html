LC08 practice (written from slides) · Q1in Exam 3 scopeWhat is the pH of a 1 mM solution of HCl, a monoprotic strong acid? (select one)
  1. A.1
  2. B.2
  3. C.3
  4. D.7
  5. E.11
⬇️ Answer?
C. 3
💡 Why + source
Strong acid dissociates fully: [H⁺] = 10⁻³ M, so pH = −log(10⁻³) = 3.

LC08 practice (written from slides) · Q4in Exam 3 scopeWhen the pH of a solution equals the pKa of a weak acid dissolved in it: (select one)

  2. B.[HA] = [A⁻]
  3. C.[HA] << [A⁻]
  4. D.The acid is fully dissociated
  5. E.The solution is at pH 7
⬇️ Answer?

LC08 practice (written from slides) · Q6in Exam 3 scopeFor a WEAK BASE drug, which form predominates when the pH is well below its pKa? (select one)
  1. A.Deprotonated, uncharged, more lipid soluble
  2. B.Protonated, charged, less lipid soluble
  3. C.Protonated, uncharged, more lipid soluble
  4. D.Deprotonated, charged, less lipid soluble
  5. E.Equal amounts of both forms
⬇️ Answer?
B. Protonated, charged, less lipid soluble
💡 Why + source
Low pH = lots of H⁺ → protonated. For a base, the protonated form (e.g., NH₄⁺) is the charged one → poor membrane crossing. Weak bases are "ionized at pH lower than pKa."

LC08 practice (written from slides) · Q15in Exam 3 scopeselect allH⁺ is added to blood: H⁺ + HCO₃⁻ ⇌ H₂CO₃ ⇌ H₂O + CO₂. Which statements are correct? (select ALL that are correct)
  1. A.The reaction shifts to the right
  2. B.Carbonic anhydrase catalyzes H₂CO₃ ⇌ H₂O + CO₂
  3. C.Increased respiratory rate vents CO₂ and pulls the reaction further right
  4. D.Bicarbonate concentration rises
  5. E.The body responds by decreasing respiratory rate
⬇️ Answer?
A. The reaction shifts to the right
B. Carbonic anhydrase catalyzes H₂CO₃ ⇌ H₂O + CO₂
C. Increased respiratory rate vents CO₂ and pulls the reaction further right
💡 Why + source
Added H⁺ is consumed by HCO₃⁻ (so bicarbonate FALLS), making carbonic acid → water + CO₂. Breathing faster removes CO₂ (mass action).
