⚠️ Precision traps

  • 📄 Built from the SLIDES only (lecture Wed 11/11) — stars and corrections update when the transcript arrives.
  • ⚠️ FLAG — palatine branch naming: slide 25 says the maxillary becomes the infraorbital 'after the DESCENDING PALATINE arises'; the overview diagram (slides 8, 10, 21, 22) just says 'palatine'; L28 (Dr. Diehl) gave minor palatine + a common trunk → major palatine + sphenopalatine. The vein figure (slide 56) labels major palatine + sphenopalatine. Same territory, different naming — ask which names the exam uses.
  • ⚠️ FLAG — slide 27 says superficial branches to the lips and nose come 'from the facial, mental and infraorbital NERVES' on a slide about vessels. Probably meant arteries/vessels (facial a., mental branches, infraorbital a.) — confirm in transcript.
  • ⚠️ FLAG — plexus name word order: slide 39 (Fig. 4-35 legend) says 'internal ventral vertebral venous plexus'; slides 47–48 say 'ventral internal vertebral venous plexus'. Same structure — write 'ventral internal vertebral venous plexus' for fill-ins unless told otherwise.
  • ⚠️ Internal vs external carotid: INTERNAL → arterial circle → brain; EXTERNAL → almost everything else in the head (slide 8). Carotid sinus is at the origin of the INTERNAL carotid (slides 10, 16).
  • ⚠️ Maxillary ARTERY (terminal branch of external carotid, through the alar canal) vs maxillary VEIN (tributary of external jugular) vs maxillary NERVE (trigeminal branch that travels WITH the artery across the pterygopalatine fossa and becomes the infraorbital nerve, slide 24).
  • ⚠️ Superficial temporal = LAST branch of the external carotid, ROSTRAL to the ear, smaller (slides 15–16). Caudal auricular = CAUDAL side of the ear (slide 14). Don't swap.
  • ⚠️ Transverse facial a. branches from the SUPERFICIAL TEMPORAL, not the facial (slide 17). Palpebral vessels also from the SUPERFICIAL TEMPORAL; malar a. from the MAXILLARY (slide 26).
  • ⚠️ Inferior alveolar a. arises BEFORE the maxillary enters the alar canal (slide 22, 'before' underlined). It enters the MANDIBULAR foramen and exits the MENTAL foramen — don't flip entry/exit.
  • ⚠️ Occipital a. JOINS the vertebral a. in the atlantal fossa (slide 33) — an anastomosis, not a branch-of.
  • ⚠️ Direction: arteries BRANCH; veins MERGE. Facial v. + lingual v. → linguofacial v.; linguofacial v. + maxillary v. → external jugular v. The two DIRECT tributaries of the external jugular are linguofacial + maxillary (slide 40) — NOT lingual or caudal auricular.
  • ⚠️ Ventral spinal a. + both vertebral aa. → BASILAR a. (slide 34). Basilar + internal carotid → arterial circle around pituitary + optic chiasm (slide 35).
  • ⚠️ Vertebral VEIN drains to the costocervical vertebral TRUNK (slide 48); more caudal intervertebral veins drain to the AZYGOUS vein.
  • ⚠️ Medial retropharyngeal lymph node = DEEP, the 'terminal node' of the head (slide 51). Lateral retropharyngeal and parotid are different nodes. Buccal node present in only 13/150 dogs (slide 53).
  • ⚠️ Enucleation danger = angularis oculi VEIN + malar ARTERY (slide 56) — vein vs artery pairing.
