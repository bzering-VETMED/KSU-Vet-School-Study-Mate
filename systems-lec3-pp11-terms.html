⚠️ Precision traps

  • Horse = HYPOventilates (PaCO₂ ↑) in max exercise · humans/most mammals HYPERventilate (PaCO₂ ↓)
  • Main cause of horse hypoxemia = short TRANSIT TIME, not hypoventilation
  • Phase I = cardiac output · Phase II = venous blood arrival · Phase III = steady state
  • Heavy = lactate STABLE (elevated) · severe = NOT stable
  • Gas exchange threshold (extra CO₂ from buffering) ≠ respiratory compensation point (PaCO₂ falls)
  • Muscle venous O₂ is LOWER than mixed venous
  • Rest → exercise: tidal volume rises FIRST, frequency later (quadrupeds: frequency locked to stride)
