⚠️ Precision traps

  • DEEP side of the rectus abdominis = cranial epigastric artery · SUPERFICIAL (pierced through) = cranial SUPERFICIAL epigastric artery.
  • Rectus thoracis ↔ rectus abdominis · transversus thoracis ↔ transversus abdominis (same layer idea).
  • Pulmonary (visceral) pleura = on the lung · costal, diaphragmatic, mediastinal pleura = PARIETAL.
  • Left lung: 2 lobes (cranial lobe has cranial + caudal PARTS) · right lung: 4 lobes (cranial, middle, caudal, accessory).
  • Cardiac notch = RIGHT lung, between cranial + middle lobes (~5th intercostal space).
  • Cranial vena cava = left + right BRACHIOCEPHALIC veins; each brachiocephalic = external jugular + subclavian. The right azygos vein is the LAST vein into the cranial vena cava.

🩸 Internal thoracic branches

Internal thoracic arteryBranch of the subclavian · runs inside the thorax along the sternum · under the transversus thoracis
Musculophrenic arteryInternal thoracic branch toward the diaphragm
Cranial epigastric arteryInternal thoracic branch · runs on the DEEP side of the rectus abdominis
Cranial superficial epigastric arteryFrom the cranial epigastric · pierces the rectus abdominis → skin + mammae
Transversus thoracisSkeletal muscle inside the sternum (≈ transversus abdominis) · reflect it to find the internal thoracic vessels

Mediastinal pleuraDouble fold: one layer from each pleural cavity · the mediastinum lies between
MediastinumMidline space between the two pleural cavities · heart, vessels, nerves, lymph nodes
Pulmonary (visceral) pleuraThin shiny membrane on the lung
Costal / diaphragmatic pleuraParietal pleura on the ribs / on the diaphragm
Plica venae cavaeDouble fold of pleura around the caudal vena cava
Pulmonary ligamentDouble fold attaching the lung to the dorsal body wall
Pericardial mediastinal pleuraMediastinal pleura over the heart
ThymusCranial mediastinum · large in young animals

🫁 Lungs + airway

Left lung lobesCranial lobe (cranial + caudal parts) + caudal lobe
Right lung lobesCranial, middle, caudal, accessory (cut through the mediastinum to see accessory)
Root of the lungVessels + bronchi enter · cut here to remove the lung
Cardiac notchRight lung, between cranial + middle lobes · direct access to the heart (~5th intercostal space)
CarinaCartilaginous plate at the tracheal bifurcation
Principal bronchiTrachea → right + left principal bronchi → lobar bronchi
Tracheobronchial lymph nodesAt the tracheal bifurcation · drain lungs + bronchi

🩸 Great veins + lymph

Cranial vena cavaFormed by the two brachiocephalic veins → right atrium
Caudal vena cavaFrom the caudal body → right atrium (in the plica venae cavae)
Brachiocephalic veinExternal jugular vein + subclavian vein
Subclavian veinBecomes the axillary vein past the first rib
Right azygos veinDrains the dorsal thoracic wall · arches over · LAST vein into the cranial vena cava
Thoracic ductBrown, beaded · LEFT side, ventral to the 5th thoracic vertebra → left brachiocephalic vein
Cisterna chyliCollects abdominal lymph → thoracic duct
Tracheal trunksHead + neck lymph → thoracic duct (left) / venous angle (right)
