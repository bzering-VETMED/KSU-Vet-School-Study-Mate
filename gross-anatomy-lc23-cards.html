⚠️ Precision traps

  • Parietal vaginal tunic is in the CLINICAL cord only; visceral vaginal tunic (mesorchium, mesoductus deferens) is in the ANATOMICAL cord.
  • Clinical cord = anatomical 7 + cremaster muscle + parietal vaginal tunic (9 total).
  • Proper ligament of the testis = testis ↔ tail of epididymis; ligament of the tail of the epididymis = tail ↔ vaginal tunic; scrotal ligament = that ligament ↔ tunica dartos. Full names on the exam.
  • Open castration incises the PARIETAL vaginal tunic only (opens vaginal cavity → peritoneal cavity); closed opens nothing. Visceral layer is never cut on purpose.
  • Dog: scrotum between thighs, penis cranially directed. Cat: scrotum just ventral to anus (dorsal), penis short, ventral and caudally directed.
  • Epididymis is dorsoLATERAL (seen only in lateral view); ductus deferens is dorsoMEDIAL — seeing only the ductus = medial view.
  • Tunica dartos (smooth muscle, just under skin) vs tunica albuginea (connective tissue, innermost) — both whitish.
  • Vaginal ring (parietal peritoneum reflection, intra-abdominal) ≠ deep inguinal ring ≠ superficial inguinal ring (slit in external abdominal oblique aponeurosis).
  • Mesorchium (testis + testicular vessels) vs mesoductus deferens (ductus deferens) — both visceral vaginal tunic.
  • Round ligament of bladder = umbilical ARTERIES (in LATERAL ligament, not median); round ligament of liver = umbilical VEIN.
  • Testes in scrotum by 6 weeks; diagnose cryptorchid only after 7 months. Abdominal vs inguinal retained.
  • Bulb of the penis (root, corpus spongiosum, bulbospongiosus) ≠ bulbus glandis (glans, the 'tie'). Crus = corpus cavernosum + ischiocavernosus.
  • Os penis = ossified corpus CAVERNOSUM, but both parts of the glans are corpus SPONGIOSUM.
  • Prostate: DORSAL surface has peritoneum. Kidney: VENTRAL surface has peritoneum. Flipped pair.
  • Pampiniform plexus = testicular VEIN, not artery.
  • Left testicular vein → left renal vein; right testicular vein → caudal vena cava.
  • Internal pudendal → (ventral perineal) → artery of the penis → dorsal, deep (cavernosum), bulb (spongiosum).
  • Preputial orifice ≠ external urethral orifice. Preputial fornix ≈ vaginal fornix (same reflection concept).
  • Prostate — no R.

⬇️ Testicular descent & cryptorchidism

Fetal location of the testes before descent?Intra-abdominal, caudal to the kidneys
Main structure driving testicular descent?Gubernaculum (gubernaculum testis)
How does the gubernaculum pull the testis down?Gubernacular bulb in the scrotum shrinks, pulling the attached testis toward it
Descent pathway from abdomen to scrotum?Abdominal cavity → deep inguinal ring → inguinal canal → superficial inguinal ring → scrotum
Inguinal canal: what is it?Flattened potential space between the deep and superficial inguinal rings
Cryptorchid: meaning of the word?Crypto = hidden; orchid = testis
Prefix orchi- refers to?Testis (orchitis = inflammation of the testis)
Most puppies have testes in the scrotum by?About 6 weeks of age
Diagnose cryptorchidism if descent not complete by?7 months
Two locations a retained testis can be?Abdominal or inguinal
Testis exited the superficial inguinal ring but not in scrotum?Inguinal testicle (inguinal cryptorchid)
Unilateral vs bilateral cryptorchid?Unilateral = one testis retained; bilateral = both

👝 Scrotum

Location of the dog scrotum?Inguinal region, between the thighs
What divides the scrotum into two compartments?Median septum (scrotal septum)
Contents of each scrotal compartment?One testis, its epididymis, distal part of the spermatic cord
Scrotal raphe?External midline mark of the internal scrotal septum (like linea alba)
Which testis is usually farther caudal?Left testis
Two main scrotal layers?Scrotal skin and tunica dartos
Scrotal skin features?Thin, pigmented, few hairs
Tunica dartos: tissue type?Smooth muscle layer immediately deep to scrotal skin
★ Told class to find it in lab; explained at length
Why is tunica dartos whitish?Smooth muscle with little myoglobin
Function of tunica dartos contraction?Retracts scrotal skin, draws testes close to the body (e.g., cold)
Voluntary control of tunica dartos?No — smooth muscle, involuntary

Cat penis: direction?Short, caudally directed (dog: cranially directed)

🥚 Testes & vaginal tunic

Testis shape and position in scrotum?Nearly spherical; oblique, long axis running dorsocaudally
Coverings enclosing the testis?Vaginal tunic, spermatic and cremasteric fasciae, connected to tunica dartos
Other name for tunica vaginalis?Vaginal tunic
Two layers of the vaginal tunic?Parietal layer and visceral layer
Vaginal tunic is derived from?Peritoneum (serous membrane) evaginated through the inguinal canal

Female equivalent: blind peritoneal pouch called?Vaginal process
Where does the epididymis attach on the testis?Dorsolateral surface; head cranial, tail caudal
Epididymis visible from which view of the testis?Lateral view only
How do you know a testis image is the medial view?You see the ductus deferens, not the epididymis

🧅 Layers of the testis (outer → inner)

Layers of the testis, outer to inner?Scrotal skin, tunica dartos, scrotal septum, external spermatic fascia, parietal vaginal tunic, visceral vaginal tunic, tunica albuginea
★ 'Important ones' you identified in lab
External spermatic fascia: tissue type?Connective tissue fascia, deep to dartos
Layer deep to external spermatic fascia?Parietal layer of the vaginal tunic
Layer directly on the testis surface before albuginea?Visceral layer of the vaginal tunic
Innermost layer of the testis?Tunica albuginea
Tunica albuginea: tissue and meaning?White connective tissue capsule of testis proper (albus = white)

Proper ligament of the testis attaches?Testis to the tail of the epididymis

Ligament of the tail of the epididymis attaches?Tail of epididymis to the (parietal) vaginal tunic and spermatic fascia
★ 'You have to say that full name'
Scrotal ligament attaches?Ligament of the tail of the epididymis to tunica dartos (scrotum)

These ligaments are remnants of?Embryonic gubernaculum testis
Same concept as pulmonary and falciform ligaments?Yes — serous membrane folds named by what they attach

🌀 Epididymis, mesorchium & sperm flow

Main function of the epididymis?Storage of spermatozoa before ejaculation
Three parts of the epididymis?Head (caput), body (corpus), tail (cauda)
Head of epididymis location?Cranial; begins craniomedially, twists toward lateral side
Body of epididymis location?Dorsolateral surface of the testis
Tail of epididymis attached by?Proper ligament of the testis, to caudal extremity of testis
Tail of the epididymis continues as?Ductus deferens (craniodorsally)
Mesorchium?Visceral vaginal tunic suspending testis and testicular vessels and nerves
★ Quizzed: 'What layer specifically? Visceral'
Mesoductus deferens?Visceral vaginal tunic fold off the mesorchium, suspending ductus deferens
★ 'That would be the full name'
Testicular bursa?Potential space between epididymis body and testis
★ Told to identify it in lab
Testicular bursa limits?Cranially epididymal head, caudally epididymal tail
Testis is composed of?Seminiferous tubules
Spermatozoa path up to the ductus deferens?Seminiferous tubules → straight tubules → rete testis → efferent ductules → epididymis head → body → tail → ductus deferens

🚰 Ductus deferens

Ductus deferens runs along which testis border?Dorsomedial border, passing cranially
Initial shape of the ductus deferens?Coiled at first, then straightens
Where does the ductus deferens enter the abdominal cavity?Vaginal ring
Vaginal ring?Reflection of parietal peritoneum at deep inguinal ring into parietal vaginal tunic
★ Student asked; re-explained with trash can analogy
Course of ductus deferens after entering abdomen?To dorsal bladder, crosses over the ureters, joins the urethra
Serous fold the right and left ducts enter?Genital fold
Female counterpart of the genital fold region?Mesometrium (uterine tubes and horns instead of ductus)

🪢 Spermatic cord & castration

Where does the spermatic cord begin?Vaginal ring (intra-abdominal)
Spermatic cord course?Vaginal ring → inguinal canal → superficial inguinal ring → testis
Superficial inguinal ring is?Slit in the aponeurosis of the external abdominal oblique
★ Quizzed aloud on cadaver image
Anatomical spermatic cord: 7 components?Ductus deferens and vessels, mesoductus deferens, testicular artery, testicular veins (pampiniform plexus), lymphatics, testicular nerve plexus, mesorchium

Clinical (surgical) cord adds which two?Cremaster muscle and parietal vaginal tunic
★ Anatomical vs clinical difference 'is important'
Which cord matters in castration?Clinical (surgical) spermatic cord
Cremaster muscle attaches to?Parietal vaginal tunic
Open castration incises which layer?Parietal vaginal tunic only
★ Quizzed class on figure A vs B
Closed castration?Parietal vaginal tunic left intact — nothing opened
★ Quizzed class on figure A vs B
Layer exposed (shiny) in open castration?Visceral vaginal tunic — never incised intentionally
Space between parietal and visceral vaginal tunic?Vaginal cavity
★ Quizzed aloud (compared to pleural cavity)
Vaginal cavity is continuous with?Peritoneal cavity — open castration creates a conduit to it
Trash can analogy: the can and the bag?Can = inguinal canal; bag lining = parietal peritoneum becoming parietal vaginal tunic

🍆 Penis: divisions & erectile tissue

Three divisions of the penis?Root, body, glans
Parts of the root?Left and right crura + bulb of the penis
★ Root = 2 crura + bulb — he drew it twice
Crus of the penis arises from?Ischiatic tuberosity (left crus from left, right from right)
Erectile tissue of the crus?Corpus cavernosum
★ "That one you need to remember" — crus = corpus cavernosum
Muscle surrounding each crus?Ischiocavernosus muscle
★ Name logic: ischium + corpus cavernosum
Bulb of the penis location and shape?Between the crura, lining the ischial arch; partially bilobed (split by the retractor penis)
Erectile tissue of the bulb?Corpus spongiosum
★ Two erectile tissues: cavernosum (crura) vs spongiosum (bulb)
Corpus cavernosum vs corpus spongiosum?Cavernosum = more blood-filled spaces; spongiosum = more connective tissue
Muscle covering the bulb?Bulbospongiosus muscle
★ Name logic: bulb + corpus spongiosum
What runs through the bulb?Urethra: pelvic urethra continues as the penile urethra
The urethra is ALWAYS surrounded by?Corpus spongiosum
★ "Always, always" — urethra sits in corpus spongiosum
Where does the body of the penis begin?Where the two crura and the bulb come together, distal to the root
Body contents?Two corpora cavernosa, corpus spongiosum, penile urethra
Capsule enveloping the body?Tunica albuginea (same name as the testis capsule)
Free part of the penis, covered by prepuce?Glans of the penis
Two parts of the glans?Bulbus glandis and pars longa glandis
★ Glans = bulbus (round) + pars longa (elongated)
Is the glans a gland?No — 'glans' is a structure, not glandular tissue
Bulbus glandis?Barrel-shaped expansion around the proximal os penis
Function of bulbus glandis?Responsible for the 'tie' or 'lock' during copulation
★ Bulbus glandis = the tie
Bulbus glandis separated from pars longa glandis by?Connective tissue septum (no connection between them)
Erectile tissue of BOTH parts of the glans?Corpus spongiosum
★ Glans = spongiosum, even though it's distal
Os penis is?Ossified corpus cavernosum in the glans
★ Corpus cavernosum → bone = os penis
Parts of the os penis?Apex (cranial), body, base
Urethral groove?Ventral groove of the os penis where the penile urethra runs
★ Cross-section D: bone + urethral groove + urethra in spongiosum
Pars longa glandis drainage (slide)?Short large vein each side into bulbus glandis

🧥 Prepuce & penis muscles

Prepuce?Skin fold covering the glans (free part of the penis)
External lamina of prepuce?Outer haired skin, continuous with the ventral abdominal wall skin
Internal lamina of prepuce?Contacts the glans; ends at the preputial fornix
Preputial fornix?Where the internal lamina reflects onto the glans — the deepest a probe can go
★ Compared again in L24 to the vaginal fornix
Preputial orifice?Where the external and internal laminae meet
Preputial orifice vs external urethral orifice?Preputial = skin opening of prepuce; external urethral = urethra opening at the tip of the glans
★ He quizzed the class on this opening
3 penis muscles you must find in lab?Ischiocavernosus, bulbospongiosus, retractor penis
★ Lab IDs — these 3 only
Ischiocavernosus muscle?From ischiatic tuberosity; encloses the crura
Bulbospongiosus muscle?Surrounds the bulb of the penis
Retractor penis muscle?Midline, superficial on the bulb, runs to the body; more important in large animals
Ischiourethralis muscle?Small; protects the dorsal vein of the penis — NOT found in lab

🌰 Accessory sex glands

Accessory sex glands of the dog?Ampullary gland and prostate (only two)
★ Dog = only 2 accessory sex glands
Ampullary gland?Glandular thickening (dilation) of the ductus deferens wall as it reaches the dorsal bladder/urethra
Prostate location?Completely surrounds the urethra at the neck of the bladder
★ Spelling: prostate (no R)
Prostate peritoneal relations?Dorsal surface covered by peritoneum; ventral surface retroperitoneal
★ Opposite of the kidney (kidney: ventral covered, dorsal free)
Prostate dorsal and ventral boundaries?Dorsal: rectum; ventral: pubic symphysis
What divides prostate into lobes?Median septum → right and left lobes
Where do ductus deferens enter the prostate?Craniodorsal surface
Colliculus seminalis?Ridge/hillock in the prostatic urethra; continuation of the urethral crest
★ Where ductus deferens AND prostate empty
What opens at the colliculus seminalis?Ductus deferens (one slit each side) and the prostatic ducts
Urethral crest continues as?Colliculus seminalis (crest + ureteric orifices form the trigone)
Bladder opened to see the trigone: which surface was cut?Ventral — you're looking at the dorsal roof (ureters enter dorsally)

🩸 Blood supply & lymph

Testicular artery is a branch of?Abdominal aorta (direct branch, left and right)
★ Testes = testicular artery off the aorta
Second artery to the testis/ductus?Artery of the ductus deferens (from the internal iliac level)
Third blood source (penis)?Internal pudendal artery
★ 3 sources: testicular a., artery of ductus deferens, internal pudendal a.
Pampiniform plexus is formed by?Testicular VEIN (around the artery)
★ "It is the vein that forms the plexus"
Function of pampiniform plexus?Thermoregulation — cool venous blood cools warm arterial blood going to the testis (radiator coil)
Last branch of internal pudendal before it becomes artery of the penis?Ventral perineal artery
Artery of the penis splits into?Dorsal artery of the penis, deep artery of the penis, artery of the bulb of the penis
★ 3 branches — know their locations
Deep artery of the penis supplies?Corpus cavernosum
Artery of the bulb of the penis supplies?Corpus spongiosum (begins at the bulb)
Dorsal artery of the penis location?Dorsal surface, outside the erectile tissue
Left testicular vein drains into?Left renal vein
★ Left ≠ right — left kidney is more caudal
Right testicular vein drains into?Caudal vena cava directly
Internal male structures drain to which lymph node?Medial iliac lymph nodes (testis, epididymis, prostate)
★ Internal → medial iliac; ignore lumbar aortic/internal iliac
External male structures drain to which lymph node?Superficial inguinal lymph nodes (scrotum, prepuce, penis)
★ External → superficial inguinal
