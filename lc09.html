Force resisted by each?

MT compression · MF tension/stretching · IF shearing. MT pushes, MF pulls, IF holds.

Subunit, nucleotide, motor?

MT α+β tubulin/GTP/kinesin+dynein; MF G-actin/ATP/myosin; IF no single protein/no nucleotide/no motors.

Card 6

Where are MTs?

Tap to reveal

Card 7

Kinesin vs dynein?

Tap to reveal
Kinesin −→+ outward/exocytosis. Dynein +→− inward/after phagocytosis.
Card 8

Why nucleation?

Tap to reveal
Minus end falls apart faster than plus grows; stabilize minus end so plus can build.
Card 9

Two MTOCs?

Tap to reveal
Centrosomes and basal bodies; γ-tubulin templates and binds minus end; − in, + out.
Card 10

Dynamic instability?

Tap to reveal
GTP dimers add; hydrolysis destabilizes; GTP cap = growth; cap loss = catastrophe; subunits leave at ends.
Card 11
