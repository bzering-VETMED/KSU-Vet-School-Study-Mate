Lab 26 veins (bold terms)

  • Medial saphenous vein: cranial branch + caudal branch; medial limb, drains to the femoral vein
  • Lateral saphenous vein: cranial branch + caudal branch; crosses the lateral hock (catheter site)

How to memorize the femoral branches

  • 🗣️ In order: "Super Lateral Proximal Saphenous Descends, Middle Distal Pops."
  • ⚠️ Trap: the deep femoral is the external iliac's only branch, and it leaves inside the abdomen, before the vascular lacuna.
  • Lab 26 guide p. 197: the caudal gluteal (the larger terminal branch of the internal iliac) gives off the iliolumbar and cranial gluteal. The iliolumbar sometimes comes straight off the internal iliac instead.
