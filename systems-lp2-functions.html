⚠️ Precision traps

  • Purkinje CELL (cerebellum neuron) ≠ Purkinje FIBER (heart conduction)
  • Zona GLOMERULOSA (adrenal) ≠ GLOMERULUS (kidney)
  • Chief cells: PARATHYROID (parathyroid hormone) ≠ stomach (pepsinogen)
  • Calcitonin (parafollicular) LOWERS calcium · parathyroid hormone RAISES it
  • Pars nervosa only STORES/RELEASES oxytocin + ADH · the hypothalamus MAKES them
  • Nucleus = cell bodies in CNS · ganglion = cell bodies in PNS · tract (CNS) vs nerve (PNS)
  • Ventral median FISSURE (atlas p.107) vs ventral median SULCUS (objectives) · write what the question uses; ask instructor if unsure
  • Dentate gyrus: atlas p.110 labels its cell layer "pyramidal layer" (textbooks often say granule cell layer)
  • Cortex / medulla / capsule exist in kidney, adrenal AND lymph node · name the ORGAN too
  • Medulla (kidney / adrenal) ≠ medulla oblongata (brainstem)
  • Bowman's capsule ≠ renal capsule
  • Type I pneumocyte = barrier · type II = surfactant
  • Respiratory epithelium (pseudostratified) ≠ transitional epithelium (urothelium)
  • Mucosa ≠ submucosa · glands sit in the SUBmucosa
  • Bronchiole has club cells, NOT goblet-rich epithelium
