Renal 1

Q1A 30 kg dog. About how much PLASMA volume does it have?

✅ B · Water 60% = 18 L · extracellular ⅓ = 6 L · plasma ≈ ¼ of extracellular = 1.5 L

Q2Which pairing of normal concentrations (mM) is correct?

✅ C · Table 12.1: Na⁺ 145 outside / 10 inside · K⁺ 5 outside / 140 inside

Q3A cow receives hypertonic saline. Once water finishes moving, which is NOT true?

✅ D · Salt stays outside → water leaves cells → cells shrink, and both compartments end at a higher osmolarity

Q4Inflammation lets protein leak into a tissue's interstitial fluid. What happens at that capillary?

✅ A · Protein in the tissue pulls water toward the tissue → edema
Renal 2

Q5Which is NOT part of the glomerular filtration barrier?

✅ B · Mesangial cells are support only

Q6Two drugs are both 30 kDa. One is positively charged, the other negatively charged. Which statement is correct?

✅ C · The basement membrane is negative → repels anions, lets cations through more easily (size > charge > shape)

Q7Which change would most DECREASE GFR?

✅ D · Efferent dilation drops glomerular pressure · the other three raise GFR

Q8GFR is 100 mL/min and renal plasma flow is 500 mL/min. What is the filtration fraction?

✅ A · Filtration fraction = GFR ÷ renal plasma flow = 100/500 = 20%

Q9Basel says the kidney prefers HIGH renal plasma flow. Why?

✅ B · Filtration fraction falls, but far more plasma passes per minute → more total filtrate
Renal 3

Q10LOW NaCl at the macula densa causes which? (select ALL that apply)

✅ C, D, E · Low NaCl = GFR too low → open the entrance + start RAAS (angiotensin II then constricts the efferent)

Q11Which is NOT a trigger for renin release?

✅ C · Atrial stretch triggers ANP, which suppresses renin

Q12A dog with heart failure has high renin. What is the main reason?

✅ D · Basel: a failing heart puts the body in fight-or-flight → sympathetic nerves → renin

Q13About 80% of the kidney's oxygen consumption is used for:

✅ A · Mostly in the proximal tubule and thick ascending limb

Q14An emaciated 15-year-old cat needs screening for early CKD. Which marker is most useful?

✅ B · SDMA rises after ~25% loss and isn't affected by muscle mass

Q15Which are hallmarks of glomerular disease? (select ALL that apply)

✅ A, B, C, D · Glucosuria with normal blood glucose = proximal tubule (Fanconi)
Renal 4

Q16How does glucose leave the proximal tubule cell to reach the blood?

✅ C · SGLT brings it in (apical) · GLUT lets it out (basolateral, passive)

Q17Which proximal tubule transporters are SECONDARY active? (select ALL that apply)

✅ A, B, D, E · The Na⁺/K⁺ pump is PRIMARY active (uses ATP directly)

Q18Why does the proximal tubule filtrate stay at ~300 even though so much is reabsorbed?

✅ D · Isosmotic reabsorption: solute out, water follows → smaller volume, same concentration

Q19A dog drank antifreeze. Which ethylene glycol metabolite is secreted into the tubule and forms crystals?

✅ A · Ethylene glycol → oxalate → secreted (OAT pathway) → crystals + tubule damage

Q20Which statement about megalin/cubam is INCORRECT?

✅ B · Tetracycline enters through OAT from the BLOOD side
Renal 5

Q21The thin descending limb is permeable to ___ but not ___.

✅ C · Water out only → filtrate concentrates as it dives

Q22Which pairing is correct for THIAZIDES?

✅ D · Loop = wastes both · thiazide = wastes K⁺, spares Ca²⁺

Q23Aldosterone acts on principal cells. What happens to potassium?

✅ A · ENaC brings Na⁺ in → lumen negative → K⁺ out through ROMK

Q24Why are the thick ascending limb and distal tubule waterproof?

✅ B · Basel: 'if water can move, it will' → block water, make the filtrate hypotonic, then let ADH decide

Q25Which statement about NKCC is INCORRECT?

✅ C · Thiazides block NCC · loop diuretics block NKCC
Renal 6

Q26Which segment SECRETES urea back into the filtrate?

✅ D · Thin loop secretes ~60% of the filtered load (recycling), via UT-A2

Q27Which best describes the vasa recta?

✅ A · Exchange = keeps the gradient; multiplication (loop + NKCC) builds it

Q28During antidiuresis, about how much of the ~1200 mOsm medulla is UREA?

✅ B · ~600 NaCl + ~600 urea

Q29If NKCC is completely blocked, what happens to the medullary gradient?

✅ C · Everything depends on NKCC
Renal 7

Q30Filtrate entering the collecting duct is ~100 mOsm. With NO ADH, the final urine is about:

✅ D · No apical aquaporins → water stays in → dilute urine

Q31Where is ADH made, and where is it released?

✅ A

Q32ADH acts on which receptor in the collecting duct?

✅ B · V1 = blood vessels (constriction) · V2 = kidney (water)

Q33A dog's dilute urine concentrates after desmopressin but not after water deprivation. Where is the problem?

✅ C · Central DI: the kidney responds, the brain doesn't make ADH

Q34Why is furosemide called a 'double whammy' diuretic?

✅ D · Narrows the filtrate–medulla difference from both sides

Q35Which is a downside of giving large volumes of 0.9% NaCl?

✅ A · Extra Cl⁻ displaces bicarbonate

Q36Which is NOT one of Basel's goals of fluid therapy?

✅ B · The 4th goal is to flush and dilute toxins
Renal 8

Q37Which are PTH effects in the kidney? (select ALL that apply)

✅ B, C, D, E · Blocking claudin-16 is CALCITONIN

Q38What happens if claudin-14 activity INCREASES in the thick ascending limb?

✅ C · Claudin-14 blocks claudin-16 (the calcium door)

Q39A dog is HYPERcalcemic and hypertensive. Which diuretic should you AVOID?

✅ D · Thiazides spare calcium (more negative distal cell pulls Ca²⁺ in)

Q40Why can too LITTLE phosphate excretion cause acidosis?

✅ A · Phosphate is the main titratable buffer in urine
Renal 9

Q41Which is the FASTEST way the body handles a sudden potassium load?

✅ B · Insulin works in minutes · the kidney takes hours

Q42Which statement about BK channels is INCORRECT?

✅ C · Aldosterone mainly regulates ROMK · BK = flow-activated safety valve

Q43Which acidosis has a NORMAL anion gap?

✅ D · Diarrhea loses bicarbonate · nothing new is added

Q44Why can't the kidney excrete a day's acid as free H⁺?

✅ A · Buffers (phosphate, ammonia) must grab the H⁺

Q45Which are features of TYPE 2 (proximal) renal tubular acidosis? (select ALL that apply)

✅ B, C, D, E · High K⁺ = type 4
Renal 10

Q46Phenylpropanolamine treats urinary incontinence by:

✅ B · Tightens the internal sphincter

Q47A dog with a lumbosacral fracture has a large, flaccid bladder that overflows. Best drug?

✅ C · Lower motor neuron = Limp → stimulate M3 (Bethanechol Bears down)

Q48During storage, why does the detrusor relax?

✅ D · β = Balloon relaxes · α₁ = sphincter shut
Cases

Q49Why does the gentamicin-treated Lab become hyperkalemic? (select ALL that apply)

✅ B, C · Basel: acidosis + low flow

Q50A Boston terrier was sick, improved, then got sick again a day later, with a high anion gap and azotemia. Why the second illness?

✅ A · Day 1 = toxin + acidosis · day 2 = kidney damage shows up

Q51An acidotic Arabian horse has K⁺ at the bottom of normal and urine pH 8. What explains the potassium?

✅ B · Type 1 RTA is the exception: acidosis with LOW K⁺

Q52A dog on tetracycline has glucose, amino acids, phosphate and bicarbonate in its urine, normal GFR, and granular casts. Most likely?

✅ C · Proximal tubule death → everything leaks

Q53A blocked cat has bilateral pyelectasis on ultrasound. What causes it?

✅ D · Postrenal obstruction · the same back pressure raises Bowman's space pressure and drops GFR

Q54A dog has BUN 58, creatinine 1.3, USG 1.050 after 3 days of not drinking. Best interpretation?

✅ A · Concentrated urine = the kidney works; BUN rises with dehydration
