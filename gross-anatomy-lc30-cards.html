⚠️ Precision traps

  • 📄 Built from the SLIDES only (lecture Wed 11/4) — stars and corrections update when the transcript arrives.
  • Palpebral conjunctiva (lines INNER surface of eyelids) vs bulbar conjunctiva (covers SCLERA) — the fornix is the angle where they meet; conjunctival sac = potential space when the eye is closed (slides 15–16).
  • Lacrimal GLAND (~70% of tears, dorsolateral) vs gland of the THIRD EYELID (~30%, medial, surrounds the cartilage stem) — and don't confuse the lacrimal gland with the lacrimal SAC (a drainage step, slides 20–21).
  • Tear drainage order: puncta → canaliculi → lacrimal SAC → nasolacrimal DUCT → nasal vestibule. Sac comes BEFORE duct (slide 20).
  • Choroid vs tapetum lucidum: tapetum is the reflective area WITHIN the choroid, in the DORSAL fundus — not a separate tunic (slides 27, 29).
  • Cornea vs sclera vs limbus: cornea = transparent, AVASCULAR, anterior; sclera = opaque white, posterior; limbus = the corneoscleral JUNCTION (slide 23).
  • Posterior chamber = between iris/pupil and LENS; anterior chamber = between CORNEA and iris/pupil. Both hold aqueous humor; vitreous chamber is behind the lens (slide 34). 'Posterior chamber' is NOT the vitreous chamber.
  • Aqueous flow: ciliary body → POSTERIOR chamber → pupil → ANTERIOR chamber → iridocorneal angle → scleral venous plexus (slide 36). Produced in the posterior chamber first.
  • Ciliary muscle CONTRACTION RELAXES zonular fiber tension → lens FATTENS (accommodation) — the inverted relationship (slide 38).
  • Constrictor pupillae (CIRCULAR, parasympathetic via oculomotor nerve, miosis) vs dilator pupillae (RADIAL, sympathetic from cranial cervical ganglion, mydriasis) (slide 38).
  • Dorsal oblique vs ventral oblique: only the DORSAL oblique is cranial nerve IV (trochlear); the ventral oblique is cranial nerve III (oculomotor) (slide 42).
  • LR6 Do4 Rest3 — but retractor bulbi is colored RED on slide 42 (same color as lateral rectus = cranial nerve VI, abducent). It is an exception to 'Rest3' (outside knowledge — confirm: retractor bulbi = abducent nerve).
  • Retractor bulbi (muscle of the GLOBE) vs retractor anguli oculi lateralis (muscle of the EYELIDS) vs levator anguli oculi medialis vs levator palpebrae superioris — look-alike names (slide 39).
  • ⚠️ FLAG (slide 23): 'Insertion of extrinsic ocular muscles' sits directly under 'Limbus – corneoscleral junction' with no bullet of its own. Read literally it says the muscles insert at the limbus; standard anatomy has the extrinsic muscles inserting on the SCLERA (outside knowledge — confirm in lecture which the professor means).
  • ⚠️ FLAG (slides 4–5): slide 4 lists 'Meridional and sagittal planes' but slide 5 defines only the meridional plane; sagittal is never defined on the slides.
