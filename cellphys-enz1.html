Name the key enzymes: Glucagon → glucose out of the liver7 enzymes · ★ must know · 💭 understanding only
⬇️ Enzyme 1?
★ Protein kinase A — activated by cyclic AMP; adds phosphates
⬇️ Enzyme 2?
💭 Adenylyl cyclase — converts ATP into cyclic AMP
⬇️ Enzyme 3?
💭 Phosphorylase kinase — middle-man; phosphorylates glycogen phosphorylase
⬇️ Enzyme 4?
★ Glycogen phosphorylase — snips glucose-1-phosphate off glycogen; ON when phosphorylated
⬇️ Enzyme 5?
★ Glycogen debranching enzyme — clears branch points so phosphorylase keeps cutting
⬇️ Enzyme 6?
💭 Phosphoglucomutase — moves the phosphate from carbon 1 to carbon 6
⬇️ Enzyme 7?
★ Glucose-6-phosphatase — liver only; frees glucose so it can leave

Name the key enzymes: Glucagon → gluconeogenesis switch7 enzymes · ★ must know · 💭 understanding only
⬇️ Enzyme 1?
★ Protein kinase A — phosphorylates the two-job enzyme
⬇️ Enzyme 2?
★ Phosphofructokinase-2 / fructose-2,6-bisphosphatase — one enzyme, two halves; phosphorylated → bisphosphatase half on
⬇️ Enzyme 3?
★ Phosphofructokinase-1 — glycolysis control step; loses its activator → slows
⬇️ Enzyme 4?
★ Fructose-1,6-bisphosphatase — gluconeogenesis control step; loses its inhibitor → speeds up

★ Pyruvate carboxylase — bypass: pyruvate → oxaloacetate
⬇️ Enzyme 6?
★ Phosphoenolpyruvate carboxykinase — bypass: oxaloacetate → phosphoenolpyruvate
⬇️ Enzyme 7?
★ Glucose-6-phosphatase — last bypass; liver only; releases free glucose

Name the key enzymes: Glucose trapping → glycolysis4 enzymes · ★ must know · 💭 understanding only
⬇️ Enzyme 1?
★ Hexokinase — low Km; most tissues; inhibited by glucose-6-phosphate
⬇️ Enzyme 2?
★ Glucokinase — high Km (~15 mM); liver + beta cells; not inhibited
⬇️ Enzyme 3?
★ Phosphofructokinase-1 — main regulatory, rate-limiting glycolysis step
⬇️ Enzyme 4?
★ Pyruvate kinase — last step; makes ATP; feed-forward activated

Name the key enzymes: Fat burning (lipolysis → beta-oxidation)8 enzymes · ★ must know · 💭 understanding only
⬇️ Enzyme 1?
★ Protein kinase A — turned on by epinephrine or glucagon; phosphorylates
⬇️ Enzyme 2?
★ Hormone-sensitive lipase — cuts fatty acids off glycerol; ON when phosphorylated
⬇️ Enzyme 3?
💭 Fatty acyl-CoA synthetase — activates fatty acid in cytosol; costs 2 ATP
⬇️ Enzyme 4?
★ Carnitine shuttle — carries fatty acyl-CoA into the mitochondria
⬇️ Enzyme 5?
💭 Acyl-CoA dehydrogenase — beta-oxidation step 1; makes FADH₂
⬇️ Enzyme 6?
💭 Enoyl-CoA hydratase — beta-oxidation step 2; adds water
⬇️ Enzyme 7?
💭 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase — beta-oxidation step 3; makes NADH
⬇️ Enzyme 8?
💭 Thiolase — beta-oxidation step 4; cuts off acetyl-CoA

💭 Glycerol-3-phosphate dehydrogenase — dihydroxyacetone phosphate → glycerol-3-phosphate (triglyceride backbone)
