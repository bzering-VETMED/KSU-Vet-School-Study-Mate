Ulnar nerve
In previous labs, you should have identified the ulnar nerve further proximally. Now we are following the ulnar nerve as it travels distally. The ulnar nerve in the antebrachium gives rise to two branches, a dorsal and palmar branch. The palmar branch is typically much larger than the dorsal branch.

Autonomous zone of the axillary nerve
Cutaneous area = ALL the skin a nerve supplies (overlaps neighbors). Autonomous zone = the part supplied ONLY by that nerve, so testing it checks that one nerve
⚠️ Pair
cutaneous area (big, overlapping) vs autonomous zone (small, exclusive)
