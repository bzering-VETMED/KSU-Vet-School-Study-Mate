The external thoracic artery leaves the axillary near its origin. It then curves around the craniomedial border of the deep pectoral with the nerve and heads to the superficial pectoral muscles. Cranial pectoral nerves
The cranial pectoral nerves are derived from ventral branches of spinal nerves 6,7 and 8. They innervate the superficial pectoral muscles. There will be thick layers of fascia in these region, but these nerves are found cranial to the larger clumping of the main brachial plexus which can be seen in the image. These nerves do not need to be dissected out but are still fair for examination questions.
