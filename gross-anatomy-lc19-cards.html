❤️ Arteries

Main artery chain of the thoracic limb?Subclavian → axillary → brachial → median
Left subclavian artery arises from…Aortic arch
Right subclavian artery arises from…Brachiocephalic trunk
Subclavian becomes axillary at the…First rib
Axillary becomes brachial after the…Cranial circumflex humeral artery
Brachial becomes median after the…Common interosseous artery
Last two branches of the subclavian?Internal thoracic, superficial cervical
External thoracic artery supplies…Superficial pectorals
Lateral thoracic artery supplies…Deep pectorals
Thoracodorsal artery supplies…Latissimus dorsi
Caudal circumflex humeral artery comes off the…Subscapular artery
Cranial circumflex humeral artery comes off the…Axillary artery
Median artery continues as the…Superficial palmar arch
Superficial paw arteries?Common digital arteries
Deep paw arteries?Metacarpal arteries

🩸 Veins

Cephalic vein drains which side?Medial
Accessory cephalic vein drains which side?Dorsal and lateral
Cephalic vein empties into the…External jugular vein
Superficial–deep link at the elbow?Median cubital vein (cephalic ↔ brachial)
Deep veins are tributaries of the…Axillary vein

🫧 Lymph nodes

Terminal lymph node of the thoracic limb?None
Terminal lymph node of the head?Medial retropharyngeal lymph node
Clinical name for the superficial cervical lymph node?Prescapular
Is the axillary lymph node always present?Yes (constant)
Accessory axillary lymph node location?Intercostal spaces 2–3
Mammary glands drained by the axillary lymph node?1–3
Sentinel lymph node?First node to drain an area
Terminal lymph node?Node all or most lymph from a region passes through
Afferent lymph vessels carry lymph…Into the node

⚡ Nerves

Brachial plexus is formed by…Ventral branches of C6–T2
Suprascapular nerve supplies…Supraspinatus and infraspinatus
Musculocutaneous nerve supplies…Biceps brachii, brachialis, coracobrachialis
Axillary nerve supplies…Deltoideus, teres major, teres minor
Thoracodorsal nerve supplies…Latissimus dorsi
Radial nerve supplies…Triceps and carpal/digital extensors
Median nerve supplies…Flexor carpi radialis, superficial and deep digital flexors
Ulnar nerve supplies…Flexor carpi ulnaris, deep digital flexor
Lateral thoracic nerve supplies…Cutaneous trunci
Largest brachial plexus nerve?Radial nerve
Nerve with a cutaneous branch but no autonomous zone?Median nerve
Fiber types in every plexus nerve?Motor, sensory, sympathetic
