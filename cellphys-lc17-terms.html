🔥 LC17 · TCA cycle, redox, electron transport

TCA cycleTricarboxylic acid cycle = citric acid cycle = Krebs cycle; inside the mitochondria.
TCA orderCitrate → isocitrate → α-ketoglutarate → succinyl-CoA → succinate → fumarate → malate → oxaloacetate.
Acetyl-CoAPyruvate minus a CO₂; enters the cycle with oxaloacetate to make citrate. "Remember forever."
OxaloacetateNeeded for step 1; made by step 8. Also used by gluconeogenesis.
TCA yield per glucose+2 GTP, +8 NADH, +2 FADH₂ (~25 ATP equivalents).
Why the TCA cycle needs O₂No step uses O₂, but O₂ (via the ETS) regenerates NAD⁺ and FAD.
Step 8 (malate → oxaloacetate)Large positive ΔG + makes NADH → without O₂ it runs backward → oxaloacetate runs out.
IschemiaToo little blood flow → no O₂ → succinate builds up from BOTH directions.
Reperfusion injuryO₂ returns → stored succinate → fumarate fast → FADH₂ surge → ETS → oxidative damage.
OxidationLosing electrons (more positive charge, gaining a bond to oxygen, losing H⁺ + e⁻).
Reduction"Reduce the positive charge": gaining electrons (gaining H⁺ + e⁻, losing a bond to oxygen).
Reducer vs oxidizerReducer gives electrons and is oxidized; oxidizer takes electrons and is reduced.
Coupled redoxEvery oxidation is paired with a reduction; NADH → NAD⁺ is coupled to O₂ → water.
Electron transport system locationInner mitochondrial membrane (impervious to H⁺).
Complex ITakes NADH electrons → NAD⁺; pumps protons.
Complex IISuccinate dehydrogenase (succinate → fumarate, FADH₂); does NOT pump protons.
Coenzyme QCarries electrons from complexes I and II to complex III.
Cytochrome cCarries electrons from complex III to complex IV.
Complex IVHands electrons to O₂ → water; pumps protons.
Intermembrane spaceGap between mitochondrial membranes where pumped protons pile up.
Proton motive forceStored energy of the proton gradient; drives ATP synthase.
ATP synthaseProtons flow through and TURN a channel → shape change → ADP + phosphate → ATP.
ATP synthase numbers10 H⁺ per full turn = 3 ATP (3.33 H⁺/ATP).
NADH vs FADH₂ yieldNADH ~10 H⁺ → ~2.5 ATP. FADH₂ ~6 H⁺ → ~1.5 ATP.
Malate/aspartate shuttleCarries electrons from cytoplasm NADH into the mitochondria.
Superoxide (O₂•⁻)Oxygen with one extra electron; very damaging. Made in the reperfusion surge.
Cat saddle thrombus caseClot buster restores flow → reperfusion injury → death hours later.
Coupled reactionA reaction driven by pairing it with another (step 8 reverses coupled with step 4 in ischemia).
Reversible vs irreversible TCA stepsIrreversible early steps (1, 3, 4); reversible later steps (6–8) can run backward.
Pyruvate → acetyl-CoAPyruvate loses one CO₂ and makes NADH; acetyl-CoA enters the TCA cycle.
OxidizerTakes electrons and is reduced (O₂ is an excellent oxidizer).
