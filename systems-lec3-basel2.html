🔬 Basel Practice Exam 2

25 Q · LC38–39 pulmonary microanatomy · from Basel's slides only

Q1Which three cell types make up the olfactory epithelium?

✅ C. Sustentacular, basal, olfactory sensory neurons · Bowman's glands sit BELOW it

Q2A muscular fold covered by stratified squamous non-keratinized epithelium is the:

✅ D. True vocal fold · False (vestibular) fold = respiratory epithelium + mixed glands

Q3In the nasal cavity, the adventitia often contains:

✅ C. Bone (nasal conchae, skull) · LC38 upper airway microanatomy

Q4The thin layer of muscle that defines the end of the mucosa is the:

✅ B. Muscularis mucosa · Mucosa = epithelium + lamina propria + muscularis mucosa

Q5Moving down the respiratory tree, which DECREASES?

✅ C. Relative number of goblet cells · Cartilage, glands, epithelial height, goblet cells all decrease

Q6Cuboidal epithelium, continuous smooth muscle, club cells, and scattered alveolar openings identify a:

✅ A. Respiratory bronchiole · Duct = mostly alveoli + knobs

Q7Besides closing the C-shaped rings, the trachealis muscle:

✅ D. Accommodates the esophagus during swallowing · Still rigid enough to prevent collapse on inhalation

Q8Cuboidal alveolar cells that secrete surfactant to reduce surface tension are:

✅ A. Type II pneumocytes

Q9Horses are obligate nasal breathers because of:

✅ B. The position of the epiglottis · LC38 species differences

Q10Collapsible lungs in dolphins help them:

✅ C. Extract O₂ efficiently and avoid the bends · Elephant = snorkel · birds = air sacs · amphibians = skin

Q11Air entering the pleural cavity collapses the lung because it:

✅ A. Removes the negative pleural pressure · Pneumothorax

Q12Compared with tracheal respiratory epithelium, bronchial epithelium is:

✅ B. Shorter, with fewer goblet cells · Still pseudostratified columnar with cilia

Q13Which structure is ABSENT from bronchioles?

✅ A. Cartilage · Also no glands

Q14The 'smooth muscle knobs' of alveolar ducts are really:

✅ C. A spiral of smooth muscle seen in cross-section · Covered by a few cuboidal cells

Q15The serous membrane that covers the surface of the lung is the:

✅ A. Visceral pleura · Parietal = on the thoracic wall

Q16Epithelium plus lamina propria (plus muscularis mucosa) make up the:

✅ C. Mucosa

Q17Basel's slide describes the outer layer of the trachea as:

✅ D. Adventitia that blends into surrounding tissue

Q18Which feature is found in bronchi but NOT bronchioles?

✅ A. Submucosal (bronchial) glands

Q19Club (Clara) cells are:

✅ B. Non-ciliated cells of the bronchiolar epithelium

Q20Respiratory bronchioles are the transition between:

✅ D. Conducting and gas-exchange airways

Q21As alveoli crowd the alveolar duct wall, its epithelium:

✅ B. Persists only in small cuboidal patches

Q22Endothelial cells of the alveolar septum are:

✅ A. Squamous cells lining the capillaries

Q23The pleural cavity wraps around each lung like:

✅ D. A double-walled balloon

Q24Pleural fluid mainly functions to:

✅ B. Minimize friction as the lungs move

Q25Bronchi have which cartilage arrangement?

✅ D. Plates, not complete rings
