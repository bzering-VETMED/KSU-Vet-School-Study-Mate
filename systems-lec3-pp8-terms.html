⚠️ Precision traps

  • Bicarbonate changes = METABOLIC (or renal compensation) · PCO₂ changes = RESPIRATORY (or respiratory compensation)
  • Compensation brings pH TOWARD 7.4, NOT all the way (don't pick "normal pH")
  • Respiratory compensation = can't tell acute vs chronic · renal compensation = chronic
  • Metabolic ALKALOSIS is compensated by HYPOventilation (↑ PCO₂), not hyperventilation
  • 0.03 (PCO₂ → mmol, acid–base) ≠ 0.003 (O₂ solubility, O₂ content)
  • Anemia = SHALLOWER buffer line = BIGGER pH swings
  • 🟠 Transcript says "carnetine" · the horse muscle buffer is carnosine (outside knowledge: carnitine is a different molecule)
