⚠️ Precision traps

  • Left gonadal vein → left renal vein; right → caudal vena cava (same rule for testis and ovary)
  • Gastric = lesser curvature; gastroepiploic = greater
  • Splanchnic nerves are PREganglionic; vertebral and hypogastric nerves are POSTganglionic
  • Major/minor splanchnic → celiacomesenteric; lumbar splanchnic → caudal mesenteric
  • Radial = extensors; median + ulnar = flexors; musculocutaneous = elbow flexion
  • Median nerve has a cutaneous branch but NO autonomous zone
  • Axillary → brachial at the CRANIAL circumflex humeral (subscapular gives the caudal one)
