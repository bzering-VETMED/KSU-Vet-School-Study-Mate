⚠️ Precision traps

  • Right shift = ↑ P50 = LESS affinity = more OFFLOADING (left = opposite)
  • Anemia changes O₂ CONTENT, not saturation or P50
  • Haldane = O₂ affects CO₂ carriage · Bohr EFFECT = CO₂/H⁺ affect O₂ affinity (≠ Bohr EQUATION for dead space!)
  • 🟠 CO₂ %: transport (venous–arterial) = 60/30/10 · total arterial = 90/5/5 · Poole said 10% dissolved, 30% carbamino in lecture
  • 🟠 Poole said in lecture "larger animals… higher P50s" · his notes say SMALLER animals have higher P50 → use the notes
