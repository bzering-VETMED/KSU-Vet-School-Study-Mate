⚠️ Precision traps

  • PK vs PD: pharmacokinetics = what the body does to the drug (ADME); pharmacodynamics = what the drug does to the body (binding, activation, systemic response). Don't flip them.
  • KD is a RATIO, not a difference: KD = k-1 / k1 (koff over kon). Off-rate on top.
  • Low KD = HIGH affinity; high KD = low affinity. Low KD means k-1 low and k1 high.
  • KD = binding (receptors bound) vs EC50 = response (50% of maximal response). Both in vitro, both units M.
  • Units trap: KD and EC50 are concentrations in M (in vitro); ED50 is a dose in μg/m2 (in vivo).
  • Concentration response = in vitro (binding + signaling); dose response = in vivo (binding + signaling + PK).
  • TI = LD50 / ED50 (lethal on top). Bigger TI = safer. Cisplatin 1.5:1 is the narrowest on the slide; amoxicillin/penicillin >>100:1 the widest.
  • EC50 = KD → no spare receptors, binding is limiting. EC50 < KD → spare receptors, signaling is limiting. Not EC50 > KD.
  • Competitive raises EC50 (shifts right); non-competitive lowers Emax. Spare receptors make a non-competitive antagonist look competitive.
  • Phenoxybenzamine is irreversible, non-competitive; it still looks like a right shift at low doses because spare α-receptors are not depleted. Raising the dose fails because its TI is too small.

PotencyS21Strength of the response a drug produces; S21 compares potency of the response (EC50) with binding affinity (KD).
EC50 = KDS21, S22No spare receptors: 1:1 receptors to pathways; response tracks % receptors bound; binding is the limiting step.
EC50 < KDS21, S22Spare receptors: more receptors than pathways; response exceeds % bound; signaling is the limiting step. S22: effect curve sits left of % bound curve.
Spare receptors (receptor reserve)S21, S23Receptors beyond what is needed for full signal; create redundancy if receptors are deactivated.
Maximal effect (Emax)S23, S25Maximum response of the pathway; lowered by an irreversible inhibitor once receptor reserve is gone.
Irreversible antagonistS23Permanently inactivates receptors; with spare receptors the curve shifts right at full effect until reserve is used up, then Emax falls (S23 figure).
Competitive antagonistS25Shifts the curve right (raises EC50) without lowering Emax.
Non-competitive antagonistS23, S25Lowers Emax no matter the agonist dose; spare receptors can make it look competitive.
