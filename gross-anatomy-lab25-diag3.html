Penis Glans
Bulbus glandis • Barrel-shaped cavernous expansion of corpus spongiosum • Surrounds the proximal third of the os penis • Not distinct in non- erect stage • Functions to provide the “tie” or “lock” during copulation • Separated by from pars longa glandis by c.t septum

Prepuce
Fold of the skin, that covers the glans of the penis in the retracted state External lamina – Haired skin of the outer surface – Continuous with the skin of the abdominal wall Internal lamina – In contact with the penis – Terminates at the fornix Preputial orifice – External and the internal lamina meet Preputial fornix – Internal lamina reflect onto the glans penis

Penis Muscles
