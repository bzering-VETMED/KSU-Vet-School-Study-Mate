🫀 Heart wall & vessels (LC19)

Endocardium · Made ofEndothelium (simple squamous) + subendothelial loose connective tissue (CT)
Endocardium · Key detailSubendocardial layer holds Purkinje fibers. Smooth, non-thrombogenic. Damage → endocarditis, thrombus
Myocardium · Made ofCardiac muscle (thickest layer)
Myocardium · Key detailBranching fibers, central nuclei, intercalated discs (gap junctions + desmosomes), rich capillaries
Epicardium · Made ofMesothelium + CT + fat
Epicardium · Key detailHolds coronary vessels + nerves · = visceral pericardium · pericarditis

🫀 Heart wall & vessels (cont.)

Purkinje fibersSubendocardial (ventricles) · modified myocytes: larger, paler, fewer myofibrils, more glycogen, many gap junctions, many sodium channels → fast conduction
ValvesEndocardium over a fibrous core, no muscle · atrioventricular (AV) valves → chordae tendineae → papillary muscles · semilunar = no chordae
Cardiac skeletonDense fibrous CT (blue on trichrome) · fibrous rings hold valves · electrical insulator → signal crosses only at the AV node

🫀 Heart wall & vessels (cont.)

Intima · Made ofEndothelium + subendothelial CT + internal elastic lamina (wavy, distinct in arteries)
Intima · JobSmooth surface · tone (nitric oxide (NO), endothelin) · clotting control
Media · Made ofConcentric smooth muscle + elastic/reticular fibers
Media · JobVessel diameter → blood pressure (BP) + flow · thick in arteries
Adventitia · Made ofLoose CT · vasa vasorum (vessels of vessels) + nervi vasorum (autonomic nerves)
Adventitia · JobAnchors + feeds the wall · thick in veins

🧬 Lymphatic tissues, nodes, spleen & thymus (LC36–37)

Lymphatic tissueUnencapsulated, inside another organ · diffuse (mucosa-associated lymphoid tissue (MALT): gut-associated lymphoid tissue (GALT), bronchus-associated lymphoid tissue (BALT), skin-associated lymphoid tissue (SALT)) or dense (nodules)
Lymphatic organEncapsulated (lymph node, spleen, thymus)
NoduleB cells · primary = resting · secondary = germinal center (activated B cells)
Peyer’s patchesSmall intestine nodules · M cells sample antigen · B-cell maturation?
TonsilsLymphocytes invading epithelium = normal here only · crypts trap antigen

🧬 Lymphatic tissues, nodes, spleen & thymus (cont.)

Outer cortexNodules (B cells) + macrophages, dendritic cells
Paracortex (inner cortex)T cells (“thymus-dependent zone”) · enter via high endothelial venules
MedullaMedullary cords (lymphocytes, plasma cells, macrophages, reticular cells) + sinuses
Pig, elephantNodes inside-out
Ruminant, horseHemal nodes filter blood

🧬 Lymphatic tissues, nodes, spleen & thymus (cont.)

Spleen organizationNo cortex/medulla → white + red pulp · trabeculae with smooth muscle (contract, release stored blood: storage spleen dogs/cats vs defensive humans)
Spleen white pulpCentral arteriole inside periarteriolar lymphoid sheath (PALS) (T cells) (central to PALS, not nodules) · nodules (B cells) + germinal centers · marginal zone = immune reactions start
Spleen red pulpSplenic cords + sinusoids (rod-shaped endothelial cells filter effete red blood cells (RBCs)) · macrophages remove old/opsonized cells · penicillar arterioles

🧬 Lymphatic tissues, nodes, spleen & thymus (cont.)

Thymus cortexPositive selection: keep T cells that see self-MHC (cortical epithelial cells)
Thymus medullaNegative selection: delete strongly self-reactive (medullary epithelial cells + dendritic cells) → self-tolerance · Hassall’s corpuscles

🧬 Lymph flow & cases
