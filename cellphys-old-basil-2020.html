            Basil metabolism · 2020 Exam 3 · Q9in Exam 3 scopePlease explain why amino acids that are metabolized into acetyl-CoA or acetoacetate cannot be glucogenic.

              Model answer

                ⬇️ Answer?
                Model answer
                💡 Why + source
                Occurs when oxaloacetate is depleted — late fasting/starvation, uncontrolled diabetes mellitus, high-producing dairy cows. Liver converts it to ketone bodies (acetoacetate, beta-hydroxybutyrate), exported as fuel for brain/muscle, sparing glucose and recycling coenzyme A.

                  ⬇️ Answer?
                  Model answer
