⚡ L22 Quick Review

Urinary System · say the answer, then tap the pink box to check · 61 items

💧 Function & location

Path of urine?Kidney → ureter → bladder (dorsal aspect) → urethra
Two hormones the kidney secretes?Renin (blood pressure) and erythropoietin (red blood cell production)
Kidneys: intraperitoneal or retroperitoneal?Retroperitoneal
Which kidney surface is covered by peritoneum, and which layer?Ventral surface only, parietal peritoneum
Kidney surface with NO peritoneum, touching hypaxial muscles?Dorsal surface
Which kidney is fixed, which is pendulous?Right is fixed; left is pendulous (variable position)
Which kidney is more cranial, and by how much?Right, by about half a kidney length
Right kidney vertebral level?T13 or L1 to L3
Left kidney vertebral level?L2 to L4
Mnemonic for kidney position?“Left behind” (left is more caudal)
How to palpate the right kidney?Find the last (floating) rib, go dorsally
Dog and cat kidney type?Unilobar
Kidney type with visible surface lobes?Multilobar (cow)

🫘 External features & relations

Lateral border shape and neighbor on the left?Convex; spleen
Medial border shape?Concave (indented)
Right kidney medial border faces the…Caudal vena cava
Left kidney medial border faces the…Abdominal aorta
Gland at the cranial pole of both kidneys?Adrenal (suprarenal) gland
Right kidney cranial pole contacts the…Caudate process of the liver (renal impression)
Left kidney cranial pole contacts the…Left lobe of the pancreas
Right kidney caudal pole contacts the…Ascending colon
Left kidney caudal pole contacts the…Descending colon (and mesovarium in females)

🔍 Internal features

Hilus?Indented medial border where vessels and ureter enter and leave
Structures at the hilus?Renal artery, renal vein, ureter
Renal sinus?Fat-filled space created by the hilus (also called recess)
Garage analogy?Driveway = vessels · garage door = hilus · garage = sinus
Renal pelvis?Dilated proximal ureter that collects urine (the “sea”)
Pelvic recesses?Extensions feeding into the renal pelvis (the “rivers”)
Fibrous capsule covers…The entire kidney (not peritoneum)
Capsule that won’t peel off at necropsy means…Adhesions / pathology (fibrosis)
Why does the cortex look granular?Renal corpuscles (glomeruli) are only in the cortex
Why does the medulla look striated?Medullary rays
Renal crest?Shelf-like medullary edge facing the pelvis, from fused renal papillae
Openings in the renal crest where urine drips into the pelvis?Papillary foramina (not visible grossly)
Renal papilla?Apex of the renal pyramid
Renal pyramid represents the embryonic…Renal lobe
Seen only in a paramedian section?Renal pyramids and renal papillae

❤️ Blood supply

Kidney blood supply he cares about?Aorta → renal artery → arcuate arteries … → renal vein → caudal vena cava
Arcuate arteries are located at the…Corticomedullary junction (Kahoot question)
Why “arcuate”?They form an arc

🚰 Ureters & bladder

Ureter wall moves urine by…Smooth muscle peristalsis
Two parts of the ureter?Abdominal part (sagittal course) and pelvic part
Ureter enters what in the male? In the female?Genital fold (male); mesometrium of the broad ligament (female)
Ureters enter which side of the bladder?Dorsal aspect
Why do ureters enter obliquely?Prevents reflux of urine back to the kidneys
Smooth muscle of the bladder wall?Detrusor muscle
Bladder epithelium?Transitional epithelium (lets it distend)
Three parts of the bladder (cranial → caudal)?Apex, body, neck
Scar on the bladder apex is a remnant of the…Urachus
Openings of the ureters into the bladder?Ureteric orifices
Three orifices not to confuse?Ureteric (ureter), uterine (uterus), urethral (urethra)
Trigone of the bladder is formed by…Two ureteric orifices + urethral crest
Urethral crest?Median ridge where male reproductive ducts and prostate empty
Number of bladder ligaments?Three: two lateral + one median
Lateral ligament connects the bladder to…Lateral walls of the pelvic cavity
Median ligament connects the bladder to…Ventral abdominal floor
Round ligament of the bladder?Thickened free edge of the lateral ligament
Round ligament contains the remnant of the…Umbilical artery

🚪 Urethra

Male urethra carries…Urine + semen and accessory gland secretions
Two parts of the male urethra?Pelvic part and penile part
Female external urethral orifice opens into the…Vagina
