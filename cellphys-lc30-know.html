Terms: binding vs concentration response S12

Slide's own definition table for the in vitro parameters.
TermDefinition
Binding StudyA study to determine the affinity of a drug for a receptor, described by KD
KDDrug concentration (units = M) at which 50% of the receptors are bound
Concentration Response StudyA study to determine the magnitude of the effect of a drug in vitro, described by EC50
EC50Drug concentration (units = M) which elicits 50% of the maximal response in vitro
