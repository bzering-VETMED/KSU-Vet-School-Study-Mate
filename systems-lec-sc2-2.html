Arrhythmia · Atrial fibrillation
❓ What does the strip look like?
No P (wavy) · irregularly irregular · fast
❓ Causes?
Atrial enlargement (mitral disease, cardiomyopathy), large breeds
❓ Treatment?
Rate control: β-blocker, Ca²⁺ blocker, digoxin (± cardioversion)
💗 TAP TO REVEAL
Arrhythmia · ventricular premature contraction (VPC) (ventricular premature)
❓ What does the strip look like?
Wide, bizarre QRS · no P · discordant T
❓ Causes?
Cardiomyopathy, myocarditis · ↓K⁺, ↓Mg²⁺, acidosis · digoxin, anesthetics · hypoxia, stress
❓ Treatment?
Slide 26: often none if isolated · fix cause · antiarrhythmics if frequent · ⚠️ Basel: “VPCs are always problematic… at least want to watch it”
💗 TAP TO REVEAL
Arrhythmia · Ventricular tachycardia
❓ What does the strip look like?
Run of wide QRS, >100, no P
❓ Causes?
Same as VPC
❓ Treatment?
Lidocaine, cardioversion
