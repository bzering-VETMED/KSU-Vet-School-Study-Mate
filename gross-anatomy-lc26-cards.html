⚠️ Precision traps

  • ✅ CORRECTED in lecture (10/26): the S1–S3 nerve at the sacral end of slide 21 is the PUDENDAL n., not 'superficial fibular' (slide typo). Superficial fibular = L6–L7, off the common fibular.
  • Deep FEMORAL artery comes off the EXTERNAL ILIAC (before the vascular lacuna) — not off the femoral artery (slide 5).
  • Pudendoepigastric trunk and medial circumflex femoral = branches of the DEEP FEMORAL. Lateral circumflex femoral = branch of the FEMORAL.
  • Popliteal a. continues mainly as the CRANIAL tibial a.; the caudal tibial a. is the small one (slide 6).
  • Dorsal pedal a. = cranial tibial renamed at the tarsus. Perforating metatarsal a. dives between MT II and III to the DEEP PLANTAR ARCH.
  • Superficial = COMMON DIGITAL arteries; deep = METATARSAL arteries — on both dorsal and plantar surfaces (slide 7).
  • Venipuncture: DOG = LATERAL saphenous v.; CAT = MEDIAL saphenous v. Don't flip them.
  • Lateral saphenous v. drains to the DISTAL CAUDAL FEMORAL v.; medial saphenous v. drains to the FEMORAL v.
  • Palpable nodes = popliteal + superficial inguinal. Medial iliac is constant but NOT palpable; it is the single terminal node.
  • Inconstant = iliofemoral (deep inguinal) + femoral. 'Deep inguinal' is another name for iliofemoral, not for superficial inguinal.
  • Lumbosacral plexus = VENTRAL branches of L3–S3 (dog).
  • Common peroneal = common fibular (old vs new name). Superficial/deep fibular = superficial/deep peroneal.
  • Caudal gluteal n. is L7 (S1, 2) and mainly the SUPERFICIAL gluteal; cranial gluteal n. (L6, 7, S1) = middle + deep gluteal + tensor fasciae latae.
  • ⚠️ FLAG — slide 5 drawing puts the saphenous a. origin at about the same level as the middle caudal femoral a.; the order between them is from the drawing only. Confirm with the transcript.
