⚠️ Precision traps

  • 🎯 SCOPE (Dr. Malreddy, 10/26): know the NAMED nerves and the MUSCLES they innervate. Spinal cord segments (L3, L4–L6…) — 'I'm not going to hold you responsible.'
  • ✅ CORRECTED in lecture: the S1–S3 nerve on slide 21 is the PUDENDAL n. — the slide's 'superficial fibular' label there is wrong. Superficial fibular = L6–L7 branch of the common fibular.
  • SUPERFICIAL circumflex iliac a. = branch of the FEMORAL a. · DEEP circumflex iliac a. = branch of the ABDOMINAL AORTA.
  • MEDIAL circumflex femoral = continuation of the DEEP femoral · LATERAL circumflex femoral = branch of the FEMORAL (caudal aspect).
  • Veins have a COMMON ILIAC v.; arteries do NOT (aorta → 2 external iliac, 2 internal iliac, median sacral).
  • Thoracic limb = SUPERFICIAL PALMAR arch · pelvic limb = DEEP PLANTAR arch.
  • Venipuncture: DOG = cranial branch of the LATERAL saphenous v. · CAT = MEDIAL saphenous v.
  • Lateral saphenous v. → DISTAL CAUDAL FEMORAL v. · Medial saphenous v. → FEMORAL v.
  • Palpable = popliteal + SUPERFICIAL inguinal (constant). DEEP inguinal = iliofemoral = inconstant.
  • Pelvic limb has a single terminal node (MEDIAL ILIAC); the thoracic limb has NONE.
  • Chyle (fat-rich milky lymph in lacteals) ≠ chyme (partly digested food in the stomach).
  • ⚠️ FLAG — slide 4 lists dorsal pedal → perforating metatarsal. Lecture: dorsal pedal → arcuate a. → dorsal metatarsal a. II → perforating metatarsal. Use the lecture chain; for a short 'main chain' answer, slide 4's order still holds.
  • ⚠️ FLAG (still open) — exact order of the saphenous a. vs. the middle caudal femoral a. Lecture only says the descending genicular and saphenous come off the CRANIAL aspect; the order is from the slide 5 drawing.
