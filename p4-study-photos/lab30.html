Cricoid cartilage
A complete signet ring: narrow ventral arch, wide dorsal lamina. It sits caudal to the thyroid cartilage and in front of the trachea.

Arytenoid cartilage
Paired cartilages that sit on the rostral border of the cricoid lamina. Their 4 processes are muscular, vocal, corniculate and cuneiform.

The lateral process of the arytenoid. The muscles that open and close the glottis attach here (cricoarytenoideus dorsalis, cricoarytenoideus lateralis, thyroarytenoideus).

Rima glottidis
Just the gap through the glottis, between the vocal folds. The cricoarytenoideus dorsalis widens it.

19.Name it: the one label on the photo (blurred)
📚 Dr. Wilhite head + eye deck #13
Cricothyroid muscle
The "bow tie" on the ventral larynx, from the cricoid to the thyroid cartilage. It tenses the vocal folds and is the only laryngeal muscle supplied by the cranial laryngeal nerve.
💗 TAP TO REVEAL
20.Name it: highlighted on the photo
📚 course slide · Lab 30 slides #40
Cricoarytenoideus dorsalis
Runs from the dorsal cricoid lamina to the muscular process. It is the only abductor: it opens the rima glottidis. When it is paralyzed, you get laryngeal paralysis.
💗 TAP TO REVEAL
21.Name it: highlighted on the photo
📚 course slide · Lab 30 slides #42
Cricoarytenoideus lateralis
Runs from the lateral cricoid arch to the muscular process. It adducts, which closes the rima glottidis.
💗 TAP TO REVEAL
22.Name it: highlighted on the photo
📚 course slide · Lab 30 slides #44
Thyroarytenoideus
The parent muscle from the thyroid cartilage to the muscular process. It splits into vocalis (medial) and ventricularis (rostral) and adducts the glottis.
💗 TAP TO REVEAL
23.Name it: highlighted on the photo
📚 course slide · Lab 30 slides #44
Vocalis
The medial part of the thyroarytenoideus. It lies inside the vocal fold, next to the vocal ligament.

Sits in the ventral orbit, medial to the zygomatic arch. You see it once the arch is removed. Its ducts open into the mouth near the last upper molar.

A jaw-closer from the pterygopalatine fossa to the medial side of the mandible ramus and angular process.

Lateral pterygoid muscle
A smaller jaw-closer next to the medial pterygoid, from the pterygopalatine fossa to the condylar process. Your lab does not ask you to separate the two.

35.Name it: the one label on the photo (blurred)
📚 Dr. Wilhite head + eye deck #12
Digastricus
The only jaw-opener. It runs from the paracondylar process to the ventral border of the mandible and has a tendinous intersection in the middle.
💗 TAP TO REVEAL
36.Name it: the one label on the photo (blurred)
📚 Dr. Wilhite head + eye deck #13
Styloglossus
An extrinsic tongue muscle from the stylohyoid bone to the tongue. It retracts the tongue.
💗 TAP TO REVEAL
37.Name it: the one label on the photo (blurred)
📚 Dr. Wilhite head + eye deck #13
Hyoglossus
An extrinsic tongue muscle from the basihyoid and thyrohyoid to the tongue. It retracts and depresses the tongue. The hypoglossal nerve runs on its lateral surface.
💗 TAP TO REVEAL
38.Name it: highlighted on the photo
📚 course slide · Lab 30 slides #77
Genioglossus
A fan-shaped extrinsic tongue muscle from the chin (medial mandible) into the tongue. It protrudes and depresses the tongue.
💗 TAP TO REVEAL
39.Name it: the one label on the photo (blurred)
📚 Dr. Wilhite head + eye deck #12
Sternohyoideus
A strap muscle from the sternum to the basihyoid. It pulls the hyoid and tongue caudally.
💗 TAP TO REVEAL
40.Name it: the one label on the photo (blurred)
📚 Dr. Wilhite head + eye deck #13
Thyrohyoideus
From the thyroid cartilage to the thyrohyoid bone. It pulls the larynx and hyoid toward each other.
💗 TAP TO REVEAL
41.Name it: the one label on the photo (blurred)
📚 Dr. Wilhite head + eye deck #12
Mylohyoideus
A sling under the tongue between the two halves of the mandible. It raises the floor of the mouth and tongue. The hypoglossal nerve runs deep to it.
💗 TAP TO REVEAL
42.Name it: the one label on the photo (blurred)
📚 Dr. Wilhite head + eye deck #13
Geniohyoideus
From the chin to the basihyoid, deep to the mylohyoideus. It pulls the hyoid rostrally.
