1.Common carotid artery
👉 #4 on the photo
Runs up the neck with the vagosympathetic trunk in the carotid sheath. It ends by splitting into the internal and external carotids.
📚 course slide · Dr. Wilhite head + eye deck #24
2.Caudal thyroid artery
👉 at the arrow / probe
A small artery to the caudal pole of the thyroid, coming up from the brachiocephalic trunk.
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #4
3.Cranial thyroid artery
👉 highlighted on the photo
The first branch of the common carotid, to the thyroid gland, larynx and pharynx.
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #6
4.Medial retropharyngeal lymph node
👉 #5 on the photo
The big lymph node dorsal to the pharynx, medial to the mandibular salivary gland, right by the carotid split. It drains the whole head.
🌐 UMN · UMN · ventrolateral, digastricus
5.Internal carotid artery
👉 highlighted on the photo
The small branch at the carotid split that runs to the brain (through the carotid canal to the arterial circle).
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #10
6.Carotid sinus
👉 highlighted on the photo
The slight swelling at the start of the internal carotid. It senses blood pressure.
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #12
7.External carotid artery
👉 highlighted on the photo
The big continuation of the common carotid. It supplies the face, tongue and jaws and ends as the maxillary + superficial temporal arteries.
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #14
8.Superficial temporal artery
👉 highlighted on the photo
One of the 2 terminal branches of the external carotid. It runs up in front of the ear to the temporalis and eyelids.
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #28
9.Maxillary artery
👉 #3 on the photo
The bigger terminal branch of the external carotid. It passes through the alar canal and ends as the infraorbital artery.
🌐 UMN · UMN · deep dissection, maxillary artery
10.Occipital artery
👉 highlighted on the photo
A dorsal branch of the external carotid running toward the back of the skull (occipital area).
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #16
11.Cranial laryngeal artery
👉 highlighted on the photo
A branch to the larynx; it travels with the cranial laryngeal nerve.
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #18
12.Lingual artery
👉 highlighted on the photo
A branch of the external carotid into the tongue, running with the hypoglossal nerve.
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #20
13.Facial artery
👉 highlighted on the photo
A branch of the external carotid that crosses the ventral border of the mandible to the face and lips.
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #22
14.Sublingual artery
👉 highlighted on the photo
A branch off the facial artery running under the tongue to the sublingual area.
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #24
15.Caudal auricular artery
👉 highlighted on the photo
A branch of the external carotid to the back of the ear.
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #26
16.Trigeminal (fifth cranial) nerve
👉 on the photo
CN V. Its mandibular branch (V3) gives the lingual, inferior alveolar, mylohyoid and auriculotemporal nerves you see in this lab.
📚 course slide · Lab 31 slides #104 (NCSU image)
17.Lingual nerve
👉 #9 on the photo
A V3 branch: general sensation to the rostral 2/3 of the tongue.
🌐 UMN · UMN · medial view, tongue reflected
18.Inferior alveolar nerve
👉 highlighted on the photo
A V3 branch that enters the mandibular foramen and runs in the mandibular canal to the lower teeth. It exits the mental foramina.
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #38
19.Mylohyoid nerve
👉 highlighted on the photo
A V3 branch, motor to the mylohyoideus and rostral digastricus.
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #40
20.Auriculotemporal nerve
👉 highlighted on the photo
A V3 branch, sensory to the skin around the ear and temple; it rides with the facial nerve branches.
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #42
21.Inferior alveolar artery
👉 highlighted on the photo
A maxillary-artery branch entering the mandibular foramen with the inferior alveolar nerve to the lower teeth.
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #44
22.Caudal deep temporal artery
👉 highlighted on the photo
A maxillary-artery branch into the temporalis muscle.
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #46
23.Middle meningeal artery
👉 #2 on the photo
A maxillary-artery branch into the skull to the dura mater.
📖 lab guide · Fig. 5-52 Dorsal aspect of base of the skull showing arteries and nerv
24.External ophthalmic artery
👉 #3 on the photo
A maxillary-artery branch into the orbit, supplying the eye muscles and eyeball.
🌐 UMN · UMN · orbit in periorbita
25.External ethmoidal artery
👉 #1 on the photo
Comes off the external ophthalmic artery toward the ethmoid/nasal cavity.
📖 lab guide · Fig. 5-52 Dorsal aspect of base of the skull showing arteries and nerv
26.Descending palatine artery
👉 highlighted on the photo
A maxillary-artery branch in the pterygopalatine fossa. It splits into the minor palatine, major palatine and sphenopalatine arteries.
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #53
27.Minor palatine artery
👉 highlighted on the photo
A descending palatine branch to the soft palate.
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #55
28.Major palatine artery
👉 highlighted on the photo
A descending palatine branch through the palatine canal to the hard palate.
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #57
29.Sphenopalatine artery
👉 highlighted on the photo
A descending palatine branch through the sphenopalatine foramen into the nasal cavity.
📚 course slide · Lab 32 slides #59
30.Infraorbital artery
👉 highlighted on the photo
The end of the maxillary artery. It runs through the maxillary foramen and infraorbital canal with the infraorbital nerve and exits the infraorbital foramen.
