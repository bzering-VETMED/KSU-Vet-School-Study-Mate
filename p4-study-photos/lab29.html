1.Nasal aperture
👉 the bony opening at the very front (rostral end) of the skull
The bony opening at the front of the nose, framed by the nasal and incisive bones.
📖 lab guide · Fig. 5-1 Bones of skull, dorsal view (app photo, Lab 28)
2.Choanae
👉 at the arrow / probe
The caudal openings of the nasal cavity into the nasopharynx, above the hard palate's back edge.
