1.Longitudinal cerebral fissure
👉 #1 (circled in pink)
The midline groove splitting the left and right cerebral hemispheres. The falx cerebri sits in it.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p3
2.Gyri
👉 #2 (circled in pink)
The ridges (elevations) of the cerebrum. You don't need to name each one.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p3
3.Sulci
👉 #3 (circled in pink)
The grooves (depressions) between gyri. Only the pia mater dips into them, not the arachnoid.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p3
4.Frontal lobe
👉 #4 (circled in pink)
The rostral third of the cerebrum, under the frontal bone (location is approximate).
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p3
5.Parietal lobe
👉 #5 (circled in pink)
The middle third of the cerebrum, under the parietal bone.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p3
6.Occipital lobe
👉 #6 (circled in pink)
The caudal third of the cerebrum, just rostral to the transverse cerebral fissure.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p3
7.Temporal lobe
👉 #7 (circled in pink)
The lateral part of the cerebrum, under the squamous temporal bone.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p4
8.Rhinal sulcus
👉 #8 (circled in pink)
The groove that separates the new brain (neocortex) above from the old smell brain (piriform lobe) below.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p4
9.Olfactory bulb
👉 #9 (circled in pink)
The most rostral part of the brain. It sits against the cribriform plate, where CN I fibers enter.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p5
10.Olfactory peduncle
👉 #10 (circled in pink)
The stalk joining the olfactory bulb to the piriform lobe.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p5
11.Piriform lobe
👉 #11 (circled in pink)
The pear-shaped "old brain" smell area on the ventrolateral cerebrum, ventral to the rhinal sulcus.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p4
12.Cerebral cortex
👉 #71 (circled in pink)
The outer gray matter of the cerebrum (cell bodies), darker on a cut surface.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p6
13.Corona radiata
👉 #73 (circled in pink)
The white matter fanning out under the cortex. Its fibers converge into the internal capsule.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p6
14.Transverse cerebral fissure
👉 #14 (circled in pink)
The transverse gap that separates the cerebrum from the cerebellum. The tentorium cerebelli sits in it.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p3
15.Tentorium cerebelli
👉 at the probe tip
The dura fold between the cerebrum and cerebellum, attached to the bony tentorium osseum. You have to cut it to release the brain.

16.Choroid plexus of the 4th ventricle
👉 #16 (circled in pink)
The small CSF-making tuft in the roof of the 4th ventricle, under the cerebellum.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p8
17.Cerebellar peduncles
👉 #17 (circled in pink)
The 3 stalks joining the cerebellum to the brainstem. Learn them as one group.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p10
18.Cerebellar hemispheres
👉 #18 (circled in pink)
The 2 lateral halves of the cerebellum, on either side of the vermis.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p3
19.Cerebellar vermis
👉 #19 (circled in pink)
The worm-like midline part of the cerebellum, between the hemispheres.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p3
20.Folia of the cerebellum
👉 #20 (circled in pink)
The thin leaf-like ridges of the cerebellum (the cerebellum's version of gyri).
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p7
21.Medulla of the cerebellum
👉 #21 (circled in pink)
The inner white matter of the cerebellum. Its branching pattern is the arbor vitae ("tree of life").
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p7
22.Thalamus
👉 #22 (circled in pink)
The big egg-shaped mass that makes up most of the diencephalon. The two sides meet at the interthalamic adhesion.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p10
23.Hypothalamus
👉 #23 (circled in pink)
The ventral part of the diencephalon, above the hypophysis.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p9
24.Optic chiasm
👉 #24 (circled in pink)
The X where the optic nerves cross, on the ventral diencephalon. It marks the rostral edge of the diencephalon.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p5
25.Optic tracts
👉 #25 (circled in pink)
The postchiasmatic visual fibers. They run caudally from the optic chiasm to the lateral geniculate nucleus.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p6
26.Hypophysis
👉 at the probe: the hypophyseal fossa, where the hypophysis usually stays stuck
The pituitary gland. It usually tears off when you remove the brain, so look for it in the hypophyseal fossa of the skull.
🎥 pre-lab video · skull interior (app photo, Lab 28)
27.Tuber cinereum
👉 #27 (circled in pink)
The gray bulge on the ventral hypothalamus around the stalk of the hypophysis.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p5
28.Infundibulum
👉 #28 (circled in pink)
The stalk (and its lumen) connecting the hypothalamus to the hypophysis.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p5
29.Mammillary bodies
👉 #29 (circled in pink)
The round bumps on the ventral hypothalamus, caudal to the infundibulum.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p5
30.Pineal body
👉 #30 (circled in pink)
A small knob on the dorsal, caudal diencephalon (part of the epithalamus), just rostral to the rostral colliculi.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p10
31.3rd ventricle
👉 the space around circled #36 (also labeled "3rd ventricle" on the photo)
The narrow midline space around the interthalamic adhesion, in the diencephalon.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p9
32.Choroid plexus of the 3rd ventricle
👉 #32 (circled in pink)
The CSF-making tuft in the roof of the 3rd ventricle.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p9
33.Interventricular foramen
👉 #33 (circled in pink)
The opening between each lateral ventricle and the 3rd ventricle.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p9
34.Lateral geniculate nucleus
👉 #34 (circled in pink)
A bump on the caudal thalamus where the optic tract ends. It relays vision.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p10
35.Medial geniculate nucleus
👉 #35 (circled in pink)
A bump caudal (and medial) to the lateral geniculate. It relays hearing.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p10
36.Interthalamic adhesion
👉 #36 (circled in pink)
The round area where the left and right thalamus fuse across the midline. The 3rd ventricle surrounds it.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p9
37.Mesencephalic aqueduct
👉 #37 (circled in pink)
The canal through the midbrain that links the 3rd ventricle to the 4th ventricle.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p9
38.Tectum
👉 #38 (circled in pink)
The roof of the mesencephalic aqueduct. It is made of the corpora quadrigemina.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p9
39.Cerebral peduncle
👉 #39 (circled in pink)
The floor of the aqueduct: tegmentum + substantia nigra + crus cerebri.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p9
40.Crus cerebri
👉 #40 (circled in pink)
The most ventral part of the cerebral peduncle, a fiber bundle on the ventrolateral midbrain.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p4
41.Oculomotor nerve
👉 #III (circled in pink)
CN III, leaving the ventral midbrain. It marks the border between the diencephalon and the midbrain.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p5
42.Corpora quadrigemina
👉 #42a + #42b (circled in pink)
The 4 bumps on the dorsal midbrain: 2 rostral colliculi + 2 caudal colliculi.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p10
43.Rostral colliculi
👉 #42a (circled in pink)
The 2 larger, rostral bumps on the dorsal midbrain. They handle visual reflexes.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p10
44.Caudal colliculi
👉 #42b (circled in pink)
The 2 smaller, caudal bumps. They handle auditory reflexes.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p10
45.Trochlear nerve
👉 #IV (circled in pink)
CN IV, the only cranial nerve that leaves the dorsal brainstem, just caudal to the caudal colliculi.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p8
46.Transverse fibers of the pons
👉 #44 (circled in pink)
The fibers running side to side across the ventral pons (the ventral metencephalon).
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p5
47.Trigeminal nerve
👉 #V (circled in pink)
CN V, the big nerve attached to the side of the pons.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p8
48.Longitudinal fibers of the pons
👉 #44 is the pons; the longitudinal fibers run lengthwise through it, deep to the transverse fibers
Fibers running lengthwise through the pons. Caudally they continue as the pyramids.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p9
49.Trapezoid body
👉 #47 (circled in pink)
A transverse band on the rostral medulla, just caudal to the pons.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p5
50.Pyramids
👉 #48 (circled in pink)
The paired ridges on the ventral medulla, either side of the ventral median fissure.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p5
51.Ventral median fissure
👉 #49 (circled in pink)
The midline groove between the 2 pyramids.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p8
52.Pyramidal decussation
👉 #50 (circled in pink)
Where the pyramid fibers cross over. It marks the border between the medulla and spinal cord.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p5
53.Abducent nerve
👉 #VI (circled in pink)
CN VI, a tiny nerve on the ventral medulla, just caudal to the trapezoid body. It goes to the lateral rectus ("LR6").
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p5
54.Facial nerve
👉 #VII (circled in pink)
CN VII, from the side of the rostral medulla, right next to CN VIII.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p8
55.Vestibulocochlear nerve
👉 #VIII (circled in pink)
CN VIII, next to CN VII on the rostral medulla. Hearing and balance.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p8
56.Hypoglossal nerve
👉 #XII (circled in pink)
CN XII. Its rootlets leave the ventral medulla right beside the pyramid.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p5
57.Accessory nerve
👉 #XI (circled in pink)
CN XI. Its spinal root runs up beside the cervical spinal cord into the skull.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p7
58.Glossopharyngeal nerve
👉 #IX (circled in pink)
CN IX, from the side of the medulla, just rostral to CN X.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p8
59.Vagus nerve
👉 #X (circled in pink)
CN X, from the side of the medulla right behind CN IX.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p8
60.Median sulcus
👉 #58 (circled in pink)
The midline groove in the floor of the 4th ventricle.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p10
61.Sulcus limitans
👉 #59 (circled in pink)
The groove along the lateral wall of the 4th ventricle, beside the median sulcus.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p10
62.Obex
👉 #60 (circled in pink)
The ridge at the caudal end of the 4th ventricle, where the central canal of the spinal cord begins.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p10
63.Lateral apertures
👉 #61 (circled in pink)
The 2 openings on the sides of the 4th ventricle, where CSF flows into the subarachnoid space (approximate location).
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p4
64.Dorsal median sulcus
👉 #62 (circled in pink)
The dorsal midline groove on the closed medulla and spinal cord, caudal to the obex.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p6
65.Corpus callosum
👉 #63 (circled in pink)
The big white band joining the 2 cerebral hemispheres, seen as an arch on a median cut.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p9
66.Internal capsule
👉 #64 (circled in pink)
The compact band of white matter where the corona radiata fibers converge, lateral to the caudate nucleus.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p6
67.Septum pellucidum
👉 #65 (circled in pink)
The thin sheet under the corpus callosum that separates the 2 lateral ventricles.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p9
68.Fornix
👉 #66 (circled in pink)
The arched white band below the corpus callosum, coming from the hippocampus. Skip its parts.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p9
69.Lateral ventricles
👉 #67 (circled in pink)
The 2 big CSF spaces inside the cerebral hemispheres. They connect to the 3rd ventricle via the interventricular foramen.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p10
70.Hippocampus
👉 #68 (circled in pink)
The bulge in the caudal floor of the lateral ventricle.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p6
71.Choroid plexus of lateral ventricle
👉 #69 (circled in pink)
The dark CSF-making tuft inside the lateral ventricle.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p6
72.Rostral commissure
👉 #70 (circled in pink)
A small round bundle of crossing fibers on the median cut, rostroventral to the fornix.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p9
73.Caudate nucleus
👉 #72 (circled in pink)
The bulge in the rostral wall of the lateral ventricle, one of the basal nuclei.
📖 Lab 35 notes, brain photo p6
