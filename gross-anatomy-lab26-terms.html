⚠️ Precision traps

  • SAPHENOUS ARTERY is plain 'saphenous' — the vein with it is the MEDIAL saphenous vein. There is NO lateral saphenous artery.
  • SUPERFICIAL circumflex iliac = branch of the FEMORAL. DEEP circumflex iliac = branch of the AORTA (Lab 23).
  • Deep femoral artery → gives the pudendoepigastric trunk → then is renamed MEDIAL circumflex femoral. LATERAL circumflex femoral comes off the FEMORAL artery.
  • Caudal gluteal = LARGER terminal branch of the internal iliac; internal pudendal = smaller.
  • Venipuncture: DOG = cranial branch of the LATERAL saphenous vein · CAT = MEDIAL saphenous vein. Don't flip them.
  • Proximal, middle + distal caudal femoral arteries all leave the CAUDAL side; the descending genicular leaves the CRANIAL side.
  • Popliteal ARTERY runs deep to the popliteUS MUSCLE (the transcript says 'popliteal muscle').
  • Cranial tibial artery runs between the cranial tibial MUSCLE and the long digital extensor — artery and muscle share a name.
  • Peroneal = fibular (same nerve; the lab guide says peroneal, the video says fibular).
