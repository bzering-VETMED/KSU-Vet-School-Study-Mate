LC10 · Cell Adhesions

VAP805 Cell Physiology I · 9.16.26 · Shuffle-enabled

Card 1

Name the 4 constitutive cell junctions.

Tap to reveal
Tight junctions, adherens, desmosomes, gap junctions.
Card 2

Tight junction — proteins + two functions?

Tap to reveal
Claudins and occludins. Barrier/gate regulates paracellular exchange; fence stops membrane proteins diffusing between apical and basolateral membranes.
Card 3

Why does the fence matter for glucose transport?

Tap to reveal
Keeps SGLT apical and GLUT basolateral; otherwise directional transport fails.
Card 4

Three ways tight junction leakiness is controlled?

Tap to reveal
Claudin/occludin modification; actin via Rho/Rho kinases/PKC; cytokines IL-13, IFN-γ, TNF. Linked clinically to IBD.
Card 5

Adherens junction — protein chain outside in?

Tap to reveal
Cadherin dimers → catenins → adhesion belt of actin.
Card 6

Rho vs Cdc42 at adherens junctions?

Tap to reveal
Rho/ROCK → actin contraction → tension → tissue modeling. Cdc42 → cadherins removed → EMT.
Card 7

Desmosome — four named proteins?

Tap to reveal
Desmocollin + desmoglein → desmoplakin → keratin intermediate filaments.
Card 8

PKC phosphorylation of desmosomal cadherins?

Tap to reveal
Phosphorylated = weaker adhesion = Ca²⁺-dependent. Dephosphorylated = stronger adhesion = Ca²⁺-independent. Trap: phospho → weak → needs calcium.
Card 9

Gap junction — subunit, count, cutoff?

Tap to reveal
Connexins; 6 per side; roughly <1 kDa.
Card 10

What regulates gap junction permeability?

Tap to reveal
↓ pH, ↑ Ca²⁺, ↑ voltage, phosphorylation (PKC? ERK?), dopamine; connexin transcription/translation and lysosomal removal.
Card 11

Which junction joins actin vs intermediate filaments?

Tap to reveal
Adherens → actin. Desmosome → intermediate filaments. Tight junctions interact with actin indirectly; gap junctions have no cytoskeletal link.
Card 12

Cadherins — calcium, binding, roles?

Tap to reveal
Ca²⁺-dependent, homophilic. Anchoring junctions, initiation, tissue development and sorting.
Card 13

CAMs — family, calcium, binding, roles?

Tap to reveal
Immunoglobulin superfamily; not Ca²⁺-sensitive; homophilic or heterophilic. Tissue sorting and immune response. N-CAM, I-CAM.
Card 14

Selectins — what bind and roles?

Tap to reveal
Carbohydrates/glycoproteins; always heterophilic. Inflammation/immune response and uterine implantation.
Card 15

L- vs E- vs P-selectin?

Tap to reveal
L: lymphocyte entry into lymphoid organs. E/P: leukocyte extravasation into tissue.
Card 16

Integrins — structure and binding?

Tap to reveal
α+β heterodimer; inactive/active; mainly ECM, some immune cell-cell; heterophilic only.
Card 17

What is LFA-1?

Tap to reveal
Leukocyte Function-associated Antigen-1, an integrin on leukocytes binding ICAM-1 for firm adhesion.
Card 18

Walk the 4 steps of leukocyte adhesion.

Tap to reveal
Inflammation → P-selectin surface → weak binding/rolling → LFA-1 activation → LFA-1 binds ICAM-1 → arrest → diapedesis.
Card 19

Where is P-selectin before inflammation?

Tap to reveal
Sequestered inside endothelial cells.
Card 20

Selectin vs integrin in cascade?

Tap to reveal
Selectin = weak binding/rolling. Integrin = strong binding/arrest + exit.
Card 21

Holstein calf, recurrent infections + neutrophilia. Dx?

Tap to reveal
Bovine Leukocyte Adhesion Deficiency (BLAD).
Card 22

BLAD etiology/mechanism?

Tap to reveal
CD18 point mutation → low β2 integrin → no firm endothelial adhesion → leukocytes cannot leave blood → infection + neutrophilia.
Card 23

Which cascade step does CD18 loss break?

Tap to reveal
Steps 3–4: LFA-1/ICAM-1 firm adhesion. Rolling still works.
Card 24

Which two junctions use cadherin-family proteins?

Tap to reveal
Adherens and desmosomes.
