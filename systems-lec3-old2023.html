Q1Bull on a ventilator: arterial PCO₂ 40, expired PCO₂ 20. Dead space ÷ tidal volume?

✅ B · (40 − 20) ÷ 40 = 0.5

Q2Healthy-lung dog, narcotic overdose: alveolar ventilation falls to 2/3 normal, CO₂ production unchanged. Arterial PCO₂?

✅ A · 40 × 3/2 = 60

Q3Same dog on room air (inspired PO₂ 149), R = 0.8. Alveolar/arterial PO₂ falls to?

✅ E · 149 − (60 ÷ 0.8 = 75) = 74

Q4Fick: Thoroughbred at max, cardiac output 300 L/min, arterial O₂ 25, venous O₂ 2 mL/100 mL. VO₂?

✅ A · 300 × 23/100 = 69 → closest option is 66 (scan may hide a different arterial value, e.g., 24 → 66)
⚠️ Not 100% sure. See note.

Q5Rhodesian ridgeback in heart failure: cardiac output 6 → 3 L/min, arterial O₂ content normal (20), VO₂ 250 mL/min. Arteriovenous O₂ difference (mL/100 mL)?

✅ C · Half the cardiac output, same VO₂ → extraction DOUBLES (normal ~5 → 10). Strict math 250 ÷ 3,000 mL = 8.3, so pick the 'doubling' answer
⚠️ Not 100% sure. See note.

Q9Dog mauled by a coyote, normal lungs, hemoglobin 6.5 g/100 mL, 97% saturated, PaO₂ 100. O₂ content?

✅ B · 6.5 × 1.34 × 0.97 = 8.45 + 0.3 = 8.75 ≈ 8.8

Q10What stimulates the peripheral chemoreceptors to increase breathing?

Q11Emphysema (increased compliance) decreases peak expiratory flow because:

Q12Blood gas: pH <7.4, bicarbonate 40, PaCO₂ 55. Best description?
