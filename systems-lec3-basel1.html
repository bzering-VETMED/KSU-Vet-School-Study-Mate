🔬 Basel Practice Exam 1

25 Q · LC38–39 pulmonary microanatomy · from Basel's slides only

Q1Which epithelium lines most of the airways until they enter the lungs?

✅ B. Pseudostratified columnar with cilia and goblet cells · Respiratory epithelium (LC38)

Q2Glands beneath the olfactory epithelium that make mucus to dissolve odors and clear old ones are:

✅ D. Olfactory (Bowman's) glands · LC38 olfactory slide

Q3The vestibular (false) vocal fold is covered by:

✅ B. Respiratory epithelium with mixed glands · True fold = stratified squamous non-keratinized

Q4Tracheal cartilage rings mainly prevent airway collapse during:

✅ C. Inhalation, when airway pressure is negative · LC38: rigidity at negative pressures (inhalation)

Q5Scattered tracheal cells, not identifiable on H&E, that secrete serotonin and contribute to asthma when misregulated are:

✅ A. Diffuse neuroendocrine cells · DNES

Q6The outermost connective tissue layer of a respiratory organ is called serosa when it:

✅ B. Is bounded by a serous membrane · Blends in = adventitia

Q7In spontaneous pneumothorax, the most common cause of air entering the pleural cavity is:

✅ C. Rupture of blebs/bullae on the lung surface · Failure of the visceral pleura (husky case)

Q8The pleural cavity is held at a slightly negative pressure by:

✅ B. Specialized lymphatic drainage · LC38 pleural membrane slide

Q9Which species has no pleural space (filled with connective tissue), which may let it snorkel?

✅ C. Elephant · Dolphin = collapsible lungs · horse = obligate nasal breather

Q10Which combination identifies a bronchus?

✅ D. Pseudostratified epithelium, glands, cartilage plates · C-rings + trachealis = trachea

Q11Which is NOT a function of club (Clara) cells?

✅ A. Secreting serotonin as neuroendocrine signals · Club cells also act as stem cells · serotonin = DNES

Q12What distinguishes an alveolar sac from an alveolar duct?

✅ B. No smooth muscle knobs and no cuboidal epithelium · LC39 alveolar sacs slide

Q13The blood–air barrier is formed mainly by:

✅ C. Type I pneumocytes and endothelial cells · Both squamous cells

Q14In feline asthma, the bright branching pattern along the airways on x-ray reflects:

✅ D. Inflammation around bronchi and bronchioles · Himalayan cat case (LC39)

Q15Birds achieve unidirectional airflow through their lungs using:

✅ C. Anterior and posterior air sacs · Buccal pumping = some lizards · skin = amphibians

Q16Basel's upper airways are the:

✅ B. Nares, nasal cavity, and pharynx · Lower = larynx, trachea, bronchial tree, lungs

Q17The loose connective tissue of the mucosa is the:

✅ A. Lamina propria

Q18In the nasal cavity, the submucosa often contains:

✅ D. Submucosal (mucus) glands

Q19The cilia and goblet cells of respiratory epithelium work together to:

✅ A. Move mucus up the airways to clear debris

Q20The larynx is best described as:

✅ C. A cartilage-walled structure housing the vocal folds

Q21Club cells act as stem cells in the:

✅ A. Bronchioles

Q22Type I pneumocytes are:

✅ D. Squamous cells lining alveoli that form the barrier

Q23Alveoli are best described as:

✅ A. Blind ends where gas exchange occurs by diffusion

Q24A dog has dusky mucous membranes, orthopneic posture, and air outside the lungs on x-ray with no trauma. Diagnosis?

✅ D. Spontaneous pneumothorax · Husky case (LC38)

Q25Which reptiles use a diaphragm similar to mammals?

✅ A. Crocodilians
