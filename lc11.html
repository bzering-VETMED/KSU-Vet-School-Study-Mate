Card 17

Integrin — both directions

Tap to reveal

Card 18

Focal adhesion vs hemidesmosome

Focal: integrin+actin, small/dynamic. Hemidesmosome: integrin+IF via plakins, large/stable epithelial.
Card 19

Why migration uses actin

Tap to reveal
IF have no motors/static. Actin treadmills and has motors.
Card 20

MMPs

Tap to reveal

Card 21

MMP activation cascade

Tap to reveal
Pro-uPA → uPA → plasminogen → plasmin → pro-MMP → active MMP; positive feedback.
Card 22
