🦴 Skull regions + bones

CraniumEncloses the brain; its roof = calvaria
FaceBones around the respiratory + digestive entrances
Temporal bone3 parts: squamous (forms the zygomatic arch), tympanic (tympanic bulla), petrous (houses the inner ear)
Zygomatic archZygomatic process of the maxilla + zygomatic bone + zygomatic process of the temporal bone; origin of the masseter
Orbital ligamentCompletes the incomplete bony orbit in the dog (complete in the cat)
Temporal fossaWhere the temporalis muscle lives
Nasal apertureFormed by the incisive + nasal bones
Hard palateIncisive (rostral) + maxilla (middle) + palatine bone (caudal)
ChoanaeCaudal openings of the nasal cavity

🕳️ Foramina — what goes through

Optic canalOptic nerve; marks the start of the rostral cranial fossa
Orbital fissureOculomotor, trochlear, abducent nerves + ophthalmic nerve
Rostral alar foramen → alar canal → caudal alar foramenMaxillary artery runs through the alar canal
Round foramenMaxillary nerve from the cranial cavity into the alar canal
Oval foramenMandibular nerve (+ middle meningeal artery)
Maxillary foramen → infraorbital canal → infraorbital foramenInfraorbital artery, vein + nerve
Sphenopalatine foramenInto the nasal cavity (sphenopalatine vessels, caudal nasal nerve)
Caudal palatine foramen → major palatine foramenMajor palatine vessels + nerve to the hard palate
Hypoglossal canalHypoglossal nerve (cranial nerve XII)
Jugular foramenCranial nerves IX, X, XI + sigmoid sinus
Internal acoustic meatusFacial + vestibulocochlear nerves
Canal for the trigeminal nerveHouses the trigeminal ganglion
Stylomastoid foramenFacial nerve exits (between the mastoid process + external acoustic meatus)
Mandibular foramen → mandibular canal → mental foraminaInferior alveolar vessels + nerve → lower lip + chin
Cribriform plateHoles for olfactory nerves (nasal → cranial cavity)
Foramen magnumSpinal cord exits

🧠 Cranial fossae

Rostral cranial fossaCribriform plate → optic canal; olfactory bulbs + rostral frontal lobes
Middle cranial fossaOrbital fissure → dorsum sellae; contains the sella turcica (pituitary)
Sella turcicaHypophyseal fossa + dorsum sellae; houses the pituitary
Caudal cranial fossaDorsum sellae / petrous part → foramen magnum
Tentorium osseum vs tentorium cerebelliBony shelf vs dura mater attached to it; separates cerebrum from cerebellum

👂 Ear

OssiclesMalleus (on the tympanic membrane) → incus (middle) → stapes (footplate in the vestibular window)
PromontoryBulge in the tympanic bulla; holds the cochlear window (membrane-closed); vestibular window is dorsal to it

🦷 Teeth + mandible

Dental formula (each side)Upper: 3 incisors, 1 canine, 4 premolars, 2 molars · Lower: 3 incisors, 1 canine, 4 premolars, 3 molars
Shearing (carnassial) teethUpper 4th premolar (3 roots) + lower 1st molar — cut meat
Cheek teethPremolars + molars
Interalveolar vs interradicular septaBetween 2 teeth vs between 2 roots of ONE tooth
Tooth surfacesVestibular (outside) · lingual (lower, tongue side) · palatine (upper, inside) · mesial (toward 1st incisor) · distal (away) · occlusal (bite)
Condylar processArticulates with the mandibular fossa = temporomandibular joint
Coronoid processTemporalis muscle attachment
Masseteric fossaMasseter attachment on the ramus
