⚠️ Precision traps

  • Nerve (axons, outside central nervous system) vs tract (axons, inside central nervous system).
  • Ganglion (cell bodies outside) vs nucleus (cell bodies inside); 'basal ganglia' is really a nucleus.
  • Spinal cord gray matter central vs brain gray matter peripheral: inverted organization.
  • Dorsal root ganglion only: there is NO ventral root ganglion.
  • Cervical nerves exit CRANIAL to their vertebra; thoracic/lumbar exit CAUDAL.
  • First cervical nerve: LATERAL vertebral foramen, not transverse foramen, not intervertebral foramen.
  • Upper motor neuron (stays in central nervous system) controls lower motor neuron (leaves it).
  • General visceral efferent = two-neuron chain; general somatic efferent and special visceral efferent = one neuron.
  • Special visceral EFFERENT = pharyngeal arch skeletal muscle; special visceral AFFERENT = taste and smell.
  • Dorsal BRANCH (spinal nerve, epaxial) vs dorsal ROOT (sensory, enters cord) vs dorsal HORN.

Two anatomical divisions of the nervous system?Central nervous system (central nervous system) and peripheral nervous system (peripheral nervous system)

Two subdivisions of the motor division?Somatic nervous system and autonomic nervous system

Spinal nerve exits the vertebral canal through…Intervertebral foramen (except first cervical)

Is there a ventral root ganglion?No; only the dorsal root ganglion exists

Can a spinal nerve be purely sensory or purely motor?No; mixed functional fiber types
