⚠️ Precision traps

  • Veins MERGE, arteries DIVIDE: external jugular = maxillary + linguofacial (they join, not branch).
  • Linguofacial vein = facial + lingual veins. Mandibular lymph nodes sit dorsal AND ventral to it; the mandibular salivary gland sits in the gap between maxillary + linguofacial.
  • Accessory nerve = cranial nerve XI, runs PARALLEL to the long axis; cervical spinal nerves (C2–C5) run PERPENDICULAR to it.
  • Lateral thoracic NERVE (cutaneous trunci → panniculus reflex) vs lateral thoracic ARTERY/VEIN: same gap, between latissimus dorsi (dorsal) + deep pectoral (ventral). "The main thing to find is the nerve."
  • Annulus fibrosus is THINNER dorsally → herniation goes dorsally into the vertebral canal.

Atlantoaxial jointRotation ("no" joint)
Apical ligamentSingle midline tie: apex of the dens → basioccipital bone
Alar ligamentsPaired: dens → occipital bone · limit rotation
Intervertebral discShock absorber + flexible joint between vertebral bodies
Annulus fibrosusOuter fibrous rings · thinner dorsally → dorsal herniation
Ventral longitudinal ligamentTies the ventral surfaces of the vertebral bodies
Dorsal longitudinal ligamentFloor of the vertebral canal, over each disc
Interspinous + supraspinous ligamentsTie the spinous processes · supraspinous → nuchal ligament cranially
Cranial + caudal costal foveaeFacets for the head of the rib (spans two vertebrae + the disc)

🩸 Neck veins + glands

External jugular veinFormed by maxillary vein + linguofacial vein merging · drains the head · often cannulated
Maxillary veinDorsal tributary of the external jugular
Linguofacial veinVentral tributary · itself = facial + lingual veins
Mandibular salivary glandIn the gap between maxillary + linguofacial veins · saliva
Mandibular lymph nodesDorsal + ventral to the linguofacial vein · drain the superficial head
Medial retropharyngeal lymph nodeLateral to the larynx, ventral to the wing of the atlas · terminal node of the head

⚡ Neck nerves

Second cervical spinal nerveVentral branch at the level of the wing of the atlas · splits into great auricular + transverse cervical
Great auricular nerveFrom C2 · toward the base of the ear
Transverse cervical nerveFrom C2 · ventral neck skin
Third, fourth, fifth cervical spinal nervesCome out separately, perpendicular to the long axis · C5 deep branch = phrenic root
Accessory nerve (cranial nerve XI)From the brain · runs parallel to the long axis · SOLE innervation of the trapezius (also brachiocephalicus, sternocephalicus, omotransversarius)
Carotid sheath contentsCommon carotid artery (middle) · vagosympathetic trunk (dorsal, big) · recurrent laryngeal nerve (ventral, thin)
Vagosympathetic trunkVagus (parasympathetic) + sympathetic trunk together, in the carotid sheath

Dorsal intercostal arteriesFirst 3 from the costocervical trunk · rest from the thoracic aorta
Ventral intercostal branchesFrom the internal thoracic artery
Intercostal nervesIntercostal muscles + thoracic wall skin
AxillaSpace between the shoulder (limb) and the trunk
Axillary lymph nodeNear the axillary vein (biggest vein there) · thoracic wall, deep limb, thoracic mammae
Lateral thoracic nerveBetween latissimus dorsi + deep pectoral · cutaneous trunci → panniculus reflex (skin twitch)
Lateral thoracic artery + veinRun with the nerve · lateral thoracic wall + cranial thoracic mamma
Cranial thoracic mammaMammary gland over the cranial thorax (lateral thoracic artery supply)
