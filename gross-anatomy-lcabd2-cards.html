🫘 Pancreatic ducts

Ducts that open at the major duodenal papilla?Common bile duct and pancreatic duct
Duct that opens alone at the minor duodenal papilla?Accessory pancreatic duct
Pancreatic duct drains which part of the pancreas?Right lobe (head)
Accessory pancreatic duct drains which part?Left lobe (tail)
Accessory pancreatic duct in the cat?Sometimes absent

❤️ Celiac artery & stomach

Three unpaired branches of the abdominal aorta (cranial → caudal)?Celiac, cranial mesenteric, caudal mesenteric
Three branches of the celiac artery?Hepatic, left gastric, splenic
Easiest way to identify these arteries in lab?Follow each one to the organ it reaches
Right gastric artery comes off the…Hepatic artery
Gastric arteries supply which curvature?Lesser curvature (short word, short curvature)
Gastroepiploic arteries supply which curvature?Greater curvature (long word, long curvature)
Gastroduodenal artery comes off the…Hepatic artery
Gastroduodenal artery supplies…Pylorus, duodenum, pancreas
Right gastroepiploic artery comes off the…Gastroduodenal artery
Cranial pancreaticoduodenal artery comes off the…Gastroduodenal artery (celiac side)
Splenic artery runs parallel to the… and gives off…Greater curvature; short gastric arteries
Splenic artery continues as the…Left gastroepiploic artery
Left and right gastroepiploic arteries…Anastomose (join) along the greater curvature
Left/right stomach arteries are relative to whose sides?The patient’s

❤️ Cranial & caudal mesenteric

Largest visceral branch of the abdominal aorta?Cranial mesenteric artery
Cranial mesenteric artery supplies…Small and large intestines
Common trunk of the cranial mesenteric gives off…Right colic and middle colic arteries, then continues as the ileocolic
Right colic artery supplies…Ascending colon, right colic flexure, a little transverse colon
Middle colic artery supplies…Transverse colon, left colic flexure, proximal descending colon
Ileocolic artery branches?Colic branch, mesenteric ileal branch, cecal artery
Caudal pancreaticoduodenal artery comes off the…Cranial mesenteric artery
Cranial and caudal pancreaticoduodenal arteries…Anastomose with each other
Number of jejunal arteries?12–15
Two branches of the caudal mesenteric artery?Left colic and cranial rectal
Left colic artery anastomoses with the…Middle colic artery
Descending colon is supplied by…Middle colic and left colic arteries (mainly caudal mesenteric)
Cranial rectal artery anastomoses with…Middle and caudal rectal arteries (from the internal pudendal)

🩸 Caudal vena cava & portal vein

Veins into the caudal vena cava?Lumbar, deep circumflex iliac, gonadal, renal, phrenicoabdominal
Deep circumflex iliac veins drain…Lateral abdominal wall (flank)
Right gonadal vein drains into the…Caudal vena cava
Left gonadal vein drains into the…Left renal vein
Caudal vena cava passes through the diaphragm at the…Caval foramen
Portal vein drains…Gastrointestinal tract, pancreas, spleen
Two portal tributaries she named?Splenic vein and gastroduodenal vein
Portal blood flows which direction?Cranially, to the liver before the heart
Why does gut blood go to the liver first?Nutrients, toxins, pathogens processed before general circulation
Liver’s oxygenated blood comes from the…Hepatic artery (celiac)
Portal and hepatic artery blood mix in the…Liver sinusoids
Order of flow out of the liver?Sinusoids → central vein → hepatic veins → caudal vena cava
Most permeable capillary in the body?Sinusoid

🫧 Peritoneum & pouches

Serous membrane of the abdominal cavity?Peritoneum
Space between parietal and visceral peritoneum?Peritoneal cavity (peritoneal sac)
Peritoneal cavity: open or closed?Closed in males; briefly open in females (oocyte)
Only thing normally in the peritoneal cavity?Serous fluid
Parietal peritoneum lines the…Walls of the abdomen
Abdominal side of the diaphragm is covered by…Parietal peritoneum (past practical question)
Thoracic side of the diaphragm is covered by…Parietal pleura
Shiny layer on the liver or rumen surface?Visceral peritoneum
Retroperitoneal means…Behind the peritoneum, covered on one side only
Retroperitoneal example?Kidneys
Extension of parietal peritoneum around the testis and spermatic cord?Vaginal tunic
Where the peritoneum sinks into the inguinal canal?Vaginal ring
Analogy for the vaginal ring?Trash bag in a trash can
Greater sac?Larger main part of the peritoneal cavity
Lesser sac?Smaller recess behind the stomach
Opening between greater and lesser sacs?Epiploic foramen
Epiploic foramen dorsal border?Caudal vena cava
Epiploic foramen ventral border?Portal vein
Epiploic foramen lateral border?Caudate process of the caudate lobe of the liver
Pouch on either side of the rectum?Pararectal fossa
Pouch between rectum and vagina/genital fold?Rectogenital pouch
Pouch between bladder and vagina/genital fold?Vesicogenital pouch
Pouch between pubis and bladder?Pubovesical pouch
Genital fold?Peritoneum spanning between the two ductus deferens

🧵 Mesentery, omenta & ligaments

Why does the rectogenital pouch matter clinically?Vaginal tear in birth can enter it → contamination
Mesentery does what?Suspends organs from the dorsal body wall; carries vessels, nerves, lymphatics
Omenta connect…Stomach to adjacent organs
Ligaments connect…Two viscera (named for both)
Nephrosplenic ligament connects…Kidney and spleen
Root of the mesentery level and artery?L1–L2; cranial mesenteric artery
Ventral mesentery examples?Falciform ligament, median ligament of the bladder
Greater omentum comes from the embryonic…Dorsal mesogastrium
Greater omentum: how many layers?Four (superficial + deep leaf, each double)
Greater omentum attaches to…Greater curvature; stomach to dorsal body wall
Space between superficial and deep leaves of the greater omentum?Omental bursa
Organs in the dog omental bursa?None (goat: part of the rumen)
Lesser omentum attaches to…Lesser curvature → liver (covers the papillary process)
Two ligaments in the lesser omentum?Hepatogastric and hepatoduodenal

💪 Linea alba, rectus sheath & inguinal canal

Linea alba runs from… to…Xiphoid process to pelvic symphysis
Linea alba is formed by…Aponeuroses of the abdominal muscles + transverse fascia
Rectus sheath surrounds the…Rectus abdominis
Cranial external lamina?External abdominal oblique + superficial leaf of internal abdominal oblique
Cranial internal lamina?Deep leaf of internal abdominal oblique + transversus abdominis + transverse fascia
Most caudal external lamina?External abdominal oblique + internal abdominal oblique + transversus abdominis
Most caudal internal lamina?Transverse fascia only
Superficial inguinal ring is…A slit in the aponeurosis of the external abdominal oblique
Why is there a slit?The external abdominal oblique aponeurosis has two insertions
Inguinal canal lies between…Deep and superficial inguinal rings (flat, small)
Deep inguinal ring is formed by…Borders of the abdominal muscles
Fatty structure in the female inguinal ring?Vaginal process
Purpose of the inguinal canal?Testes descend through it to the scrotum
