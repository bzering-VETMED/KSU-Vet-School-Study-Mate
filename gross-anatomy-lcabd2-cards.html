⚠️ Precision traps

  • Right gonadal vein → caudal vena cava; left gonadal vein → left renal vein.
  • Gastric arteries → lesser curvature; gastroepiploic arteries → greater curvature.
  • Right gastric comes from hepatic; right gastroepiploic comes from gastroduodenal (itself from hepatic).
  • Left gastroepiploic is the continuation of the splenic artery, not a left gastric branch.
  • Left/right on the stomach = patient's left/right, never yours.
  • Abdominal diaphragm surface = parietal peritoneum; organ surface (liver, rumen) = visceral peritoneum.
  • Peritoneal cavity = peritoneal sac. Greater sac ≠ omental bursa ≠ peritoneal cavity as a whole.
  • Epiploic foramen: dorsal = caudal vena cava; ventral = portal vein. Do not flip.
  • Lesser omentum covers the PAPILLARY process; epiploic foramen's lateral border is the CAUDATE process.
  • Major papilla = common bile + pancreatic duct; minor papilla = accessory pancreatic duct alone.
  • Superficial inguinal ring = external abdominal oblique aponeurosis slit; deep ring = abdominal muscle borders.
  • Caudal rectus sheath: external lamina = all three muscles; internal lamina = transverse fascia only.
