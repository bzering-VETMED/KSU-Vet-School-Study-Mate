🔴 Common carotid

Common carotid arteryGives cranial thyroid (+ variable caudal thyroid) → splits into internal + external carotid
Cranial thyroid arteryVentral surface of the common carotid at the larynx → thyroid
Caudal thyroid arteryVariable origin; often absent; meets the cranial thyroid
Medial retropharyngeal lymph nodeSingle terminal lymph center of the head; in the atlantal fossa (wing of atlas)
Internal carotid arteryTo the brain; ID by the carotid sinus at its base
Carotid sinusBulge at the internal carotid origin; baroreceptor

🔴 External carotid branches (in order)

Occipital arteryFirst branch of the external carotid
Cranial laryngeal arterySmall, inconsistent → larynx
Lingual arteryExternal carotid → tongue; runs with the hypoglossal nerve
Facial arteryAcross the face; gives the sublingual artery
Sublingual arteryBranch of the FACIAL (lingual is from the external carotid)
Caudal auricular artery→ base of the ear; last branch before termination
Terminal branchesSuperficial temporal (Y-shaped trunk) + maxillary (goes deep to the masseter)

🔴 Maxillary artery

Maxillary arteryEmerges from the rostral alar foramen
Inferior alveolar arteryMandibular foramen → mandibular canal → lower teeth
Caudal deep temporal artery→ temporalis (usually cut)
External ophthalmic artery→ periorbita / orbit
Buccal artery→ buccinator
Minor palatine arteryFrom the maxillary/descending palatine
Descending palatine arteryShort trunk → major palatine + sphenopalatine
Major palatine arteryCaudal palatine foramen → major palatine canal → hard palate
Sphenopalatine arterySphenopalatine foramen → nasal cavity
Infraorbital arteryMaxillary continues as this after the descending palatine; maxillary foramen → infraorbital canal → infraorbital foramen; upper teeth

⚡ Trigeminal (fifth cranial) nerve

DivisionsOphthalmic (V1) · maxillary (V2) · mandibular (V3)
Lingual nerveMandibular branch; rostral-most of the trident on the medial pterygoid; sensory to the rostral 2/3 of the tongue
Mylohyoid nerveMiddle branch → mylohyoideus
Inferior alveolar nerveCaudal branch; with the inferior alveolar artery into the mandibular foramen
Auriculotemporal nerveMandibular branch; piggybacks on the dorsal buccal (facial) for facial sensation
Buccal nerve (not bold)Mandibular branch with the buccal artery → buccinator; ≠ dorsal buccal branch of the facial
