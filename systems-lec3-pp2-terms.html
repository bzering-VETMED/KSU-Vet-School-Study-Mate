Why transit time falls in exerciseCardiac output ↑ 6–10× but capillary volume only ~2×

★ Functional residual capacityEnd of normal exhale; NO muscles acting; intrapleural ≈ −5 cm H₂O
Total lung capacityMaximum air after deepest breath in
Residual volumeAir left after maximal exhale (never 0)
Vital capacityTotal lung capacity − residual volume
Can a spirometer measure residual volume?No → use nitrogen washout, helium dilution, or plethysmography
Nitrogen washoutBreathe 100% O₂, collect N₂ → N₂ volume ÷ 0.79 = lung volume
Exhalation at restPassive
EmphysemaObstructive; airways collapse (crackles); ↑ functional residual capacity + residual volume
Ventilation equationTotal ventilation = dead space ventilation + alveolar ventilation
★ Deep-slow vs rapid-shallowDeep, slow = more alveolar ventilation · rapid, shallow wastes air in dead space
Panting dogShallow breaths for heat loss, NOT hyperventilation
Dead space / tidal volume ratioHuman ~0.3 · horse/cattle 0.5–0.75 · falls with exercise
★ Bohr equationDead space / tidal volume = (PaCO₂ − expired PCO₂) ÷ PaCO₂
Cow: PaCO₂ 40, expired PCO₂ 20Dead space / tidal volume = 0.5
Anatomic vs physiologic dead spaceAnatomic = conducting zone · physiologic adds ventilation-perfusion mismatch
★ Alveolar ventilation equationAlveolar ventilation = (CO₂ output ÷ PaCO₂) × 863
Alveolar ventilation doubles, CO₂ output unchangedPaCO₂ halves
Ventilation-perfusion mismatchAlways bad → hypoxemia; severe → hypercapnia too
★ Which region gets most ventilation?BASE (gravity)
Apex alveoliStretched, stiff → expand less
Base alveoliSmall, compliant → fill easily
ComplianceVolume change ÷ pressure change (lung ~10× a balloon)
Diseased lungs + exerciseRespiratory muscles steal cardiac output from limbs → exercise intolerance

⚠️ Precision traps

  • Functional residual capacity (no muscles acting) ≠ residual volume (after max exhale)
  • Vital capacity = total lung capacity − residual volume
  • Panting ≠ hyperventilation (shallow breaths, mostly dead space)
  • Most ventilation goes to the BASE (compliant alveoli), not the apex
