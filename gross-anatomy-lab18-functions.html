⚠️ Precision traps

  • Brachiocephalic trunk order: LEFT common carotid → RIGHT common carotid → right subclavian. LEFT subclavian comes straight off the aortic arch.
  • Subclavian branches: costocervical trunk (Y, dorsal) · vertebral (C6 foramen) · internal thoracic (ventral, along sternum) · superficial cervical. Subclavian → AXILLARY at the first rib.
  • Rami communicantes = SYMPATHETIC ONLY ("don't put parasympathetic on the exam"). "Communicating branch" is accepted.
  • Splanchnic nerves = PREganglionic · vertebral nerve + hypogastric nerve = POSTganglionic.
  • Dorsal vagal trunk forms MORE CAUDALLY than the ventral (esophagus enters the diaphragm at an angle).
  • Right recurrent laryngeal hooks the right SUBCLAVIAN · left hooks the AORTIC ARCH.
  • Pectinate muscles = ATRIA · trabeculae carneae = VENTRICLES.
  • Great cardiac vein = PARACONAL groove (left) · middle cardiac vein = SUBSINUOSAL groove (right). Both → coronary sinus.
