Name #1–#8📖 lab guide · 3-17 · part 2
🧭 Orient first: view, recumbency, cranial/caudal, dorsal/ventral?
Fig. 3-17 Heart and great vessels, ventral view.
❓ #1 — name it (complete + specific)
Aortic arch
#1 · ⭐ Key fact?
Sharp turn dorsally + left; where the big branches leave
❓ #2 — name it (complete + specific)
Cranial vena cava
#2 · ➡️ Ends?
Sinus venarum of the right atrium
❓ #3 — name it (complete + specific)
Ligamentum arteriosum
#3 · 🧩 What?
Remnant of the fetal ductus arteriosus: pulmonary trunk → aorta
#3 · 🩺 Clinical?
Left recurrent laryngeal n. hooks caudal to it; persistent = PDA
❓ #4 — name it (complete + specific)
Pulmonary trunk
#4 · 📍 Arises from?
Right ventricle (conus arteriosus)
#4 · 🌿 Divides?
Right + left pulmonary aa.; linked to the aorta by the ligamentum arteriosum
❓ #5 — name it (complete + specific)
Left coronary artery
#5 · 📍 Arises from?
Left aortic sinus; short trunk
#5 · 🌿 Branches?
Circumflex br. + paraconal IV br. + septal br. (dominant in the dog)
❓ #6 — name it (complete + specific)
Right auricle
#6 · ⭐ Key fact?
Blind pouch of the right atrium, lined by pectinate mm.
❓ #7 — name it (complete + specific)
Left auricle
#7 · ⭐ Key fact?
Seen on the LEFT (auricular) surface, cranial to the paraconal groove
❓ #8 — name it (complete + specific)
Paraconal interventricular branch
#8 · 📍 Where?
Paraconal groove on the LEFT (auricular) surface
