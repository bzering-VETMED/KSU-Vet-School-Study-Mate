❓ Rat brain, sagittal (MHS 283): name 1–6.
1 = olfactory bulb · 2 = cerebrum · 3 = hippocampus · 4 = nucleus (cluster of neuron cell bodies in the CNS) · 5 = cerebellum · 6 = brain stem
🔎 How do you know?
Cerebellum = sharp layered folds (arbor vitae) · cerebrum = wide pale gray matter · brain stem = gray and white matter mixed, nuclei instead of layers
💡 Function / remember
Cerebrum = conscious perception + voluntary movement · cerebellum = coordination (feed-forward motor control)

❓ Golgi stain (MHS 284): name this cell. What is significant about it?
Purkinje cell · the efferent (output) pathway of the cerebellar cortex
🔎 How do you know?
Big cell body with a huge dendritic tree going into the molecular layer · axon goes through the granular layer
💡 Function / remember
Final common pathway out of the cerebellum (Klimek)
