      Basil metabolism · 2019 Exam 2 · Q24in Exam 3 scopeWhy is reperfusion post ischemia often more destructive than the ischemia itself? Please describe (1) the type of damage that occurs; (2) what causes that damage; and (3) how oxaloacetate and succinate are central to this process. Propose a treatment to prevent or reduce reperfusion damage.
        ⬇️ Answer?
        Model answer
        💡 Why + source
        (1) Oxidative damage from reactive oxygen species (superoxide). (2) During ischemia, tricarboxylic acid (TCA) cycle steps 6–8 run backward (oxaloacetate → malate → fumarate → succinate) to regenerate NAD+, so succinate builds up.
