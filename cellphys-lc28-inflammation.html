Eicosanoids mediate all four cardinal signs of inflammation

Rubor
Redness due to vasodilation
Tumor
Swelling due to vascular permeability
Calor
↑ Temperature due to activation of leukocytes (cytokines)
Dolor
Pain due to increased nociception

⚠️ Precision traps

  • Rubor = redness (vasoDILATION) · Tumor = swelling (vascular PERMEABILITY), not a cancer here.
  • Calor = heat from leukocyte activation (cytokines) · Dolor = pain from ↑ nociception (hyperalgesia).
  • Slide says FOUR cardinal signs (textbooks add a 5th, loss of function, outside).
  • Not every arrow is "bad": eicosanoids also enhance epithelial barrier function.
