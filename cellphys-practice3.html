LC14 · Protein structure & diagnostics

Q1Which amino acid sorts into the same group as lysine?

✅ B · Positive group = lysine, arginine, histidine.📖 LC14 S8; transcript

Q2Why should a positive ELISA for equine infectious anemia be confirmed with a Coggins test?

✅ C · ELISA: faster and more sensitive (enzyme multiplies signal) but more false positives → confirm with Coggins; Western blot if they disagree.📖 LC14 S24

Q3Which bond locks tertiary structure in place through a covalent link between side chains?

✅ A · Tertiary structure is often stabilized by disulfide bonds between cysteine residues.📖 LC14 S12
LC15 · Protein regulation & enzymes

Q4Ubiquitination reduces the amount of a protein in a cell by:

✅ D · Ubiquitin-protein ligase attaches ubiquitin → proteasome depolymerizes the protein.📖 LC15 S9

Q5A catalyst is added to a reaction. Which change occurs?

✅ B · Catalysts lower activation energy only; the energy difference (ΔG) is unchanged.📖 LC15 S17

Q6Enzyme A has a Km of 0.5 mM and enzyme B has a Km of 10 mM for the same substrate. Which statement is correct?

✅ A · Low Km = high affinity. Km says nothing about Vmax.📖 LC15 S19
LC16 · Glucose transport & glycolysis

Q7Which tissues have an absolute glucose requirement and rely mainly on GLUT1 and GLUT3?

✅ D · Brain doesn't burn fat; red cells have no mitochondria → both need glucose.📖 LC16 S7; transcript

Q8Why does phosphorylating glucose allow glucose to keep entering the cell?

✅ C · Glucose trapping: G6P is charged and can't leave, and free glucose stays low so GLUTs keep moving glucose in.📖 LC16 S8

Q9What is the net ATP cost of the Cori cycle per glucose?

✅ A · +2 ATP in muscle (glycolysis) and −6 ATP in liver (gluconeogenesis) = net −4.📖 LC16 S21
LC17 · TCA cycle & electron transport

Q10During ischemia, which citric acid cycle steps run backward to produce succinate?

✅ C · Step 8 reverses to refill NAD⁺ (coupled with step 4); steps 4–5 run forward and 6–8 run backward → succinate from both sides.📖 LC17 S12

Q11Which statement correctly describes ATP synthase?

✅ D · Protons push the channel around (10 H⁺ per turn = 3 ATP); rotation changes the synthase conformation.📖 LC17 S22

Q12In NADH + ½O₂ → NAD⁺ + H₂O, oxygen acts as:

✅ B · The oxidizer gains electrons and is reduced (O₂ → water). NADH is the reducer and is oxidized.📖 LC17 S15–17
LC18 · Glycogen & gluconeogenesis

Q13How does insulin promote glycogen storage?

✅ D · Insulin → protein phosphatase 1. Same dephosphorylation, opposite effects on the two enzymes.📖 LC18 S15

Q14Which enzyme pair carries pyruvate back up to phosphoenolpyruvate in gluconeogenesis?

✅ B · Bypass of pyruvate kinase: pyruvate → oxaloacetate → phosphoenolpyruvate.📖 LC18 S22

Q15Several dairy cows four weeks after calving have low milk yield, lethargy, weight loss, and ketones in their milk. Which explanation fits best?

✅ C · Type 1 ketosis: negative energy balance + milk glucose demand → gluconeogenesis maxed → precursors depleted → ketosis. Treat with simple carbohydrates.📖 LC18 S29
LC19 · Amino acid catabolism

Q16Which amino acid is BOTH glucogenic and ketogenic?

✅ A · Both: phenylalanine, tyrosine, threonine, isoleucine, tryptophan. Lysine is ketogenic only; alanine and glutamate are glucogenic.📖 LC19 S5

Q17Which diet is recommended for a dog with hepatic encephalopathy from a portosystemic shunt?

✅ D · Lower NH₄⁺ but keep body weight; add BCAA to fix the BCAA:AAA balance.📖 LC19 S22

Q18Creatinine in the blood comes from:

✅ C · Creatine (from urea-cycle nitrogen + glycine) stores phosphate in muscle; it breaks down spontaneously to creatinine.📖 LC19 S13, S18; transcript
LC20 · Lipid metabolism & ketones

Q19Which signal ACTIVATES acetyl-CoA carboxylase?

✅ B · ACC on: citrate (feed-forward) + insulin. ACC off: AMPK, glucagon, epinephrine (all phosphorylate).📖 LC20 S5, S11

Q20During starvation, why does the liver make ketone bodies instead of burning all its acetyl-CoA in the TCA cycle?

✅ A · No oxaloacetate → acetyl-CoA can't enter the cycle → CoA stays tied up → ketogenesis frees it.📖 LC20 S14–15

Q21The first step of β-oxidation (acyl-CoA dehydrogenase) produces:

✅ C · Step 1 → FADH₂; step 3 → NADH; step 4 (thiolase) releases acetyl-CoA.📖 LC20 S9–10
LC21 · Cell signaling

Q22Aldosterone increases Na⁺ retention in the collecting duct by:

✅ D · Steroid → mineralocorticoid receptor → new protein (SGK1) → Na⁺/water retained, K⁺ secreted.📖 LC21 "Intracellular/Nuclear Receptors"

Q23At the neuromuscular junction, acetylcholine acts on:

✅ A · nAChR opens → depolarization → ↑ cytosolic Ca²⁺ → contraction.📖 LC21 "Neuromuscular Junction"

Q24A molecule made inside a cell after a hormone binds a surface receptor is called a:

✅ B · Second messenger = intracellular signal generated after transduction (cAMP, IP3, DAG, cGMP, Ca²⁺).📖 LC21 "Terminology Review"

Q25Select all that apply. Which signals from lecture act as paracrine signals?

✅ B, C · Paracrine = diffuses to nearby cells. Vasopressin and epinephrine are (neuro)endocrine; aldosterone is endocrine.📖 LC21 "Paracrine Signaling"
