⚠️ Precision traps

  • Splanchnic nerves = preganglionic sympathetic; vertebral and hypogastric nerves = postganglionic sympathetic.
  • Pelvic nerve = preganglionic PARAsympathetic; hypogastric nerve = postganglionic sympathetic. Both reach pelvic plexus.
  • White ramus = preganglionic, myelinated, entrance; gray ramus = postganglionic, unmyelinated, exit.
  • Rami communicantes are sympathetic only; dorsal/ventral branches of spinal nerve are not rami communicantes.
  • Paravertebral (chain) = beside vertebrae, short pre; prevertebral = ventral to vertebrae, long pre.
  • Thoracolumbar = sympathetic; craniosacral = parasympathetic. Don't flip.
  • Sympathetic neuron 2 releases norepinephrine EXCEPT sweat glands (acetylcholine).
  • Adrenal medulla pathway: no ganglion synapse; chromaffin cells ARE modified neuron 2.
  • Preganglionic = presynaptic; postganglionic = postsynaptic. Same neuron, two names.
  • Major + minor splanchnic → celiacomesenteric; lumbar splanchnic → caudal mesenteric ganglion.
  • Cranial cervical ganglion = head; cervicothoracic → vertebral nerve; middle cervical sits near ansa axillaris.
  • Celiacomesenteric and pelvic plexus are sympathetic synapse sites only; vagus and pelvic nerve pass through.

★ Repeated throughout; basis of every pathway question

★ 'You need to remember that thoracolumbar means sympathetic'
Sympathetic neuron lengths (general rule)?Short preganglionic, long postganglionic
★ Listed as a 'main take-home'
Sympathetic neurotransmitters?Preganglionic acetylcholine; postganglionic norepinephrine
★ Blueprint compares neurotransmitters; contrasted repeatedly
Sympathetic exception releasing acetylcholine from neuron 2?Sweat glands (cholinergic)
★ Called 'another important exception'
Chain ganglia are also called…Paravertebral ganglia
★ Main take-home
Why 'paravertebral'?Lie on either side of the vertebral bodies
What links the chain ganglia?Sympathetic trunk
★ Main take-home
Prevertebral ganglia location?Ventral to (in front of) the vertebral column
Prevertebral ganglia neuron lengths (the exception)?Long preganglionic, short postganglionic
★ Main take-home; student question confirmed

Sympathetic ganglia visible in lab?Yes, visible to the naked eye
Why is sympathetic generally excitatory?Neuron 2 releases norepinephrine

★ Blueprint objective; in-class exam-style question
Gray ramus carries…Postganglionic sympathetic axons (neuron 2)
★ Blueprint objective; 'important to know'

Gray rami found on which spinal nerves?All spinal nerves, incl. cervical, sacral, caudal
Function of gray rami at all levels?Disperse thoracolumbar sympathetic output to the whole body
