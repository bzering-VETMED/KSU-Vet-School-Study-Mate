⚙️ Autonomic basics

Autonomic nervous system: motor or sensory?Motor
Three autonomic targets?Smooth muscle, cardiac muscle, glands
Somatic motor neurotransmitter?Acetylcholine
Neuron 1 is also called…Preganglionic (presynaptic)
Neuron 2 is also called…Postganglionic (postsynaptic)
Sympathetic origin?Thoracolumbar lateral horn
Parasympathetic origin?Craniosacral
Parasympathetic cranial nerves?III, VII, IX, X
Sacral parasympathetic nerve?Pelvic nerve
Sacral parasympathetic segments?S1–S3
Sympathetic neuron lengths?Short pre, long post
Parasympathetic neuron lengths?Long pre, short post
Sympathetic neurotransmitters?Acetylcholine, then norepinephrine
Parasympathetic neurotransmitters?Acetylcholine, then acetylcholine
Sympathetic exception that uses acetylcholine?Sweat glands

🔗 Ganglia & rami

Chain ganglia are also called…Paravertebral ganglia
What links the chain ganglia?Sympathetic trunk
Parasympathetic ganglia in organ walls?Terminal ganglia
Which ganglia are visible in lab?Sympathetic
White ramus carries…Preganglionic sympathetic axons
Gray ramus carries…Postganglionic sympathetic axons
Why is the white ramus white?Myelinated
Rami communicantes connect…Spinal nerve and sympathetic trunk
Rami belong to which division?Sympathetic only
