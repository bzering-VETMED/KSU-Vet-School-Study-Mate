Schultz 2025 Exam 4 · Q8Exam 4 lectureCells lining the vas deferens secrete bicarbonate into the reproductive duct when exposed to norepinephrine or adenosine. Schultz 2025 Exam 4 · Q11Exam 4 lectureAtrial natriuretic peptide (ANP) stimulates a pathway to cause vascular muscle relaxation. What other signaling molecule stimulates the same pathway, increasing the concentration of the same second messenger, in vascular smooth muscle cells? (select one correct response)
  1. A.Acetylcholine
  2. B.EPO
  3. C.Myosin light chain phosphatase
  4. D.NO (nitric oxide)
  5. E.Sildenafil (Viagra)
⬇️ Answer?
D. NO (nitric oxide)
💡 Why + source
NO stimulates (soluble) guanylyl cyclase → cGMP in vascular smooth muscle, the same second messenger as ANP. Sildenafil raises cGMP by blocking PDE5, not by stimulating the pathway; ACh acts on the endothelium.
