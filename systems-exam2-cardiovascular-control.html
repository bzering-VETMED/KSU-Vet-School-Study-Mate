How does ACh slow HR?

Tap to reveal
Muscarinic signaling increases K⁺ efflux and decreases Ca²⁺ entry → hyperpolarization → longer time to threshold.
Contractility

What does β₁ phosphorylation do in myocytes?

Tap to reveal
Increases L-type Ca entry, SR Ca release, cross-bridge cycling, and speeds Ca reuptake/relaxation.
Contractility

Effect of β₁ on Frank-Starling curve?

Tap to reveal
Shifts it upward: greater stroke volume at a given preload.
Clinical

Why must LV and RV output match?

Tap to reveal
They share HR, so long-term output must match; mismatch causes fluid accumulation, especially pulmonary edema.
