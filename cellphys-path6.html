★ Gq/IP₃/DAG · α1-adrenergic → vascular smooth muscle CONTRACTSStart: Norepinephrine (NE) reaches vascular smooth muscle
⬇️ 1 · Ligand + receptor?
★ NE binds the α1-ADRENERGIC receptor (7-pass GPCR)
⬇️ 2 · G protein switch?
★ Gq: GDP OUT, GTP IN on αq → αq separates from βγ
⬇️ 3 · What does αq turn on?
★ PHOSPHOLIPASE C (PLC)
⬇️ 4 · What does PLC cut, into what?
★ PIP₂ (membrane lipid) → IP₃ + DAG (two second messengers)
⬇️ IP₃ arm 5 · What does IP₃ do?
★ Opens receptor-gated channels on the ER → Ca²⁺ released
⬇️ IP₃ arm 6 · Ca²⁺ binds?
★ CALMODULIN (CaM)
⬇️ IP₃ arm 7 · Ca²⁺-CaM activates?
★ MYOSIN LIGHT CHAIN KINASE (MLCK) → phosphorylates myosin light chain (MLC) → myosin binds actin → CONTRACTION (uses ATP)
⬇️ DAG arm 5 · DAG + Ca²⁺ activate?
★ PROTEIN KINASE C (PKC)
⬇️ DAG arm 6 · PKC phosphorylates?
★ CPI-17 → which INHIBITS myosin light chain PHOSPHATASE (MLCP)
⬇️ DAG arm 7 · Net effect?
★ MLC stays phosphorylated → contraction MAINTAINED (relaxation reduced)
⬇️ Termination?
💭 NE leaves · αq GTPase: GTP → GDP · IP₃ → inositol, DAG → phosphatidic acid · CPI-17 dephosphorylated → MLCP on · Ca²⁺ pumped back to ER / out of cell

★ Gq/NO/cGMP · H1 histamine → vessel RELAXESStart: Histamine released by a MAST CELL
⬇️ 1 · Ligand + receptor + WHICH CELL?
★ Histamine binds the H1 receptor on the ENDOTHELIAL cell (S18)
⬇️ 2–4 · First steps (same as the other Gq)?
★ Gq → αq → PLC → PIP₂ → IP₃ + DAG → IP₃ releases Ca²⁺
⬇️ 5 · What does Ca²⁺ activate here?
★ NO SYNTHASE → makes nitric oxide (NO, a gas) from ARGININE
⬇️ 6 · Where does NO go?
★ A gas: diffuses a very SHORT distance from the endothelial cell into the nearby SMOOTH MUSCLE cell, then breaks down (nitrite/nitrate)
⬇️ 7 · In smooth muscle, NO stimulates?
★ GUANYLYL cyclase: GTP → cGMP
⬇️ 8 · cGMP activates?
★ PROTEIN KINASE G (PKG)
⬇️ 9 · PKG phosphorylates?
★ MYPT-1 → STIMULATES myosin phosphatase → MLC dephosphorylated → RELAXATION (vasodilation)
⬇️ Compare (S16)!
★ CPI-17 (PKC) INHIBITS myosin phosphatase → contraction · MYPT-1 (PKG) STIMULATES it → relaxation
⬇️ Termination?
💭 Histamine leaves · αq GTPase · NO oxidized · cGMP → GMP by phosphodiesterases · MYPT-1 dephosphorylated · Ca²⁺ removed

★ Gs/cAMP · β1-adrenergic → heart rate ↑Start: Norepinephrine at the sinus node (pacemaker cells)
⬇️ 1 · Ligand + receptor?
★ NE binds the β1-ADRENERGIC receptor
⬇️ 2 · G protein?
★ Gs (stimulatory): GDP → GTP, αs separates from βγ (βγ can activate GIRK channels)
⬇️ 3 · αs turns on?
★ ADENYLYL CYCLASE: ATP → cAMP
⬇️ 4a · cAMP binds directly to?
★ HCN channels → faster "funny current" (If) → faster depolarization
⬇️ 4b · cAMP also activates?
★ PKA (cAMP binds the REGULATORY subunit → catalytic subunit freed)
⬇️ 5 · PKA phosphorylates?
★ Cav3.1 T-type Ca²⁺ channels (ICa,T) → faster depolarization
⬇️ 6 · Result?
★ Faster depolarization → HEART RATE ↑
⬇️ Termination?
💭 NE leaves · αs GTPase · cAMP leaves HCN/PKA · PHOSPHODIESTERASES: cAMP → AMP · phosphatases dephosphorylate

★ Gi/cAMP · M2 muscarinic → heart rate ↓Start: Acetylcholine (ACh) from parasympathetic (vagal) fibers
⬇️ 1 · Ligand + receptor?
★ ACh binds the M2 MUSCARINIC receptor
⬇️ 2 · G protein?
★ Gi (inhibitory): GDP → GTP, αi separates from βγ
⬇️ 3a · What does βγ do here?
★ Opens GIRK (inwardly rectifying K⁺ channel) → HYPERPOLARIZATION
⬇️ 3b · What does αi do?
★ INHIBITS adenylyl cyclase → cAMP ↓ (phosphodiesterases keep breaking it down)
⬇️ 4 · Effect on HCN?
★ Fewer HCN channels have cAMP → slower funny current → slower depolarization
⬇️ 5 · Effect on PKA / Cav3.1?
★ Less PKA → phosphatases dephosphorylate Cav3.1 → less ICa,T
⬇️ 6 · Result?
★ Hyperpolarized + slower depolarization → HEART RATE ↓
⬇️ Termination?
💭 ACh leaves · αi GTPase: GTP → GDP · βγ leaves GIRK and rebinds GDP-bound α
