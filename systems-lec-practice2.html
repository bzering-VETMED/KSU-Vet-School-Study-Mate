SECTION 1 – Electrocardiophysiology & Electrocardiogram

Q1In this nodal cardiomyocyte, which channel produces the slow drift in section C?

✅ B · LC20 slide 14: phase 4 = funny current through HCN channels drifts the cell up to threshold.

Q2Section A of this nodal action potential is produced mainly by:

✅ B · LC20 slides 13–14: pacemaker phase 0 = Ca²⁺ in through T-type (Cav3.1) channels, slower than the sodium upstroke of working muscle. (Dr. Musch Part 1 slide 13 calls the upstroke L-type; L-type is not an option here.)

Q3Section B of this nodal action potential is produced by:

✅ C · Phase 3 repolarization = potassium out.

Q4Ivabradine (class 0) slows heart rate by:

✅ B

Q5Why is the plateau phase important in cardiomyocytes?

✅ A · LC20: “causes the cell to completely contract before relaxing” (no tetany).

Q6M2 receptor activation in nodal cells couples to:

✅ A · Acetylcholine → M2 → Gi → ↓cAMP (less HCN) + GIRK opens (K⁺ out → hyperpolarized).

Q7Sotalol is unusual because it has activity in which antiarrhythmic classes?

✅ C · LC20 table: “Sotalol (class II/III).”

Q8Which lead uses the right forelimb as reference (−) and the left forelimb as recording (+)?

✅ A

Q9The normal mean electrical axis range in the dog is:

✅ B · Dog 0° to +100° · human −30° to +90° (LC21–22).

Q10What is the angle of lead aVF?

✅ C · F = foot = straight down = +90°.

Q11The ECG marked “A” is consistent with:

✅ C · P waves march regularly but have no relationship to the slow, wide escape QRS complexes.

Q12The patient with ECG “A” has repeated syncope. The best long-term treatment is:

✅ D · Symptomatic advanced 2nd or 3rd degree AV block → pacemaker.

Q13The ECG marked “B” is consistent with:

✅ D · Normal sinus beats, then a long pause (sinus arrest) with no P waves, then beats resume. Westies are the classic case.

Q14The early, wide, bizarre complexes in the ECG marked “C” are:

✅ C · Wide + bizarre + no P before + discordant T = ventricular origin.

Q15Which is NOT a common cause of the complexes in ECG “C”?

✅ C · Hypothyroidism is a cause of sinus BRADYcardia. VPC causes: structural disease, low K⁺/Mg²⁺, acidosis, digoxin, hypoxia, stress.

Q16The six-lead ECG marked “D” is consistent with:

✅ B · Lead II QRS is NOT upright → check lead I: upright → LEFT axis deviation.
