⚠️ Precision traps

  • Spell it ILEUM (with an E), not ilium (the bone).
  • In the dog the ileum does NOT open into the cecum: ileocolic orifice → colon; cecum opens via the cecocolic orifice.
  • Pin on the ABDOMINAL side of the diaphragm = PARIETAL peritoneum (past practical question).
  • Kidneys are RETROPERITONEAL · "left behind": left kidney ≈ L2–L4, right ≈ T13/L1–L3.
  • Gastric arteries → LESSER curvature (short word) · gastroepiploic → GREATER curvature (long word).
  • Major duodenal papilla = bile duct + pancreatic duct · minor = accessory pancreatic duct.
  • It is "pelvic recess" (not recesses in the singular sense) · "renin" with one N.
