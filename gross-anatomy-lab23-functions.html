⚠️ Precision traps

  • Middle colic = FIRST branch of the common colic trunk.
  • Splanchnic nerves = preganglionic; synapse in the celiacomesenteric / caudal mesenteric ganglia.
  • Hypogastric nerve = postganglionic sympathetic → pelvic plexus · pelvic nerve = parasympathetic.
  • Portal vein → LIVER (not the caudal vena cava).
