Retractor penis muscle
📝 Slide note (professor) The retractor penis muscle is an elongated slip of mixed smooth and straited muscle fibers. It originates from the ventral surface of the sacrum and blends with the external anal sphincter and extends distally on the central surface of the penis to the level of the glans, where it inserts. Observe the retractor penis on the body of the penis.

Bulbus glandis
📝 Slide note (professor) The bulbus glandis, surrounding the proximal end of the os penis, is a dorsal extension of the corpus spongiosum. It is an expansile vascular structure that is largely responsible for retaining the penis within the vagina during copulation. Dissection: Make a longitudinal incision on the dorsum of the penis through the glans to observe its structure.

📝 Slide note (professor) The vestibule is the cavity that extends from the vagina to the vulva. The vestibule contains the fossa clitoridis. Dissection: Open the vestibule and vagina by a dorsal midline longitudinal incision.
