💊 LC30 · Intro to Receptor Pharmacology quiz

18 questions · Dr. Basel style · Exam 4

Exam-style questions on LC30 only, written from the slides (not an official key). Tap an answer to check it; select-all needs ✓ Check. Keep your FIRST answer unless you can name the fact that proves it wrong. 📖 = slide number.

Q1Which phrase from the lecture describes pharmacokinetics (PK)?

✅ A · PK = what the body does to the drug (ADME); PD = what the drug does to the body.📖 S5

Q2Which of the following is NOT one of the pharmacokinetic ADME processes?

✅ B · ADME = absorption, distribution, metabolism, elimination. Activation is a pharmacodynamic step.📖 S4, S6, S7

Q3How is the dissociation constant (KD) defined from the rate constants k1 (kon) and k-1 (koff)?

✅ D · KD = k-1/k1, a ratio with the off-rate on top.📖 S9, S10

Q4Drug A binds a receptor with a dissociation constant (KD) of 1 nM; Drug B binds the same receptor with a KD of 100 nM. Which statement is true?

✅ C · Low KD = high affinity; low KD means k-1 (off-rate) is low.📖 S10

Q5Which definition matches the dissociation constant (KD)?

✅ B · KD = concentration (M) at 50% receptor occupancy; the others are EC50, ED50, LD50.📖 S10, S12

Q6A researcher adds increasing amounts of drug to a constant amount of receptor in a cell-free system and plots [DR] against [D]. What does this study measure?

✅ A · A binding study measures affinity (KD) and does not care about effect.📖 S9

Q7A researcher treats cultured cells with increasing drug concentrations and plots concentration against the cellular effect. Which study and parameter is this?

✅ C · In vitro, drug concentration vs cellular effect = concentration response study, described by EC50 (M).📖 S11, S12

Q8Which parameter is paired with the unit shown for it on the lecture slides?

✅ D · KD and EC50 are concentrations in M; ED50 is a dose in μg/m2.📖 S12, S15, S19

Q9Why is dose, rather than concentration, the better measurement for in vivo studies?

✅ C · Dose does not need absolute or local concentration, but it brings PK principles into play.📖 S14

Q10A dose response study in a population of dogs depends on which combination of effects?

✅ B · Dose response studies combine PD and PK: binding, signaling, and PK effects.📖 S15

Q11How is the therapeutic index (TI) defined on the slides?

✅ A · TI = LD50/ED50, a comparison of doses; larger = safer.📖 S17, S19

Q12Based on the approximate therapeutic indexes on the slides, which drug has the narrowest margin between therapeutic and lethal doses?

✅ D · Cisplatin 1.5:1 < digoxin 2:1 < cocaine 15:1 < morphine 70:1.📖 S18

Q13For an agonist, the EC50 is lower than its dissociation constant (KD). What does this suggest about the system?

✅ D · EC50 < KD = spare receptors; signaling, not binding, is limiting.📖 S21, S22

Q14For an agonist, the EC50 equals its dissociation constant (KD). Which step is limiting in the signal pathway?

✅ A · EC50 = KD: response tracks receptors bound, so binding is the limiting step.📖 S21

Q15A tissue has spare receptors. A low dose of an irreversible antagonist is added before the agonist curve is repeated. What happens to the agonist curve?

✅ C · With receptor reserve, full effect persists despite some irreversible inhibitor; non-competitive looks competitive.📖 S23, S25

Q16A dog with a pheochromocytoma receives low, tolerable doses of phenoxybenzamine before surgery, yet massive epinephrine release still causes a hypertensive crisis. Why?

✅ B · Spare α-adrenoceptors exist in smooth muscle; low-dose phenoxybenzamine (irreversible, non-competitive) does not fully deplete the reserve.📖 S25, S26

Q17In the same pheochromocytoma case, why is the phenoxybenzamine dose not simply raised until all alpha-adrenoceptors are blocked?

✅ B · Full blockade causes substantial toxicity that outweighs the benefit; the TI is too small.📖 S27

Q18Which statements about spare receptors are correct? (select all that apply)

✅ A, C · Reserve keeps full signaling and masks non-competitive inhibition; once reserve is gone, Emax falls. Spare receptors give EC50 < KD and signaling is limiting.📖 S21, S23
