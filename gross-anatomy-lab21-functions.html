⚠️ Precision traps

  • Pudendoepigastric trunk (from the deep femoral) → CAUDAL EPIGASTRIC (deep side of rectus abdominis) + EXTERNAL PUDENDAL (out the inguinal canal).
  • External pudendal → caudal SUPERFICIAL epigastric (+ cranial scrotal/labial).
  • Flank nerves cranial → caudal: T13 → L1 cranial iliohypogastric → L2 caudal iliohypogastric → L3 ilioinguinal → L4 lateral cutaneous femoral.
  • Testicular artery = straight off the abdominal AORTA · deferent artery/vein = from the internal pudendal (prostatic).
  • Mesorchium suspends the TESTIS (testicular vessels) · mesoductus deferens suspends the DUCTUS DEFERENS.
  • Proper ligament of the testis = testis → tail of epididymis · ligament of the tail of the epididymis = tail → parietal vaginal tunic.
  • Cremaster = slip of the INTERNAL abdominal oblique. Superficial inguinal ring = slit in the EXTERNAL abdominal oblique aponeurosis.
⚠️ Vaginal process in males · Pre-lab said the vaginal process is "seen only in females; in males you see the spermatic cord." Standard anatomy: the male vaginal process becomes the vaginal tunic around the cord + testis. For the practical: a blind fatty pouch at the superficial inguinal ring in a FEMALE = vaginal process; in a male name the spermatic cord / vaginal tunic.
