  1. A.Adenine – Cytosine
  2. B.Adenine – Thymine
  3. C.Guanine – Cytosine
  4. D.Guanine - Thymine
⬇️ Answer?
C. Guanine – Cytosine
💡 Why + source
Guanine–cytosine pairs form three hydrogen bonds versus two for adenine–thymine, so they have more binding energy. A–C and G–T are mismatches.
