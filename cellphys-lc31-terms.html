⚠️ Precision traps

  • pRb binds E2F; p53 does not. p53 acts through p21, which inhibits CDK. Don't swap which molecule binds which (S15).
  • Phosphorylating pRb RELEASES E2F (turns the brake off). Unphosphorylated pRb = brake on (S15).
  • CDK activation needs ALL three: bind cyclin + ADD a phosphate + REMOVE a phosphate (phosphatase). Not add OR remove (S9).
  • Objective: pRb mutations turn the G1/S checkpoint 'permanently on' = the go signal (E2F) is always on / checkpoint bypassed, NOT permanent arrest (S16 vs S18).
  • G1 = 1 set of chromosomes, G2 = 2 sets. G0 = quiescence (outside the cycle), not a growth phase (S5).
  • Interphase = G1 + S + G2; it is NOT part of M phase even though S4 lists it with the mitosis terms (S4, S6).
  • Nuclear envelope disassembles in prometaphase, reforms in telophase. Prophase = condense (S6).
  • Cyclin B + CDK = mitosis promoting factor. Cyclin D = G1 and the Wnt/beta-catenin output. Don't flip D and B (S13, S14).
  • G1/S checks growth signals; G2/M checks DNA replication; M checks spindle attachment + chromosome distribution. DNA damage and cell size are checked at BOTH G1/S and G2/M (S8).
  • Wnt inhibits the Axin/GSK3/APC complex → beta-catenin is spared. Without Wnt, beta-catenin goes to the proteasome (S13). PI3-K makes PIP2 → PIP3, not the reverse (S12).
