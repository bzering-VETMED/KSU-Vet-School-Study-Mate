📝 Slide note (professor): The facial nerve innervates all of the superficial muscles of the head as well as the caudal belly of the digastricus and the platysma of the neck. Dissect deep between the parotid and sublingual glands to expose the facial nerve arising from the stylomastoid foramen caudal to the horizontal portion of the ear canal. The maxillary vein may be transected as it crosses the lateral surface here.
