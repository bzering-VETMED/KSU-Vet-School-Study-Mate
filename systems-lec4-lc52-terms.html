Total body water~60% of body weight (S4)
Intracellular fluid (ICF)~2/3 of body water = ~40% of body weight (S4)
Extracellular fluid (ECF)~1/3 of body water = ~20% of body weight · split into intravascular (plasma) + interstitial (tissue) fluid (S4)
Hole-in-the-bucket modelReaches a STEADY STATE, but not homeostasis: more input → more fluid stays in the system (S5)
Steady-state model with a sensorSensor compares fluid level to a SET POINT → effector opens/closes the "hole" → same fluid level at any input (S6)
2 things needed for fluid homeostasisA way to SENSE fluid level + a way to CHANGE how much fluid is retained (S6)

Osmotic pressureForce of that water flow · proportional to the NUMBER of particles in solution (S9)
OsmolaritySum of the molarity of ALL solutes (S9)
Semipermeable membraneLets water through but not solute (S9)
Hypertonic solution → cell?Cell loses water and SHRINKS (S10)
Hypotonic solution → cell?Cell takes on water, SWELLS, may burst (S10)
