O₂ uptake kineticsHow FAST O₂ uptake rises at exercise onset · tau (time constant): lower = faster
Tau across speciesHorse ~10 s (fastest) · human ~30 s · slower (~75 s) in diseased/slow species
★ Heart failure and kineticsSlower O₂ uptake kinetics → bigger O₂ deficit → less exercise tolerance · therapy goal: speed kinetics
★ Delivery or mitochondria limit kinetics?Capillary red cell flow rises within the FIRST contraction → delivery is fast → limit is mitochondrial ATP production speed ("enzyme inertia")
Myth 1: capillary hematocrit = systemicFALSE · capillary hematocrit only 20–30% of systemic (glycocalyx slows plasma, red cells move ~3× faster)
GlycocalyxFuzzy sugar-protein layer on arteriole/capillary endothelium · damaged in diabetes
Myth 2: big diffusion distances → anoxic muscleFALSE · O₂ moves easily in muscle (mitochondria form a connected network)
Myth 3: precapillary sphincters control flowFALSE in skeletal muscle · no good structural evidence
Myth 4: capillary recruitment in exerciseFALSE · 80–90% of capillaries already have moving red cells at rest
★ Myth 5: muscle transit time limits O₂ in horsesFALSE · muscle red cell transit ~0.5 s even at max flow → enough time (unlike the LUNG)

⚠️ Precision traps

  • Horse arterial PO₂ and saturation FALL in exercise, but O₂ CONTENT RISES (spleen)
  • Training raises CARDIAC OUTPUT, not arterial O₂ content
  • Muscle transit time is NOT limiting (~0.5 s) · LUNG transit time IS (<0.25 s)
  • Heart size matters RELATIVE to body mass (whale/elephant ≠ athletic)
  • Kinetics limited by mitochondria (enzyme inertia), NOT O₂ delivery (in health)
