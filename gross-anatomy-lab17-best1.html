❓ Identify the highlighted structure.
Right coronary artery
📍 Arises from? Ascending aorta (aortic sinuses): the first branches of the aorta
📝 Slide note (professor) Note:
The right coronary artery leaves the coronary sinus of the aorta. It encircles the right side of the heart in the coronary groove and often extends to the subsinuosal interventricular groove.

The coronary arteries can be difficult to find as they branch off the ascending aorta immediately after the aortic valve. You will need to dig through the fat of the coronary groove to find these arteries.

Notice how the right auricle has been reflected back to help visualize this area.
