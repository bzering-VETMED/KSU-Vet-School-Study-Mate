🔀 LC27 · G Protein-Coupled Receptors quiz

20 questions · Dr. Schultz style · Exam 4

Exam-style questions on LC27 only, written from the slides (not an official key). Tap an answer to check it; select-all needs ✓ Check. Keep your FIRST answer unless you can name the fact that proves it wrong. 📖 = slide number.

Q1A heterotrimeric G protein has guanosine diphosphate (GDP) bound to its α subunit. Which describes its state? (select one correct response)

✅ A · GDP on α = switched off; the trimer is intact. GTP = on, and α and βγ dissociate.📖 S5

Q2Which activity switches a heterotrimeric G protein off by converting guanosine triphosphate (GTP) to guanosine diphosphate (GDP)? (select one correct response)

✅ E · The α subunit possesses GTPase activity and hydrolyzes its own GTP to GDP, then re-associates with βγ.📖 S5

Q3Questions 3–4. A hypothetical drug, vasotonin-X, is a full agonist at α1-adrenergic receptors on vascular smooth muscle. Which enzyme does the activated αq subunit stimulate? (select one correct response)

✅ D · αq stimulates phospholipase C (PLC), which cleaves phosphatidylinositol bisphosphate (PIP2) into IP3 and DAG.📖 S7, S8, S10

Q4(Linked to question 3.) The diacylglycerol (DAG) made in response to vasotonin-X, together with Ca²⁺, activates which kinase, and which protein does that kinase phosphorylate? Key: PKC/PKG/PKA = protein kinase C/G/A; CPI-17 = C-kinase potentiated phosphatase inhibitor; MYPT-1 = myosin phosphatase target 1. (select one correct response)

✅ B · DAG + Ca²⁺ activate protein kinase C (PKC), which phosphorylates CPI-17 (C-kinase potentiated protein phosphatase inhibitor, 17 kDa). MYPT-1 is the PKG substrate.📖 S12

Q5In α1-adrenergic signaling in vascular smooth muscle, what is the effect of phosphorylated CPI-17? (select one correct response)

✅ C · Phosphorylated CPI-17 inhibits MLCP, so MLC stays phosphorylated; the DAG pathway reduces relaxation.📖 S12

Q6In α1-adrenergic signaling, inositol trisphosphate (IP3) produces contraction through which sequence? Key: ER = endoplasmic reticulum; MLCK/MLCP = myosin light chain kinase/phosphatase; MLC = myosin light chain; NO = nitric oxide; PKG/PKA = protein kinase G/A. (select one correct response)

✅ E · IP3 opens receptor-gated channels on the endoplasmic reticulum (ER); Ca²⁺–calmodulin activates myosin light chain kinase (MLCK), which phosphorylates myosin light chain (MLC).📖 S11

Q7Per the lecture, phosphorylation of myosin light chain (MLC) by myosin light chain kinase (MLCK) has which direct effect? (select one correct response)

✅ A · Phosphorylated MLC raises myosin head affinity for actin; MLCP dephosphorylation lowers it (relaxation).📖 S11, S13

Q8During termination of Gq/IP3/DAG signaling, inositol trisphosphate (IP3) and diacylglycerol (DAG) are broken down to what, respectively? (select one correct response)

✅ C · S13/S17: 'IP3 and DAG are broken down to inositol and phosphatidic acid, respectively.'📖 S13, S17

Q9Which events occur during activation of the Gq/NO/cGMP pathway by histamine at the H1 receptor? Key: NO = nitric oxide; cGMP = cyclic guanosine monophosphate; PIP2 = phosphatidylinositol bisphosphate; IP3 = inositol trisphosphate; DAG = diacylglycerol; MYPT-1 = myosin phosphatase target 1. (select all that apply)

✅ A, B, C · PLC → IP3 → Ca²⁺ → NO synthase (from arginine, not glutamine) → NO → guanylyl cyclase → cGMP → PKG → MYPT-1, which STIMULATES myosin phosphatase. PIP2 = phosphatidylinositol bisphosphate; IP3 = inositol trisphosphate; DAG = diacylglycerol; cGMP = cyclic guanosine monophosphate.📖 S15, S16

Q10Norepinephrine at α1-adrenergic receptors and histamine at H1 receptors both activate Gq and phospholipase C in the vessel wall. Why do they produce opposite effects on vascular tone? Key: NO = nitric oxide; cGMP = cyclic guanosine monophosphate; PKG/PKC = protein kinase G/C; DAG = diacylglycerol. (select one correct response)

✅ D · Histamine's Ca²⁺ activates nitric oxide (NO) synthase → cyclic guanosine monophosphate (cGMP) → protein kinase G (PKG) → MYPT-1 → myosin phosphatase stimulated → vasodilation.📖 S9, S14, S16, S18

Q11How does cyclic adenosine monophosphate (cAMP) speed the funny current (If) through the hyperpolarization and cyclic nucleotide gated (HCN) channel in β1-adrenergic signaling? Key: PKA = protein kinase A; cGMP = cyclic guanosine monophosphate; GIRK = G protein-gated inwardly rectifying K⁺ channel. (select one correct response)

✅ B · cAMP binds HCN directly; protein kinase A (PKA) is the route to Cav3.1 (ICa,T), not HCN.📖 S20, S22

Q12A hypothetical drug, cardiozine, selectively blocks phosphodiesterases in sinoatrial node pacemaker cells. A dog given cardiozine then receives norepinephrine. Compared with norepinephrine alone, which outcome is expected? Key: cAMP/cGMP = cyclic adenosine/guanosine monophosphate; IP3 = inositol trisphosphate. (select one correct response)

✅ C · β1/Gs raises cyclic adenosine monophosphate (cAMP); phosphodiesterases end the signal by breaking cAMP to AMP. Blocking them prolongs the cAMP-driven ↑ heart rate.📖 S22, S23

Q13Which steps terminate β1-adrenergic Gs/cAMP signaling in cardiac pacemaker cells? Key: cAMP = cyclic adenosine monophosphate; GTP/GDP = guanosine tri/diphosphate; AMP = adenosine monophosphate; IP3 = inositol trisphosphate; DAG = diacylglycerol. (select all that apply)

✅ A, B, D · S23 lists ligand dissociation, αs GTPase, cAMP leaving HCN/PKA, phosphodiesterases (cAMP → AMP), phosphatases. IP3/DAG breakdown is Gq termination.📖 S21, S23

Q14In an M2 muscarinic receptor pacemaker cell, the αi subunit has just inhibited adenylyl cyclase. What lowers the existing cytosolic cyclic adenosine monophosphate (cAMP)? Key: AMP/ATP = adenosine mono/triphosphate; cGMP = cyclic guanosine monophosphate; GIRK = G protein-gated inwardly rectifying K⁺ channel. (select one correct response)

✅ A · αi stops new cAMP synthesis; ongoing phosphodiesterase activity lowers the cAMP already present.📖 S25, S26

Q15In the Gi/cAMP pathway in cardiac pacemaker cells, which subunit activates the inwardly rectifying K⁺ (GIRK) channel, and with what effect? (select one correct response)

✅ D · The βγ subunit binds and activates GIRK → hyperpolarization; with slower HCN and Cav3.1 activity this decreases heart rate.📖 S24, S26

Q16Acetylcholine (ACh) acts on sinoatrial node pacemaker cells, bronchial smooth muscle, and vascular endothelium. Which receptor–pathway–response pairing matches the lecture? Key: NO = nitric oxide; cGMP/cAMP = cyclic guanosine/adenosine monophosphate; IP3 = inositol trisphosphate; DAG = diacylglycerol. (select one correct response)

✅ B · ACh: M2/Gi in heart (↓ rate); M1, M3/Gq/IP3/DAG in bronchi (constriction); M3/Gq/NO/cGMP in vascular endothelium (vasodilation). One ligand, many receptor subtypes (S30).📖 S26, S29, S30

Q17Which pairing of G protein, second messenger, and major target matches the lecture's summary table? Key: IP3 = inositol trisphosphate; DAG = diacylglycerol; NO = nitric oxide; cGMP/cAMP = cyclic guanosine/adenosine monophosphate. (select one correct response)

✅ E · S6: Gq → IP3/DAG → PKC; Gq → NO/cGMP → PKG; Gs → cAMP ↑ → PKA ↑; Gi → cAMP ↓ → PKA ↓. (NO = nitric oxide; cGMP/cAMP = cyclic guanosine/adenosine monophosphate.)📖 S6

Q18Which statement about G protein-coupled receptors (GPCRs) is NOT consistent with the lecture? (select one correct response)

✅ A · GPCRs are seven-pass transmembrane proteins with an extracellular ligand-binding site and a cytosolic G protein binding site.📖 S3, S30

Q19According to the lecture summary, which statements about G protein-coupled receptor (GPCR) signaling are correct? Key: GTP = guanosine triphosphate. (select all that apply)

✅ B, D, E · S30 summary points. GTP hydrolysis ENDS signaling; GDP/GTP exchange and subunit dissociation initiate it.📖 S5, S30

Q20Vasopressin acts on the kidney distal nephron and on arteries. Which receptor and pathway match each tissue in the lecture appendix? Key: cAMP = cyclic adenosine monophosphate; IP3 = inositol trisphosphate; DAG = diacylglycerol; NO = nitric oxide; cGMP = cyclic guanosine monophosphate. (select one correct response)

✅ E · Appendix: V2 (Gs/cAMP) → water reabsorption; V1 (Gq/IP3/DAG) → vasoconstriction. Appendix scope unconfirmed until transcript.📖 S28, S29
