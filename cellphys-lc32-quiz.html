💀 LC32 · Apoptosis and Oncogenes quiz

18 questions · Dr. Basel style · Exam 4

Exam-style questions on LC32 only, written from the slides (not an official key). Tap an answer to check it; select-all needs ✓ Check. Keep your FIRST answer unless you can name the fact that proves it wrong. 📖 = slide number.

Q1A cell undergoes death in which its DNA is catalytically fragmented, the cytoplasm forms blebs, and no inflammation follows. Which process is this?

✅ D · Fragmented DNA, blebbing, and no spilled contents (no inflammation) define apoptosis.📖 S5, S6

Q2Which example does the lecture give for the REGULATION role of normal apoptosis?

✅ C · Regulation = excess CD8+ T cells; maintenance = old liver cells; surveillance = damaged cells; development = digits, neuron pruning.📖 S5

Q3Which feature belongs to necrosis and NOT to apoptosis? (ECM = extracellular matrix)

✅ B · Necrosis: uncontrolled, acute stress, rupture, contents spilled into ECM, inflammatory.📖 S6

Q4A dog has progressive neuron loss in which apoptosis outweighs mitosis. How does the lecture classify this tissue state?

✅ A · Apoptosis > mitosis = degeneration (e.g. Parkinson's); mitosis > apoptosis = neoplasia.📖 S7

Q5Which caspases are the EFFECTOR caspases in this lecture?

✅ A · Initiators = 8 or 9; effectors = 3, 6, 7.📖 S8

Q6After Bax opens mitochondrial membrane pores, what happens NEXT?

✅ D · Bax pores → cytochrome C release → combines with Apaf-1 → apoptosome → procaspase 9.📖 S10

Q7Which complex activates procaspase 9 in the internal pathway?

✅ B · Cytochrome C + Apaf-1 = apoptosome, which activates procaspase 9.📖 S10

Q8A neuron is withdrawn from its trophic (survival) factors. Which outcome is expected from the slides?

✅ C · Trophic factors drive Bcl-2; their removal is listed as a Bax pathway trigger.📖 S9, S10

Q9Per the S9 figure, which of the following can trigger the internal (Bax/mitochondrial) apoptosis pathway? (select all that apply)

✅ A, C, D · S9 lists damage sensing (DNA damage → p53 → Bax) and removal of trophic factors; the figure also shows oxidative stress + calcium (Ca2+) overload acting on mitochondria. FasL is the external pathway; Bcl-2 inhibits.📖 S9

Q10In the external pathway, after Fas ligand (FasL) binds the Fas receptor and Fas trimerizes, what is recruited?

✅ A · FasL binds Fas → trimerizes → recruits FADD → procaspase 8 activated.📖 S13

Q11A tumor evades cytotoxic cluster of differentiation 8 (CD8+) T cells by killing them on contact. Based on the Fas ligand (FasL) slide, what does the tumor express?

✅ B · Tumor anti-immunity: tumor cell expresses FasL, activating Fas/FADD on the T cell → T-cell apoptosis.📖 S14

Q12A Ras mutation leaves the protein active WITHOUT a growth signal. What can the mutated Ras no longer do? (GTP = guanosine triphosphate; GDP = guanosine diphosphate)

✅ D · Mutated Ras cannot hydrolyze GTP, so its off switch fails; it keeps signaling through MAPK.📖 S17, S18

Q13Mutated AKT promotes tumorigenesis mainly by which mechanism?

✅ C · Constantly active AKT → high Bcl-2 → internal apoptosis inhibited → overpopulation.📖 S20

Q14How does the lecture classify Ras, AKT, p53, and APC?

✅ C · Ras and AKT = protooncogenes (S17, S19); p53 and APC = tumor-suppressor genes (S21, S23).📖 S17–S24

Q15A cell carries p53 that cannot be phosphorylated. After ATM (the damage-sensing kinase) senses DNA damage, what follows? (CDK = cyclin-dependent kinase)

✅ D · Mutated p53 can't respond to ATM → no p21 → no CDK inhibition → cell advances despite damage.📖 S22

Q16Mutated APC (a tumor-suppressor gene) cannot bind Axin/GSK3. Which product is constantly transcribed as a result?

✅ B · No APC–Axin/GSK3 binding → beta-catenin not ubiquitinated → high beta-catenin → cyclin D.📖 S24

Q17Which human papilloma virus (HPV) protein inactivates its target by causing that target's ubiquitination and degradation?

✅ A · E6 → p53 ubiquitination/degradation; E7 binds Rb, freeing E2F.📖 S29

Q18A 9-year-old German Shepherd has nodular colon masses and neoplastic biopsy cells. Which mutation order fits the colon carcinoma slide?

✅ B · Normal epithelium → APC → Ras (adenoma) → p53 (carcinoma) → further mutations → metastasis.📖 S31, S32
