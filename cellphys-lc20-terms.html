⚠️ Precision traps

  • HSL = breakdown (lipolysis) · ACC = synthesis (lipogenesis)
  • Fat synthesis uses NADPH, not NADH
  • Carnitine shuttle = fatty acids · malate/aspartate = NADH electrons
  • Ketosis > 0.5 mM (normal) · ketoacidosis > 1.5 mM (pathological)
  • Liver makes ketones but can't use them (no 3-oxoacid CoA transferase)
  • Ketones form when oxaloacetate + free CoA run out

🧈 LC20 · Lipid metabolism & ketones

LipogenesisStoring energy as fat: fatty acid then triglyceride synthesis (liver + adipose).
Acetyl-CoA carboxylaseCommitted, rate-limiting step of fatty acid synthesis: acetyl-CoA → malonyl-CoA.
Fatty acid synthesis carrierUses NADPH (not NADH); adds 2 carbons at a time.
ACC turned ON byHigh citrate (feed-forward) + insulin dephosphorylation.
ACC turned OFF byPhosphorylation by AMP-activated kinase, glucagon (PKA), or epinephrine (PKA).
Triglyceride synthesisGlycerol-3-phosphate (from DHAP) + 3 fatty acids; liver ships it out as VLDL.
VLDLVery low density lipoprotein: carries liver triglyceride to the body.
LipolysisUsing fat for energy: hormone-sensitive lipase, then β-oxidation.
Hormone-sensitive lipaseMain lipolysis enzyme; cuts fatty acids off glycerol. Phosphorylation turns it ON.
Carnitine shuttleMoves fatty acyl-CoA into the mitochondria (it can't cross alone).
β-oxidation stepsAcyl-CoA dehydrogenase (FADH₂) → enoyl-CoA hydratase → 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase (NADH) → thiolase.
ThiolaseCuts off acetyl-CoA; chain shortens by 2 carbons per round.
Palmitate yield16-carbon fat → ~106 ATP.
Epinephrine / glucagon on fatPKA phosphorylates → HSL ON, ACC OFF → burn fat.
Insulin on fatProtein phosphatase dephosphorylates → HSL OFF, ACC ON → store fat.
Ketogenesis triggerOxaloacetate (used by gluconeogenesis) and free CoA run out.
Ketogenesis purposeRegenerates free CoA; ketones fuel heart and brain. Liver and kidney.
Ketone bodiesAcetoacetate, β-hydroxybutyrate, acetone.
Liver can't use ketonesLacks 3-oxoacid CoA transferase.
Ketone levelsNormal ~0.1 mM · ketosis > 0.5 mM (normal) · ketoacidosis > 1.5 mM (pH < 7.35).
Diabetic ketoacidosisGlucose can't enter cells (no insulin) → cells "starve" → burn fat → ketones.
Malonyl-CoAAcetyl-CoA carboxylase product; 3-carbon donor for fatty acid synthesis.
AMP-activated protein kinaseLow-energy sensor; phosphorylates ACC (off) and HSL (on).
Fatty acyl-CoA synthetaseActivates fatty acids in the cytosol (costs 2 ATP).
KetonuriaKetones in urine; barely detectable in ketosis, obvious in ketoacidosis.
AcetoneKetone body formed spontaneously from acetoacetate.
