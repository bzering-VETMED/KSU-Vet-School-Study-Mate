❓ Identify the highlighted structure.
Major splanchnic nerves
The major splanchnic nerve leaves the sympathetic trunk at the level of the 12th or 13th thoracic sympathetic ganglion. It passes dorsal to the crus of the diaphragm, enters the abdominal cavity, and courses to the adrenal and celiacomesenteric ganglia and plexus.

❓ Abdomen, transverse section. Name 1–6.
#1 Abdominal cavity (everything inside the wall) · #2 Peritoneal cavity (space between peritoneal layers) · #3 Parietal peritoneum · #4 Visceral peritoneum · #5 Connecting peritoneum (mesentery: double fold) · #6 Kidney = retroperitoneal
Parietal lines the wall, visceral covers organs, connecting peritoneum (mesenteries, omenta, ligaments) joins them. Peritoneal cavity = potential space inside the peritoneum; abdominal cavity = the whole space inside the wall
⚠️ Pair
abdominal cavity (big) ≠ peritoneal cavity (inside the peritoneal sac only)

Mesometrium
📝 Slide note (professor)
The mesometrium arises from the lateral wall of the pelvis and the lateral part of the sublumbar region and attaches to the lateral part of the cranial end of the vagina, uterine cervix and the corresponding uterine horn. ❓ Fetus, left lateral. Name 1–4.
#1 Umbilical vein · #2 Falciform ligament · #3 Liver · #4 Umbilicus
The umbilical vein runs from the umbilicus to the liver in the free border of the falciform ligament. After birth it becomes the round ligament of the liver
🔁 Fetal remnants
Umbilical vein → round ligament of the liver · ductus arteriosus → ligamentum arteriosum · foramen ovale → fossa ovalis
