👃 Nasal cavity

Nasal septumSeparates left + right nasal cavities; cartilaginous rostrally, bony caudally
ConchaeScrolls (turbinates): dorsal, ventral (more extensive), ethmoidal
Dorsal / middle / ventral nasal meatusNasal bone↔dorsal concha · dorsal↔ventral concha · ventral concha↔hard palate
Common nasal meatusVertical space beside the septum joining all meatuses
Paranasal sinuses (dog)ONLY 2: frontal sinus + maxillary recess
Lateral nasal glandOccupies the maxillary recess
Vomeronasal organPheromone chemosensation; dorsal to the hard palate; drains via the incisive duct at the incisive papilla

😶 Face muscles (facial nerve)

PlatysmaCutaneous muscle of head + neck
Orbicularis orisCloses the lips (round muscle around the mouth)
BuccinatorReturns food from the vestibule to the occlusal surfaces
Levator nasolabialisRaises the upper lip + dilates the nostril
Orbicularis oculiCloses the eyelids
Retractor anguli oculi lateralisAt the lateral commissure (near the angularis oculi vein)
Levator palpebrae superiorisRaises the upper lid — OCULOMOTOR nerve (not facial)
Scutiform cartilageBoot-shaped; rostral + caudal auricular muscles attach

👁️ Eye + tears

Palpebral fissureOpening between the eyelids
Medial palpebral ligamentBetter developed than the lateral
Conjunctival fornixWhere palpebral meets bulbar conjunctiva (full 360°)
Lacrimal caruncleMedial triangular thickening; pools tears
Tear drainageLacrimal punctum → lacrimal canaliculus → lacrimal sac → nasolacrimal duct → opening at the ventral alar fold (rostral ventral nasal meatus)
Clinical: fluoresceinExits at the nostril via the nasolacrimal duct opening
Third eyelidSuperficial gland of the third eyelid contributes tears

👅 Mouth + salivary

Parotid ductOpens at the papilla by the upper 4th premolar
Zygomatic ductsOpen by the last upper molar
Mandibular + major sublingual ductsRun in the sublingual fold → open at the sublingual caruncle
Parotid lymph nodeRostral to the parotid salivary gland
PapillaeFiliform (body + apex) · conical (root) · fungiform (spots) · vallate (root, circular) · foliate (lateral root)
LyssaFibrous rod on the ventral midline of the tongue apex; drinking/lapping

🔁 Pharynx

OropharynxPalatoglossal arch → palatopharyngeal arch
NasopharynxChoanae → palatopharyngeal arch; auditory tube opens here
LaryngopharynxDorsal to the larynx → pharyngoesophageal limen (start of the esophagus)
Palatine tonsilUnder the semilunar fold in the oropharynx
Pharyngeal constrictors (rostral → caudal)Hyopharyngeus → thyropharyngeus → cricopharyngeus (HTC); cricopharyngeus continues into the esophagus
Cranial laryngeal nervePasses between the hyopharyngeus (cranial) + thyropharyngeus (caudal)
