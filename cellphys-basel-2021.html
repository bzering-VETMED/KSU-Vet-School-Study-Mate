Basel 2021 Exam 4 · Q64Exam 4 lectureshort answerYou are characterizing a new NSAID drug candidate (New NSAID). You have run binding studies, efficacy studies, and dose-response studies on the new drug. Please describe the following (include correct units on all answers):
  • a. What are the K_(D) of New NSAID at the COX1/PTGS1 and COX2/PTGS2 enzymes?
  • b. What are the EC₅₀ of New NSAID in the gastric mucosa and in endothelium?
  • c. What is the E_(Max) of New NSAID in the gastric mucosa and in endothelium?
  • d. What is the ED₅₀ and LD₅₀ of New NSAID?
  • e. What is the therapeutic index of New NSAID?
  • f. Is New NSAID a PTGS1 selective inhibitor, a PTGS2 selective inhibitor, or both equally? Explain.
  • g. Predict a significant side effect of New NSAID. Explain your prediction.
⬇️ Answer?
(a) KD: COX-1 ≈ 100 ng/mL, COX-2 ≈ 1000 ng/mL. (b) EC50: gastric mucosa ≈ 10 ng/mL; endothelium ≈ 1000 ng/mL. (c) Emax: gastric ↓ PGE2 by ~35 ng/mL (45 → 10); endothelium ↓ PGI2 by ~22 ng/mL (30 → 8). (d) ED50 ≈ 0.1 mg/kg; LD50 ≈ 1000 mg/kg. (e) TI ≈ 10,000 (unitless). (f) PTGS1 (COX-1) selective: 10× lower KD at COX-1 and 100× lower EC50 in gastric mucosa (COX-1 tissue). (g) Gastric ulcers (↓ protective PGE2, shunt to LTB4); also possible bleeding (↓ platelet TxA2).likely
💡 Why + source
Read KD from the binding graphs (50% bound), EC50 from halfway between the top and bottom plateaus, ED50/LD50 at 50% population.
📖 LC29–LC30; LC28 S20–S26
⚠️ Gastric EC50 (10) < COX-1 KD (100) suggests spare enzyme capacity/amplification in gastric mucosa.
